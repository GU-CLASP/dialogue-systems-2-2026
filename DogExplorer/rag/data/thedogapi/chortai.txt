Breed: Chortai
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Rare, elegant sighthound from Central Asia with athletic build and short coat, bred for exceptional speed and endurance when coursing game across harsh steppe terrain

History:
Ancient sighthound breed developed in the steppe regions of Kazakhstan and southern Russia for hunting hare, fox, and larger game. Bred purely for function—speed, stamina, and hunting ability—across vast steppes. Nearly extinct after Russian Revolution but preserved by enthusiasts.

Temperament: Independent, intelligent, loyal, athletic, calm
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Southern Russia/Central Asia
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 25-35; Female: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 64-74; Female: 61-71 cm
