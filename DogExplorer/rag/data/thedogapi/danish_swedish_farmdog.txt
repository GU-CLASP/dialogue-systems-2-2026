Breed: Danish-Swedish Farmdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact, and slightly rectangular working dog with short smooth coat and alert expression, developed as a versatile all-purpose farm dog in Denmark and Sweden

History:
Ancient Scandinavian farm dog breed from eastern Denmark and southernmost Sweden, used as versatile all-purpose farm dog for ratting, herding, guarding, and companionship. Officially recognized in Denmark and Sweden in 1987, by FCI in 2008-2019, and The Kennel Club (UK) in 2019

Temperament: Alert, friendly, energetic, intelligent, adaptable, eager to please
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Denmark and Sweden
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: 7-11 kg
Height: Male: 32-38; Female: 29-37 cm
