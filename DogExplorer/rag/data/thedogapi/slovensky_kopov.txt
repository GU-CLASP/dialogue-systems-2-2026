Breed: Slovenský kopov
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized black and tan scent hound of Slovakian origin, known for exceptional endurance and tenacious courage. Bred specifically for tracking and hunting wild boar in mountainous and rugged terrain, this persistent scenthound combines intelligence with a distinctive work-focused temperament.

History:
Ancient national breed developed in Slovakia during the 1950s by crossing local hunting dogs with other European scent hounds. Specifically bred as a versatile scenthound for tracking and hunting wild boar and deer in mountainous terrain. The breed standard was first established in 1963.

Temperament: Courageous, energetic, intelligent, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Slovakia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 15-20 kg
Height: Male: 45-50; Female: 40-48 cm
