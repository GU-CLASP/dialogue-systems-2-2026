Breed: Fila Brasileiro
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Molosser-type mastiff guardian breed with distinctive loose skin, natural guarding instincts, and tracking ability

History:
Developed in Brazil from Mastiff, Bulldog, and Bloodhound ancestry for plantation work, estate guarding, cattle herding, and tracking large game. Recognized by FCI but not AKC-recognized.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, courageous, devoted, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Brazil
Life span: 9-11 years
Weight: Male: 50-82; Female: 40-73 kg
Height: Male: 65-75; Female: 60-70 cm
