Breed: Yorkipoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed between a Yorkshire Terrier and Toy or Miniature Poodle, known for its intelligent, spirited personality and low-shedding, variable coat type that can range from wavy to curly

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States by crossing Yorkshire Terriers with Toy or Miniature Poodles. The goal was to create a small companion dog combining the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat with the Yorkshire Terrier's affectionate, compact nature. Not recognized by major kennel clubs

Temperament: Intelligent, affectionate, playful, energetic, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 2-6.5 kg
Height: 18-38 cm
