Breed: Braque Français
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient French pointing breed coming in two types: the larger Gascogne and the smaller, more popular Pyrenean. This gentle, athletic, and highly trainable gundog features a distinctive brown and white coat and versatile hunting abilities.

History:
Ancient French pointing breed dating to 15th century with two distinct varieties: the larger Gascogne and smaller Pyrenean. Descended from one of the oldest pointing-type dogs prized as versatile and biddable hunting companion

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, loyal, affectionate, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: France
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 18-25 kg
