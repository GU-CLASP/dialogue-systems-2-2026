Breed: Dikkulak
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare Turkish landrace dog characterized by distinctive erect ears from which it gets its name ('prick ear'). Small to medium-sized with spitz-type features and sighthound heritage, bred for guarding homesteads and hunting in rural Anatolia.

History:
The Dikkulak is a rare, regional landrace from eastern Turkey, primarily found in rural Anatolia. Its name translates to 'prick ear' in Turkish, distinguishing it by its erect ears. Traditionally used for guarding homesteads and controlling vermin, this breed has been shaped by its environment and function rather than formal breeding programs. Not officially recognized by major international kennel clubs.
