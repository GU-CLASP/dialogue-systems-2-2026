Breed: Cane di Oropa
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized herding dog from the Italian Biellese Alps with a rustic appearance, strong build, and merle coat. Known for its mountain working heritage and keen, energetic temperament suited for flock management.

History:
Ancient pastoral dog native to the Biellese Alps and Oropa Valley in Italy's Piedmont region. Traditionally used by local shepherds for centuries to manage sheep and cattle in mountain terrain. The breed faced extinction before preservation efforts by the Associazione Amici del Cane di Oropa breed club.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, energetic, work-focused, alert, devoted
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Biellese Alps, Piedmont (Oropa Valley), Italy
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-30; Female: 17-27 kg
Height: Male: 48-56; Female: 45-54 cm
