Breed: Calupoh
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic wolf-like breed developed in Mexico through controlled hybridization of Mexican Gray Wolves and domestic dogs. Known for their distinctive appearance, strong build, and loyal temperament, combining the genetic heritage of wolves with the trainability of domestic dogs.

History:
Developed in Mexico in the mid-1990s through controlled hybridization of Mexican Gray Wolves with domestic dogs, primarily German Shepherds and Alaskan Malamutes, to create a wolf-like companion breed that preserves cultural heritage while maintaining domestic dog temperament and trainability. Provisionally recognized by the FCI and the Federación Canófila Mexicana.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, alert, calm, protective, confident
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Mexico
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 31-45; Female: 27-40 kg
Height: Male: 61-75; Female: 56-70 cm
