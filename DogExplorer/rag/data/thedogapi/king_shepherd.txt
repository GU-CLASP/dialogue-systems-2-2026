Breed: King Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, robust shepherd-type dog developed as an improved German Shepherd with better health and temperament. Resembles a giant, heavy-boned German Shepherd with a plush or coarse double coat and calmer, more stable demeanor intended as a family companion and guardian.

History:
Developed in the 1990s by American breeders Shelley Watts-Cross and David Turkheimer. Created by crossing American German Shepherds with Shiloh Shepherds and later infusing Great Pyrenees and Alaskan Malamute to create a larger, healthier, more robust shepherd with improved temperament. Recognized by the American King Shepherd Club and United Kennel Club.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, confident, protective, gentle, courageous
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-11 years
Weight: Male: 41-68; Female: 34-50 kg
Height: Male: 69-79; Female: 64-69 cm
