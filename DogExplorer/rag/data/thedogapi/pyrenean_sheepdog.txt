Breed: Pyrenean Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized herding dog with long, flowing coat, known for its intelligence, boundless energy, and mischievous nature. Bred for working sheep in the Pyrenees mountains, this lean and agile breed exists in both rough-faced and smooth-faced varieties.

History:
Ancient breed developed in the Pyrenees mountains of France for herding sheep, often working in partnership with Great Pyrenees as flock guardians. Recognized by FCI as Chien de Berger des Pyrénées with two coat varieties.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, courageous, playful, devoted, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: France (Pyrenees Mountains)
Weight: Male: 7-15; Female: 7-13 kg
Height: Male: 39-47; Female: 38-46 cm
