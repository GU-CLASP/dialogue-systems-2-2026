Breed: Kanni
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Rare, elegant sighthound landrace from Tamil Nadu, India, with a lean, athletic build and short, fine coat typically in black-and-tan or cream. Known for incredible speed, loyalty, and independent nature, traditionally prized for hunting.

History:
The Kanni, meaning 'pure' in Tamil, is an ancient sighthound landrace from Tamil Nadu, traditionally prized by royalty, zamindars, and tribal communities for hunting deer, wild boar, hare, and other small game. Recognized by Indian government and kennel clubs, though not by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, independent, alert, athletic, reserved
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Tamil Nadu, India
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-25; Female: 15-23 kg
Height: Male: 61-69; Female: 58-64 cm
