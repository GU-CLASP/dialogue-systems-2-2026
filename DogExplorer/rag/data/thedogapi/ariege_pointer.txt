Breed: Ariège Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, elegant and muscular French pointing dog with distinctive orange and white coat pattern and fine nose, known for its endurance, docile nature, and versatile hunting abilities

History:
Originated in the Ariège region of southern France in the 19th century, descended from old French pointing breeds crossed with Spanish Pointer and other southern pointing breeds. Faced near extinction after World War II but was revived by passionate breeders in the 1990s.

Temperament: Intelligent, gentle, docile, loyal, work-focused, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Ariège, France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-30; Female: 22-27 kg
Height: Male: 60-67; Female: 56-63 cm
