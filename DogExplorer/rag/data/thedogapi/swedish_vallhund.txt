Breed: Swedish Vallhund
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, powerful spitz-type herding dog with short legs, pointed ears, curled tail, and distinctive wolf-like markings. This ancient and fearless breed is often referred to as 'the Viking dog' and features a weather-resistant double coat.

History:
Ancient Swedish breed dating back over 1,000 years to the Viking era, used as an all-purpose farm dog for herding cattle, vermin control, and guarding. Also called Västgötaspets, believed to share ancestry with the Welsh Corgi. Recognized by Swedish Kennel Club in 1943, AKC in 2007.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, friendly, intelligent, courageous, playful, eager to please
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Sweden
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 9-16 kg
Height: Male: 32-35; Female: 29-33 cm
