Breed: English Setter
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant and athletic gundog distinguished by its unique speckled (belton) coat, feathered markings, and graceful movement, known for gentle hunting instincts and friendly disposition

History:
Developed in England over 400 years ago from spaniel stock, refined in the 19th century by Edward Laverack and R. Purcell Llewellin to create a specialized bird dog for locating and setting game birds

Temperament: Friendly, gentle, affectionate, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 29-36; Female: 20-32 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 58-64 cm
