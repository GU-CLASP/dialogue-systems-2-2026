Breed: Shiba Inu
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient Japanese spitz breed with a small, compact, well-muscled frame and distinctive fox-like appearance. Known for its spirited personality, confident bearing, and agile movement.

History:
One of Japan's six oldest native breeds, originally developed for hunting small game in mountainous terrain. Nearly extinct after WWII but saved through dedicated breeding programs. First brought to the United States in 1954.

Temperament: Alert, confident, intelligent, independent, loyal, courageous
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Japan
Weight: Male: 8-11; Female: 7-9 kg
Height: Male: 37-42; Female: 34-39 cm
