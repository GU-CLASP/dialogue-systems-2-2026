Breed: Sprocker Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A popular, highly energetic and intelligent crossbreed combining the Springer Spaniel and the Cocker Spaniel. Originally bred by gamekeepers for working and hunting, Sprockers have boundless stamina, exceptional agility, and a very friendly, loyal temperament.

History:
Developed in the United Kingdom by gamekeepers who desired a robust working gundog that combined the stamina of the English Springer Spaniel with the size and agility of the Cocker Spaniel. Very popular in the UK as both a working dog and a companion.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, friendly, loyal, affectionate, active, eager to please
Breed group: Mixed breed
Origin: United Kingdom
Life span: 10-14 years

Height: Male: 38-46; Female: 35-43 cm
