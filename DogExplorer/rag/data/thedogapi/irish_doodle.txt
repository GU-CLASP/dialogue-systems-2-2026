Breed: Irish Doodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large crossbreed combining the friendly, outgoing nature and athleticism of the Irish Setter with the intelligence and low-shedding, wavy or curly coat of the Standard Poodle

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed primarily in the United States in the 1990s and 2000s by crossing Irish Setter with Standard Poodle, aiming to combine the energetic sporting nature and temperament of the Setter with the hypoallergenic coat qualities and intelligence of the Poodle

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, energetic, playful, affectionate, outgoing
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-34; Female: 18-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-71; Female: 53-66 cm
