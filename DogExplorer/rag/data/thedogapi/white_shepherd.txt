Breed: White Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A direct descendant of the German Shepherd Dog, the White Shepherd is a large, athletic working dog with erect ears, striking white double coat, and confident demeanor. Known for its noble expression and versatile capabilities as an intelligent herding breed.

History:
Developed from white-coated German Shepherd Dogs. When white coats were deemed a fault by German breed clubs in the 1930s, fanciers in North America and Europe continued breeding them separately, eventually establishing the White Shepherd as a distinct breed recognized by various registries including UKC and FCI (as Berger Blanc Suisse).

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, alert, confident, courageous, gentle
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Germany/Switzerland, developed further in North America
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 35-40; Female: 25-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
