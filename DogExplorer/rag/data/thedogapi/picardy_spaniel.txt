Breed: Picardy Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strongly-built French pointing dog with distinctive grey-mottled brown feathered coat, drop ears, and gentle, docile expression. This elegant gundog combines calm adaptability with natural hunting ability.

History:
One of the oldest continental spaniel-type pointing breeds, originating in the Picardy region of France. Valued by hunters for its versatility and weather-resistant coat, its numbers declined with imported English breeds but were saved from extinction by dedicated breeders after World War II.

Temperament: Gentle, calm, intelligent, docile, devoted, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Picardy, France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 20-25 kg
Height: 56-61 cm
