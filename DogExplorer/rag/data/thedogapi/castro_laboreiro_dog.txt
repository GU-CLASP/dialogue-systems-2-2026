Breed: Castro Laboreiro Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient, large, and powerful Portuguese livestock guardian dog with a distinctive wolf-like appearance and movement. This rustic mountain breed is known for its formidable and unique graduated bark that starts low and rises in pitch.

History:
Ancient Portuguese livestock guardian breed from the Peneda-Gerês mountains of northern Portugal. Developed to protect herds from wolves and other predators, it is one of the oldest and rarest breeds on the Iberian Peninsula.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, intelligent, independent, alert, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Castro Laboreiro region, Portugal
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 30-40; Female: 25-35 kg
Height: Male: 58-63; Female: 55-60 cm
