Breed: Portuguese Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, athletic gundog of noble appearance with short dense coat and expressive face, known for its extreme devotion to its master, excellent scenting ability, and dedicated, persistent hunting style

History:
Ancient Portuguese hunting breed descended from Iberian hunting dogs, used for centuries by nobility to hunt partridge and game birds. Known locally as Perdigueiro Português, the breed standard was established in early 20th century, reviving it from near extinction.

Temperament: Affectionate, loyal, intelligent, devoted, energetic, alert
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Portugal
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-27; Female: 16-22 kg
Height: Male: 52-56; Female: 48-54 cm
