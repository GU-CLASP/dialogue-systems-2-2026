Small designer crossbreed between Havanese and Toy Poodle, known for its intelligent, affectionate personality and soft, low-shedding coat that ranges from wavy to curly

Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States in recent decades, combining Cuban Havanese with Toy Poodle to create a small companion dog with intelligence, low-shedding coat, and gentle disposition. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.
