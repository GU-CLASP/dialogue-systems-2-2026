Breed: Pomeranian
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact, active toy spitz breed with abundant fluffy double coat, fox-like expression, and plumed tail carried over the back

History:
Descended from larger Arctic spitz-type sled dogs and bred down to current small size in the Pomerania region. Popularity greatly increased by Queen Victoria who became a breeder and established distinct breed standards

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, playful, confident, outgoing
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Pomerania region (now parts of Germany and Poland)
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 1.4-3.2 kg
Height: 15-18 cm
