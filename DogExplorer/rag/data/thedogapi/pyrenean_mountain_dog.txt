Breed: Pyrenean Mountain Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, majestic, and immensely powerful livestock guardian with thick white double coat and natural guardian instincts developed for mountain work

History:
Ancient breed used for centuries by shepherds in the Pyrenees Mountains of France and Spain to guard flocks against predators like wolves and bears. Known as 'Chien de Montagne des Pyrénées' and standardized by the FCI.

Temperament: Gentle, confident, protective, independent, calm, loyal
Breed group: Working
Origin: Pyrenees Mountains, France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-73; Female: 39-52 kg
Height: Male: 69-81; Female: 63-74 cm
