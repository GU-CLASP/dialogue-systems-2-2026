Breed: Stephens Stock
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized American treeing hound with compact, muscular build and agile frame. Known for courage, intelligence, and tenacity in hunting game from squirrels to bears. Features a short, dense coat typically black with white markings or black and tan coloring.

History:
Developed in southeastern Kentucky by the Stephens family in the early 20th century from Mountain Cur stock. Selectively bred for specific 'silent on track, tree with a chop' hunting style for small game, notably squirrel and raccoon. Recognized by United Kennel Club in 1995.

Temperament: Courageous, loyal, intelligent, alert, work-focused, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Southeastern Kentucky, United States
Life span: 10-16 years
Weight: Male: 18-29; Female: 16-25 kg
Height: Male: 46-58; Female: 41-53 cm
