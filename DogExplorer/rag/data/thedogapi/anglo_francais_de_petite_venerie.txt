Breed: Anglo-Français de Petite Vénerie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, elegant and well-balanced French scenthound with a short tricolor coat, bred for hunting small game in packs with excellent scenting ability and strong pack hunting instincts

History:
Developed in France by crossing English Foxhounds and Harriers with various French scenthound breeds like the Poitevin and Porcelaine to create efficient pack hounds suited for 'petite vénerie' - the hunting of small game

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, loyal, work-focused, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 16-20 kg
Height: 48-56 cm
