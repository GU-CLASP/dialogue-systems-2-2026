Breed: Saint Miguel Cattle Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, robust Portuguese cattle herding dog with distinctive brindle coat, known for intelligence, strong work ethic, and guarding instincts. Native to the Azores islands.

History:
Developed on São Miguel Island in the Azores, Portugal. Origins linked to extinct Terceira Dog and molosser types brought by early settlers. Served for centuries as exceptional cattle herding and farm guardian dog, with first written standard appearing in 1984. Also known as Cão Fila de São Miguel.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, protective, work-focused, courageous, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: São Miguel Island, Azores, Portugal
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 18-27 kg
Height: Male: 48-60; Female: 46-58 cm
