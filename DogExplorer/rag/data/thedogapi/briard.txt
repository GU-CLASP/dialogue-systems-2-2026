Breed: Briard
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful French herding dog with a luxurious long wavy double coat, distinctive beard and eyebrows creating a peek-a-boo hairdo, and a loyal, intelligent disposition

History:
Ancient French herding breed developed for centuries to herd and guard flocks. Served as sentries, messengers, and pack animals for the French army during World War I. Nearly extinct after World War II but successfully revived. Recognized by the AKC in 1928.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, protective, confident, courageous, devoted
Breed group: Herding
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 32-45; Female: 23-29 kg
Height: Male: 58-69; Female: 56-65 cm
