Breed: Lucas Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy working terrier with a dense, shaggy wiry coat, specifically bred for a friendly, non-quarrelsome temperament while maintaining robust health and working ability

History:
Developed in the late 1940s by Sir Jocelyn Lucas crossing Norfolk Terriers and Sealyham Terriers to create a small, sturdy working terrier with a more companionable temperament and fewer health problems

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, alert, affectionate, adaptable
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 6-9; Female: 5-9 kg
Height: Male: 25-32; Female: 23-30 cm
