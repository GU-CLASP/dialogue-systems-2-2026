Breed: Leonberger
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Giant, majestic working dog with a lion-like appearance, thick water-resistant double coat, and gentle, balanced temperament. This powerful yet serene breed combines an imposing presence with a calm, patient demeanor.

History:
Developed in the 1840s by Heinrich Essig in Leonberg, Germany, crossing Newfoundlands, Saint Bernards, and Pyrenean Mountain Dogs to create a 'lion-like' dog resembling the lion on the town crest. Became popular companion for European royalty

Temperament: Gentle, calm, loyal, friendly, intelligent, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Leonberg, Germany
Life span: 7-9 years
Weight: Male: 50-77; Female: 41-64 kg
Height: Male: 71-80; Female: 65-75 cm
