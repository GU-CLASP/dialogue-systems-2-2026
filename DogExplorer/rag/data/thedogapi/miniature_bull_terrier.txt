Breed: Miniature Bull Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, muscular terrier with distinctive egg-shaped head and spirited, mischievous personality. A strongly built breed known for its playful and comical nature.

History:
Developed in 19th-century England as a smaller version of the Bull Terrier by selecting for smaller size while maintaining distinctive characteristics. Initially used for ratting and vermin control, evolved into companion dogs prized for courage and comical nature. Recognized by AKC in 1991.

Temperament: Playful, energetic, courageous, loyal, mischievous, outgoing
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: 8-13 kg
Height: 25-36 cm
