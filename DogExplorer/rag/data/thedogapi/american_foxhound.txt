Breed: American Foxhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A sleek, powerful scenthound with a long, fine-boned body and athletic build, known for incredible stamina, speed, and melodious baying voice. One of the oldest American-bred dogs, developed for endurance and pack hunting.

History:
Developed from English Foxhounds imported to Colonial America in the 1650s, refined for American terrain and fox hunting. George Washington was a key figure in the breed's development, breeding them to be lighter, taller, and faster for hunting American red fox over varied terrain.

Temperament: Independent, friendly, gentle, energetic, intelligent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 29-32; Female: 27-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-64; Female: 53-61 cm
