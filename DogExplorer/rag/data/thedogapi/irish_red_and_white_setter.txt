Breed: Irish Red and White Setter
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large athletic gundog with distinctive red and white coat pattern and aristocratic appearance. This versatile sporting breed combines a powerful, athletic build with a kind expression and was bred specifically for pointing and retrieving game birds.

History:
One of Ireland's oldest gundogs with history dating back to the 17th century, predating the Irish Setter. Bred as versatile gundogs for finding and retrieving gamebirds. Nearly extinct by the 1920s but revived through dedicated breeding programs by enthusiasts in the 20th century.

Temperament: Friendly, energetic, intelligent, courageous, devoted, gentle, eager to please
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Ireland
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 57-61 cm
