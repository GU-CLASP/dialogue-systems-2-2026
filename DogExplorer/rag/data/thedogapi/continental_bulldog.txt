Breed: Continental Bulldog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletically built bulldog with a broad head, muscular compact body, and short coat, designed for improved health and breathing compared to traditional bulldogs with smooth movement and endurance

History:
Developed in Switzerland in the early 2000s by breeder Imelda Angehrn to create a healthier more athletic bulldog with fewer respiratory issues by crossing English Bulldogs with Olde English Bulldogges, provisionally recognized by FCI in 2022

Temperament: Friendly, confident, calm, alert, adaptable, loyal
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Switzerland
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-30; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 42-47; Female: 38-44 cm
