Breed: Nenets Herding Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type herding dog from the Siberian Arctic with erect ears, thick weather-resistant double coat, and agile build. Developed by the indigenous Nenets people to manage reindeer herds in harsh tundra conditions, possessing enduring stamina and an independent, work-focused nature.

History:
An ancient landrace breed developed by the indigenous Nenets people of the Yamal Peninsula in Siberia. For centuries, it has been an essential partner for herding, guarding, and transporting reindeer in harsh Arctic conditions. The breed is an ancestor to the modern Samoyed and was fundamental in the development of several modern northern breeds.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, work-focused, independent, alert, loyal, hardy
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Yamal Peninsula, Siberia, Russia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 18-25; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 44-52; Female: 41-48 cm
