Breed: Thai Bangkaew Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type dog with a fox-like face, thick double coat, pricked ears, and plumed tail, known for its loyalty and protective nature

History:
Originated from Wat Bangkaew monastery in early 20th century from a cross between a native domestic dog and wild canid. Developed as guard dog and companion in rural Thailand, traditionally kept as watchdogs. Recognized by FCI in Group 5.

Temperament: Alert, loyal, protective, intelligent, independent
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Bangkaew, Phitsanulok Province, Thailand
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 16-20.5; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: Male: 46-53; Female: 41-51 cm
