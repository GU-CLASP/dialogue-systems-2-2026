Breed: Chow Chow
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient, powerful, and sturdily built dog of Arctic spitz-type, known for its distinctive lion-like mane, unique blue-black tongue, stilted gait, and dense double coat

History:
One of the oldest recognizable breeds with origins dating back over 2,000 years in northern China, where it served as hunting, herding, guarding, and companion dog. Refined during Tang Dynasty and recognized by AKC in 1903.

Temperament: Loyal, independent, aloof, alert, calm, protective
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: China
Life span: 8-12 years
Weight: 20-32 kg
Height: 43-51 cm
