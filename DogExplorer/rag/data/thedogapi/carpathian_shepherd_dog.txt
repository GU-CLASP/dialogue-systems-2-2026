Breed: Carpathian Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog native to the Carpathian Mountains with a wolf-like appearance and thick double coat. Known for its courageous and protective nature, this robust mountain dog was developed for guarding sheep flocks from predators including wolves and bears.

History:
Ancient breed developed over centuries by Romanian shepherds in the Carpathian Mountains for protecting sheep flocks from predators. First breed standard written in 1934 by the National Institute for Animal Husbandry and officially recognized by the FCI in 2005.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, courageous, calm, independent, alert, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Carpathian Mountains, Romania
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 35-45; Female: 30-36 kg
Height: Male: 65-73; Female: 59-68 cm
