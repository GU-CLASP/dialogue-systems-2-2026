Breed: Lobito Herreño
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A primitive, wolf-like medium-sized herding dog from the island of El Hierro, known for its agility, loyalty, and reserved nature. Also known as Pastor Herreño, this rustic breed was traditionally used for livestock management in volcanic terrain.

History:
A primitive landrace dog native to El Hierro island, descended from ancient dogs brought by pre-Hispanic settlers. Traditionally used for herding goats and sheep in volcanic terrain. Formally recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE) in 2013 after recovery efforts saved it from near extinction.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, alert, independent, protective
Breed group: Herding
Origin: El Hierro, Canary Islands, Spain
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 18-22 kg
Height: Male: 52-54; Female: 47-52 cm
