Breed: Cardigan Welsh Corgi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Low-set, sturdily built herding dog with a long body, fox-like brushy tail, and rounded ears, known for intelligence and versatility

History:
One of the oldest herding breeds, believed brought to Wales by Celtic tribes around 1200 BC, developed as versatile farm dogs for cattle herding and property guarding

Temperament: Affectionate, loyal, intelligent, alert, adaptable
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Cardiganshire, Wales
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 14-17; Female: 11-15 kg
Height: 27-32 cm
