Breed: Shih Poo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed between Shih Tzu and Poodle with soft, wavy or curly coat and hypoallergenic potential. Known for friendly temperament and playful nature, though appearance and temperament can vary as a mixed breed.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the late 20th/early 21st century in the United States by crossing Shih Tzu with Toy or Miniature Poodle. Primary goal was to create a small companion dog combining Shih Tzu affectionate nature with Poodle intelligence and low-shedding coat. Not recognized as standardized breed by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Affectionate, friendly, playful, intelligent, outgoing
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-17 years
Weight: 3.5-8 kg
Height: 20-38 cm
