Breed: Plummer Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, energetic working terrier with a distinctive smooth red and white coat, bred specifically for vermin control and known for its tenacious, fearless nature and athletic build

History:
Developed in the 1960s by Dr. David Brian Plummer in the UK by crossing Jack Russell Terriers, Beagles, Fell Terriers, and Bull Terriers to create a hardy, versatile working terrier specialized for fox control and vermin hunting

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, energetic, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: United Kingdom
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 5-8; Female: 4-7 kg
Height: Male: 28-36; Female: 25-33 cm
