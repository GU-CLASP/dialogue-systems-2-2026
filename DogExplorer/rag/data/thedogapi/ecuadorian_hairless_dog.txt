Breed: Ecuadorian Hairless Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Rare, ancient hairless landrace breed from Ecuador with medium-sized, sleek body, elegant build, and expressive features. This indigenous breed is known for its warm skin, calm demeanor, and loyalty to family.

History:
Ancient indigenous landrace breed from pre-Columbian coastal Ecuador, believed to share ancestry with other South American hairless dogs. Extremely rare with limited breeding programs and preservation efforts. Not recognized by major kennel clubs but described in regional breed registries.

Temperament: Calm, alert, loyal, gentle, affectionate, intelligent, independent
Breed group: Unknown
Origin: Santa Elena Peninsula, Ecuador
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 11-16; Female: 9-14 kg
Height: Male: 38-46; Female: 36-43 cm
