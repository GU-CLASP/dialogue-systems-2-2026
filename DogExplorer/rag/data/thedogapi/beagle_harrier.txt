Breed: Beagle-Harrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, sturdily built French scent hound created by crossing Beagles and Harriers, known for its balanced build, short dense coat, and energetic disposition with a passion for hunting

History:
Developed in 19th century France by Baron Gérard, crossing English Beagles with French Harriers to create a medium-sized pack hound for hunting hare and other small game. Recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, friendly, courageous, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 19-27 kg
Height: 45-50 cm
