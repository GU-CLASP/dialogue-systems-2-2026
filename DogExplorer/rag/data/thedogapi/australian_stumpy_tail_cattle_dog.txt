Breed: Australian Stumpy Tail Cattle Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, rugged working dog with naturally short tail, bred for herding cattle in harsh Australian conditions. This compact, athletic breed combines intelligence, stamina, and exceptional herding prowess.

History:
Developed in the early-to-mid 1800s by Australian cattlemen crossing British herding dogs (such as the Smithfield) with native Australian dingo-type dogs. Bred specifically for working ability and natural bob-tail, developing parallel to the Australian Cattle Dog as an independent herding breed.

Temperament: Loyal, alert, energetic, intelligent, courageous, independent, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Australia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-23; Female: 14.5-20.5 kg
Height: Male: 46-51; Female: 43-48 cm
