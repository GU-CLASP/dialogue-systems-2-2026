Breed: Chippiparai
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient Indian sighthound breed from Tamil Nadu, known for its lean, elegant build and exceptional speed. This athletic breed has a very short, close coat typically in shades of fawn, reddish-brown, or brindle, and is prized for its hunting prowess and reserved loyalty to family.

History:
Ancient sighthound breed developed by royalty in the Chippiparai region near Madurai in Tamil Nadu, India. Traditionally used by royal families and aristocrats for hunting wild boar, deer, and hare. The breed is prized for its speed, endurance, hunting prowess, and robust health adapted to the local climate.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, independent, alert, gentle, athletic
Breed group: Sighthound
Origin: Tamil Nadu, India
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-22; Female: 12-18 kg
Height: Male: 61-72; Female: 56-66 cm
