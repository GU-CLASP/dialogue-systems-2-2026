Breed: Laizhou Hong
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Molosser-type working dog from Shandong, China, with distinctive reddish-brown coat and black mask, developed for guarding and protection

History:
Modern Chinese breed developed in the 1950s-1970s in the Laizhou region of Shandong province by crossing German Shepherds, Great Danes, Rottweilers, and local Chinese breeds to produce a versatile working and guard dog

Temperament: Loyal, protective, confident, intelligent, alert
Breed group: Working
Origin: Laizhou, Shandong Province, China
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 35-45; Female: 30-40 kg
Height: Male: 71-81; Female: 66-76 cm
