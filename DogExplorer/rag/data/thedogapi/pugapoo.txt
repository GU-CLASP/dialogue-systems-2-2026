Breed: Pugapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed combining Pug and Poodle traits, featuring a compact build with wavy or curly coat and lively, affectionate temperament. Appearance varies significantly depending on genetic inheritance from parent breeds.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in late 20th century by crossing Pug with Poodle to combine affectionate comical Pug temperament with Poodle intelligence and potentially lower-shedding coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, intelligent, outgoing, adaptable
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 6-14 kg
Height: 25-38 cm
