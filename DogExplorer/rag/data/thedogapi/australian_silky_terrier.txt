Breed: Australian Silky Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, spirited toy terrier with a refined but substantial build, characterized by its long, straight, glossy blue-and-tan silky coat that is parted down the middle and lively, alert demeanor

History:
Developed in Australia in the late 19th century by crossing Yorkshire Terriers with Australian Terriers to create a small companion dog with a long, silky coat, combining hardiness with refined features and vermin hunting ability

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, alert, energetic, confident
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Australia
Weight: 3.5-5 kg
Height: 23-25 cm
