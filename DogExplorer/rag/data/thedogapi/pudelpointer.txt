Breed: Pudelpointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large versatile hunting dog combining Poodle intelligence with Pointer field abilities, featuring a distinctive dense wiry coat and keen, energetic temperament bred for exceptional performance in both field and water

History:
Developed in Germany in the late 19th century by Baron von Zedlitz, who crossed German Poodles (Pudel) with English Pointers to create the ideal versatile gundog for field and water work

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, eager to please, work-focused, energetic, playful
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 20-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 53-64 cm
