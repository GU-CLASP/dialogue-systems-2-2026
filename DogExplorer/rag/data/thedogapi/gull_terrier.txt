Breed: Gull Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An athletic, muscular, medium-sized landrace breed from Pakistan with a smooth coat and strong build. Known for its strength, tenacity, guarding instincts, and alert demeanor. Often predominantly white in color with strong predatory instincts.

History:
Developed in the Punjab region of Pakistan and India during the British Raj by crossing imported English Bull Terriers with local bully-type landrace dogs. Traditionally used for hunting, guarding, animal baiting, and rural protection. Maintains strong working abilities from its historical background.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, intelligent, protective, independent, energetic, alert
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Pakistan (Punjab region)
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-30; Female: 22-27 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 43-53 cm
