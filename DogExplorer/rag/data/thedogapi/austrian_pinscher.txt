Breed: Austrian Pinscher
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, sturdy farm dog with robust build, distinctive pear-shaped head, and dense double coat, originally developed as a versatile guardian and ratter with strong protective instincts

History:
Descended from old Austrian farm dogs of the 18th century, developed as versatile all-purpose farm dog for guarding livestock and controlling vermin. Systematic breeding began in 1921 with official recognition in 1928. Nearly extinct post-WWII and by the 1970s, saved through dedicated breeding programs using remaining bloodlines.

Temperament: Alert, protective, loyal, energetic, confident, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Austria
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 12-18 kg
Height: Male: 42-50; Female: 40-48 cm
