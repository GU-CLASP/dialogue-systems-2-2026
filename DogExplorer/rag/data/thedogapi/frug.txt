Breed: Frug
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, charming companion crossbreed resulting from a cross between a French Bulldog and Pug, known for its playful personality, distinctive flat-faced (brachycephalic) appearance, and compact build

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in late 20th century by crossing French Bulldog and Pug to combine the desirable companion traits of both parent breeds

Temperament: Playful, affectionate, outgoing, adaptable, alert, happy
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 6-13 kg
Height: 25-33 cm
