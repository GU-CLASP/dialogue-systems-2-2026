Breed: Brittanydoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large crossbreed combining the athletic bird-dog instincts of the Brittany with the intelligence and low-shedding coat of the Standard Poodle, typically featuring a wavy or curly coat

History:
Modern crossbreed developed in the United States as part of the 'doodle' trend. Created by crossing athletic Brittany sporting dogs with Standard Poodles to produce a versatile family and sporting companion with reduced shedding. Not recognized by major kennel clubs; standards vary by breeder.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, friendly, eager to please, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-16 years
Weight: Male: 16-30; Female: 14-25 kg
Height: Male: 44-64; Female: 44-58 cm
