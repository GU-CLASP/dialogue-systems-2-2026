Breed: Gaucho Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic herding dog from southern Brazil with dense double coat, strong working instincts, and calm yet protective demeanor

History:
Developed in Rio Grande do Sul, southern Brazil by Gaúcho shepherds through crossbreeding European herding dogs brought by immigrants, including Collies, German Shepherds, and Serra da Estrela Mountain Dogs. Selectively bred for herding and guarding livestock in the region's unique terrain and open ranges. Recognized by CBKC and provisionally recognized by FCI.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, protective, alert, calm
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-34; Female: 18-29 kg
Height: Male: 51-61; Female: 48-58 cm
