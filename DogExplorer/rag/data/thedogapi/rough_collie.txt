Breed: Rough Collie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An elegant and graceful large herding dog with a long, profuse double coat and intelligent expression, known for unwavering loyalty and gentle nature

History:
Developed in the Scottish Highlands and Northern England as a versatile sheep-herding dog. The breed's popularity soared after Queen Victoria became an enthusiast in the 19th century. The fictional character 'Lassie' made the Rough Collie a global icon.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, devoted, gentle, protective, alert, athletic
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Scotland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-34; Female: 23-30 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
