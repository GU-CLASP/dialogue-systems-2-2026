Breed: Mudhol Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient, elegant Indian sighthound with lean, athletic build and narrow head, known for remarkable speed, stamina, and loyalty. Also known as the Caravan Hound.

History:
Ancient Indian sighthound developed by rulers of Mudhol State for hunting in Deccan plateau region. Also known as Caravan Hound, prized for hunting prowess and guarding abilities. Recognized by Kennel Club of India and provisionally by FCI, recently inducted into Indian Army.

Temperament: Loyal, independent, intelligent, alert, courageous
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Mudhol, Karnataka, India
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 22-28; Female: 18-26 kg
Height: Male: 68-72; Female: 61-68 cm
