Breed: Can de Chira
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, agile, and rustic traditional Spanish herding dog from the Aragon region with a weather-resistant double coat and energetic temperament, prized for its intelligence and ability to manage livestock in mountainous terrain

History:
Traditional Aragonese shepherd dog from the Pyrenees mountains valued for cattle and sheep herding. Its name derives from herding 'chiras,' a local sheep breed. The breed faced near extinction but is undergoing recovery thanks to efforts by the Club del Can de Chira and recognition as a Canine Ethnic Group by the Real Sociedad Canina de España.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Aragon, Spain
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 16-20; Female: 12-16 kg
Height: Male: 42-47; Female: 38-43 cm
