Breed: Flat-Coated Retriever
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, elegant sporting dog with a lustrous, sleek flat coat in solid black or liver, known for their cheerful, perpetually youthful 'Peter Pan' personality and optimistic temperament

History:
Developed in mid-19th century England by crossing St. John's Water Dog with various retrievers, setters, spaniels and sheepdogs to create a versatile gundog for work on both land and water

Temperament: Friendly, outgoing, optimistic, intelligent, energetic, playful, devoted
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 8-10 years
Weight: Male: 27-32; Female: 25-29 kg
Height: Male: 58-62; Female: 56-60 cm
