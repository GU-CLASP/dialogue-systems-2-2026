Medium to large mixed breed combining Siberian Husky and American Eskimo Dog traits, featuring a wolf-like appearance with Spitz-type characteristics, thick double coat, and energetic, intelligent temperament

Modern designer crossbreed developed by crossing Siberian Husky with American Eskimo Dog, aiming to create a Husky-like appearance in a more adaptable package while combining working heritage with companion traits. Not recognized by major kennel clubs
