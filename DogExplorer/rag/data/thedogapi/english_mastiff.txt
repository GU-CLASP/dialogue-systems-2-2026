Breed: English Mastiff
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Massive, powerful giant breed with a short fawn, apricot, or brindle coat, distinctive black mask, and dignified demeanor. Known for combining imposing size with a gentle, loyal temperament and protective instincts.

History:
Ancient breed known in Britain for over 2,000 years, descended from Roman Molossian war dogs. Used as estate guardian, war dog, and big-game hunter. Modern breed refined in medieval England and saved from extinction after World War II.

Temperament: Gentle, loyal, protective, calm, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: England
Life span: 6-10 years
Weight: Male: 73-104; Female: 54-77 kg
Height: Male: 76-86; Female: 70-81 cm
