Breed: Pitsky
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A powerful and energetic mixed breed combining the athleticism and striking appearance of the Siberian Husky with the strength of the American Pit Bull Terrier, known for its athletic build and energetic temperament

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in recent decades by crossing the American Pit Bull Terrier with the Siberian Husky to create a dog with the Terrier's strength and the Husky's endurance and appearance. No formal breed club or standard exists.

Temperament: Energetic, loyal, intelligent, confident, playful
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-32; Female: 14-27 kg
Height: Male: 48-61; Female: 46-56 cm
