Breed: Treeing Feist
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized, athletic hunting dog developed in the Southern United States, known for its tenacity in treeing small game like squirrels and raccoons with high energy and strong prey drive

History:
Developed in the rural Southern United States by crossing terriers and hounds brought by early settlers, bred to be a low-maintenance versatile hunting dog excelling at tracking and treeing small game

Temperament: Energetic, alert, intelligent, eager to please, work-focused
Breed group: Foundation Stock Service
Origin: Southern United States
Life span: 10-18 years
Weight: 7-14 kg
Height: 30-46 cm
