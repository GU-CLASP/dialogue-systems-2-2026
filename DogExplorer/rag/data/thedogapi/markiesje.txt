Breed: Markiesje
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, elegant black spaniel-type companion dog from the Netherlands, characterized by its silky, medium-length coat and intelligent, calm expression. This attentive and lively breed has a friendly temperament and appeared in Dutch paintings from the 17th and 18th centuries as a companion to noblewomen.

History:
Ancient Dutch breed appearing in paintings from the 17th and 18th centuries as companion to noblewomen. Nearly extinct by 1970s but revived through dedicated breeding program starting in 1980s. Officially recognized by Dutch Kennel Club in 1999 and provisionally recognized by FCI.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, calm, alert, affectionate, energetic
Breed group: Companion
Origin: Netherlands
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 6-8 kg
Height: Male: 35-40; Female: 34-39 cm
