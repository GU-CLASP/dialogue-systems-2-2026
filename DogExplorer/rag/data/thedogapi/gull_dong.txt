Breed: Gull Dong
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A large, powerful, and muscular mastiff-type dog from Pakistan, known for its formidable presence and strong protective instincts. Developed as a cross between the Gull Terrier and the Bully Kutta, this breed features a short, smooth coat and agile build designed for guarding and protection work.

History:
Developed in the 19th century in the Punjab region of Pakistan by crossing the Gull Terrier with the Bully Kutta, bred specifically for guarding, personal protection, and historically for dog fighting. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Protective, courageous, loyal, independent, intelligent
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Punjab region, Pakistan
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 36-64; Female: 36-54 kg
Height: 61-71 cm
