Breed: Tyrolean Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic scent hound with a strong, sinewy build and dense double coat, developed for endurance and tracking ability in mountainous terrain

History:
Descended from Celtic Hounds, systematically bred in the Tyrol region starting around 1860 for hunting hare, fox, and larger game in mountainous terrain. Emperor Maximilian I used these hounds as early as the 1500s. Officially recognized in 1896 by the FCI

Temperament: Intelligent, independent, energetic, alert, work-focused, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Austria (Tyrol region)
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 15-22 kg
Height: Male: 44-50; Female: 42-48 cm
