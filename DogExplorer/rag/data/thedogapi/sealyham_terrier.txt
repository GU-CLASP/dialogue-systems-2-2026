Breed: Sealyham Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy white terrier with rectangular body shape, long hard wiry coat, and distinctive beard and furnishings on face and legs

History:
Developed in the mid-19th century by Captain John Edwardes at his Sealy Ham estate in Wales for hunting badgers foxes and otters. Recognized by The Kennel Club in 1910 and AKC in 1911

Temperament: Friendly, alert, confident, outgoing, courageous
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Wales, United Kingdom
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 10-11; Female: 9.5-10 kg
Height: 27-28 cm
