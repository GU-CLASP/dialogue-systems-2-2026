Breed: Papipoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed combining the Papillon's distinctive butterfly ears and alertness with the Toy Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding qualities, featuring a coat that ranges from straight and silky to wavy or curly

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in recent decades by intentionally crossing Papillon and Toy Poodle to create a small, intelligent companion dog with a low-shedding, potentially hypoallergenic coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs but seen in designer breed registries.

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, playful, affectionate, friendly
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 2-6 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
