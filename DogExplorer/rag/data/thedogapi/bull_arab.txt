Breed: Bull Arab
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and athletic Australian hunting dog specifically developed for tracking and catching feral pigs. Known for its muscular build, strength, speed, and resilience with a short coat.

History:
Developed in Australia in the 1970s by Mike Hodgens crossing Bull Terrier for tenacity, Greyhound for speed, and German Shorthaired Pointer for scenting ability and endurance to create the ultimate pig-hunting dog

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, protective, courageous, energetic
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Australia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 32-43; Female: 27-40 kg
Height: Male: 61-69; Female: 61-66 cm
