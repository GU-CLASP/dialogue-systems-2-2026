Breed: Pastor Garafiano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium to large, lupoid-type herding dog from the Canary Islands with a robust build, long abundant double coat, and strong working instincts. Known for its docile character and suitability as both a herding dog and family companion.

History:
An autochthonous breed from La Palma island in the Canary Islands, developed from pre-Hispanic dogs and used traditionally for herding goats and sheep in mountainous terrain. Officially recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España and provisionally by the FCI (Standard No. 321)

Temperament: Docile, intelligent, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: La Palma, Canary Islands, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-35; Female: 22-30 kg
Height: Male: 56-64; Female: 53-59 cm
