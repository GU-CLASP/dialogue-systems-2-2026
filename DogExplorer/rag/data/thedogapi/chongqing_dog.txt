Breed: Chongqing Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized ancient Chinese spitz-type dog with distinctive bamboo-like tail, short reddish-brown coat, upright ears, and muscular build with protective temperament

History:
Ancient Chinese breed from Chongqing and Sichuan provinces, traditionally used for hunting and guard work. Nearly extinct but undergoing revival efforts, with breed standards established by regional Chinese registries.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, alert, confident, independent
Breed group: Utility
Origin: Chongqing, China
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 15-25; Female: 12-20 kg
Height: Male: 38-50; Female: 35-46 cm
