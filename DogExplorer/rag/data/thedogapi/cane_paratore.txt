Breed: Cane Paratore
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, rustic Italian herding dog from the Piedmont Alps, known for its agility, intelligence, and typically merle coat. This athletic breed combines herding instincts with protective qualities, traditionally used for managing livestock in mountainous terrain.

History:
A landrace breed developed over centuries by shepherds in the western Italian Alps (Piedmont) to manage sheep and cattle in mountainous terrain. Valued for its hardiness and working ability, the breed is undergoing official recognition by the Italian Kennel Club (ENCI). Also known as the Pastore d'Oropa.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, protective, work-focused, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Piedmont Alps, Italy
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-30; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 47-55; Female: 45-53 cm
