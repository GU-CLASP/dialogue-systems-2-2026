Breed: Ariégeois
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, elegant French scenthound with distinctive tricolor coat, lean body, long ears, and light limbs. Bred for pack-hunting hare and deer, known for keen nose, stamina, and scent-tracking abilities.

History:
Developed in the Ariège region of southern France in the early 20th century by crossing local briquet hounds with Grand Bleu de Gascogne and Grand Gascon-Saintongeois to create a versatile and durable pack hound for hunting hare

Temperament: Gentle, calm, intelligent, loyal, affectionate, friendly
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Ariège, France
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-30; Female: 22-30 kg
Height: Male: 52-61; Female: 50-56 cm
