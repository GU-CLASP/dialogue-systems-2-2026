Breed: Tarsus Çatalburun
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare Turkish gundog known for its distinctive split nose and exceptional scenting abilities. This athletic hunting breed from the Tarsus region combines keen pointing skills with a unique congenital split nose believed to enhance scenting ability.

History:
A rare landrace gundog from the Tarsus region of Turkey, prized by local hunters for centuries. This ancient breed is one of the few with a congenital split nose, believed to enhance scenting ability. Traditionally used for hunting partridge and small game, it is recognized as a national breed by the Turkish Kennel Club (KIF) but not by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, energetic, alert, work-focused

Origin: Tarsus, Turkey
