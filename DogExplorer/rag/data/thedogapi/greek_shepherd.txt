Breed: Greek Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog native to the mountainous regions of Greece, known for its thick double coat, independent nature, and strong protective instincts developed over centuries of guarding flocks

History:
An ancient landrace breed developed over centuries in the mountainous regions of Greece by shepherds to protect flocks from predators. Documented by the FCI under breed standard No. 246 as 'Greek Shepherd Dog'

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, courageous, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: Greece
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 40-55; Female: 32-50 kg
Height: Male: 63-75; Female: 58-68 cm
