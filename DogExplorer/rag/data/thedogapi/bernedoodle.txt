Breed: Bernedoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A crossbreed between a Bernese Mountain Dog and a Standard Poodle, resulting in a medium to large-sized intelligent companion with a wavy or curly low-shedding coat and friendly disposition. Size and appearance can vary significantly depending on the generation and the Poodle parent's size.

History:
Developed in Canada in 2003, the Bernedoodle is a recent designer crossbreed created by crossing Bernese Mountain Dogs with Standard Poodles to combine the calm, loyal nature of the Bernese Mountain Dog with the high intelligence and low-shedding coat of the Poodle

Temperament: Intelligent, friendly, gentle, playful, affectionate, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Canada
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 32-41; Female: 27-36 kg
Height: Male: 58-74; Female: 53-69 cm
