Breed: Saint-Usuge Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, elegant, and robust French spaniel with a brown coat, known for its versatility as a pointing and retrieving gundog with a gentle and affectionate personality

History:
An ancient French breed developed in the 19th century in the Bresse region of France as a versatile gundog. It nearly became extinct after World War II, but its survival is credited to the dedicated efforts of Father Robert Billard, who began a careful breeding program in 1950 using remaining stock and regional historical standards. The breed was recognized by the Société Centrale Canine (France) in 1990 and provisionally by the FCI in 2003.

Temperament: Gentle, affectionate, intelligent, eager to please, loyal, devoted, energetic, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Bresse region, France
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 13-20; Female: 13-19 kg
Height: Male: 45-54; Female: 41-52 cm
