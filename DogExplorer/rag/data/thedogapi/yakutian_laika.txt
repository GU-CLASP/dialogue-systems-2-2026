Breed: Yakutian Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strong and compact Spitz-type sled dog from the Arctic region of Siberia with a thick double coat and expressive eyes, known for versatility, resilience, and endurance in extreme cold climates

History:
Ancient aboriginal breed developed by indigenous peoples of Yakutia for sled pulling, hunting, herding reindeer, and companionship in extreme Arctic conditions. Recognized by the FCI in 2019.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, loyal, alert, friendly, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Yakutia, Siberia, Russia
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 18-25; Female: 16-25 kg
Height: Male: 53-60; Female: 51-58 cm
