Breed: Russian Toy
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Very small, elegant toy breed with fine bone structure and lively expression, available in both smooth and long coat varieties. Known for their cheerful disposition and fragile build.

History:
Developed in Russia from English Toy Terriers in the early 20th century. Nearly extinct after the Russian Revolution but rebuilt from remaining dogs post-WWII, leading to standardization of both smooth and long-coated varieties. Recently recognized by AKC in 2022.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, playful, affectionate, energetic, outgoing
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Russia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 1.4-3 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
