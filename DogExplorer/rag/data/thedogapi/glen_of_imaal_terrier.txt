Breed: Glen of Imaal Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A strong, low-set working terrier of medium length with a sturdy, powerful build. Known for its harsh, weather-resistant double coat and courageous working temperament developed in the rugged Irish mountains.

History:
Originated in the rugged Glen of Imaal valley as an all-around farm dog for hunting badgers and foxes, eradicating vermin, and historically used as turnspit dogs. Recognized by Irish Kennel Club in 1934 and AKC in 2004.

Temperament: Courageous, gentle, loyal, intelligent, spirited
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Glen of Imaal, County Wicklow, Ireland
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-18; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: 32-36 cm
