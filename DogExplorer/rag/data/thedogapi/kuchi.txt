Breed: Kuchi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, rugged landrace livestock guardian dog from the mountainous regions of Afghanistan with powerful build, thick double coat, and protective instincts. Developed by nomadic Kuchi people for guarding livestock and caravans against large predators like wolves and snow leopards.

History:
Ancient landrace breed developed over centuries by the nomadic Kuchi people of Afghanistan for guarding livestock and caravans. Bred to be hardy and formidable, capable of surviving harsh mountain climates and defending against large predators.

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, courageous, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Afghanistan
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 50-60; Female: 40-50 kg
Height: Male: 69-76; Female: 64-71 cm
