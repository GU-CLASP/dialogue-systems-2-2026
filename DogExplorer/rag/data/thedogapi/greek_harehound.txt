Breed: Greek Harehound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic scenthound with a short, dense black and tan coat, bred for stamina and endurance when hunting hare in rugged Greek terrain. Known for its keen sense of smell and tenacious tracking ability.

History:
Ancient breed native to Greece believed descended from hounds brought by Phoenicians. Developed as hardy tenacious scenthound for hunting hare in rugged rocky terrain. Officially recognized by FCI in 1996 as national breed of Greece.

Temperament: Energetic, independent, alert, courageous, intelligent, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Greece
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: 17-20 kg
Height: Male: 47-55; Female: 45-53 cm
