Breed: Staffordshire Bull Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, muscular, and stocky terrier with broad head and strong build, known for great strength, agility, courage, and affectionate nature with family

History:
Developed in 19th-century Staffordshire, England by crossing bulldogs with local terriers for bull-baiting and dog fighting. After these sports were banned, breeders refined the breed into the affectionate and reliable family companion known today

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, affectionate, loyal, confident, energetic
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Staffordshire, England
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 13-17; Female: 11-15 kg
Height: 36-41 cm
