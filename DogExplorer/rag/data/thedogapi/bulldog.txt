Breed: Bulldog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized muscular dog with loose wrinkled skin, distinctive wrinkled face, and pushed-in nose, known for its gentle but courageous disposition

History:
Originally developed in medieval England for bull-baiting, descended from ancient mastiff-type dogs. After blood sports were outlawed in 1835, refined into the gentle companion breed known today

Temperament: Friendly, courageous, calm, docile, loyal
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 8-10 years
Weight: Male: 23-25; Female: 18-23 kg
Height: 36-38 cm
