Breed: Kokoni
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small ancient Greek companion dog with a lively and cheerful disposition, characterized by a slightly elongated body, fluffy coat, plumed tail carried in a curve, and intelligent, alert expression

History:
Ancient Greek breed with depictions found on artifacts dating back thousands of years. Developed from small landrace dogs throughout the region, served as companions and household watchdogs. Formally recognized by Greek Kennel Club in 2004-2005 and in AKC Foundation Stock Service.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, alert, playful, affectionate, loyal
Breed group: Companion
Origin: Greece
Weight: 4-6 kg
Height: 23-28 cm
