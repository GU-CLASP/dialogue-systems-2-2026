Breed: Tamaskan Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic wolf-like breed developed to resemble wolves while maintaining a friendly, trainable domestic dog temperament. Features a thick double coat and striking wolf-like appearance created through selective breeding of Nordic breeds.

History:
Developed in Finland in the 1980s by crossing Siberian Huskies, Alaskan Malamutes, and German Shepherds to create a dog with distinctive wolf-like appearance combined with gentle, family-oriented temperament. Part of AKC Foundation Stock Service but not fully recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, friendly, athletic, alert, energetic
Breed group: Working
Origin: Finland
Weight: Male: 30-45; Female: 23-39 kg
Height: Male: 63-71; Female: 61-69 cm
