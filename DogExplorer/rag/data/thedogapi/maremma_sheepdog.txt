Breed: Maremma Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, well-balanced livestock guardian with thick white coat and confident demeanour. Also known as 'Maremmano-Abruzzese Sheepdog'

History:
Ancient livestock guardian from central Italy, developed to protect sheep from predators in the regions of Maremma and Abruzzo. Recognized by FCI as Cane da Pastore Maremmano-Abruzzese.

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, alert, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: Italy (Maremma and Abruzzo regions)
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 35-45; Female: 30-40 kg
Height: Male: 65-73; Female: 61-68 cm
