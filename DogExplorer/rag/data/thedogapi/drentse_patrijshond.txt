Breed: Drentse Patrijshond
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized versatile hunting dog from the Netherlands with distinctive white coat and orange/brown markings, known for pointing and retrieving abilities. This intelligent spaniel-type breed serves as both an excellent gundog and devoted family companion.

History:
Ancient Dutch breed originating in the 16th century from Spioenen dogs from Spain, developed in Drenthe province as versatile farm and hunting dog for pointing, tracking and retrieving game birds. Nearly extinct after WWII but successfully revived.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, loyal, gentle, energetic, alert
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Drenthe province, Netherlands
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-33; Female: 22-25 kg
Height: Male: 57-63; Female: 55-60 cm
