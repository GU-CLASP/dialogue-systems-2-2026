Breed: Donggyeongi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, spitz-type dog native to Korea, characterized by its naturally short or bobbed tail, alert expression, and gentle, friendly disposition

History:
An ancient indigenous Korean breed from the Silla Dynasty period, originating in Gyeongju. Primarily used for hunting and valued for companionship and as a guard dog for centuries. The breed faced near extinction but was revived through dedicated conservation efforts and was designated a Korean National Treasure in 2012. Recognized as a Korean cultural heritage breed but not by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Friendly, gentle, alert, intelligent, loyal, courageous, adaptable
Breed group: Spitz
Origin: Gyeongju, South Korea
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-18; Female: 14-16 kg
Height: Male: 47-51; Female: 45-49 cm
