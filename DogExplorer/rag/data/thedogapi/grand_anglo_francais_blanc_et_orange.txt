Breed: Grand Anglo-Français Blanc et Orange
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and elegant French scenthound with distinctive white and orange coat, bred for pack hunting of large game. This athletic hunting hound combines French and Anglo heritage with strong hunting abilities and determined tracking instincts.

History:
Developed in 19th century France by crossing native French scenthounds with English Foxhounds to create a powerful and fast pack hound for hunting large game like deer. Breed standard established by FCI.

Temperament: Energetic, loyal, independent, courageous, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 30-35 kg
Height: Male: 60-70; Female: 58-70 cm
