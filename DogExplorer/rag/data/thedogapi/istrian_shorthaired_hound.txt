Breed: Istrian Shorthaired Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, noble-looking scent hound of ancient lineage with a smooth white coat and distinctive orange markings, bred for hunting hare and fox with a strong, sonorous voice

History:
Ancient breed from the Istrian peninsula with depictions dating back to 1497. Developed as resilient scenthound for hunting hare and fox, breed standard established in 1924 and FCI recognized in 1949. Considered one of oldest hound breeds in Balkan region.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, independent, gentle, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Istria Peninsula, Croatia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-25; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 48-59; Female: 46-57 cm
