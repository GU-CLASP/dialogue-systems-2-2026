Breed: Sabueso Español
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, ancient Spanish scenthound characterized by its long, pendulous ears, noble expression, and exceptional scenting ability, bred for tracking game across varied terrain of the Iberian Peninsula

History:
Believed to be the oldest of the Iberian scenthound breeds, with descriptions dating back to the 14th century in writings by King Alfonso XI. Developed for tracking wild boar and other game across the Iberian Peninsula, recognized by the FCI

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, calm, devoted, courageous
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Iberian Peninsula, Spain
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 23-25; Female: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 52-57; Female: 48-53 cm
