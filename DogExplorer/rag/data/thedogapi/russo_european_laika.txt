Breed: Russo-European Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strong spitz-type hunting dog of the northern forest zone with erect ears, curled bushy tail, and dense weather-resistant double coat, known for its lively, balanced temperament and versatile hunting abilities

History:
Developed from aboriginal hunting dogs (Laikas) from the northern regions of European Russia and northeastern Europe. Officially standardized in the mid-20th century to preserve the working qualities of these versatile hunting spitz for hunting game including small mammals and birds.

Temperament: Alert, courageous, independent, intelligent, loyal, energetic, confident
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Northern European Russia
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-25; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 52-58; Female: 48-56 cm
