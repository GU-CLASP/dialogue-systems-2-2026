Breed: Himalayan Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient and powerful livestock guardian breed from the Himalayan region with formidable size, thick double coat, and fiercely protective instincts. Also known as the Gaddi Kutta, Bhote Kukur, or Himalayan Mastiff, this sturdy mountain dog was developed for herding and guarding livestock in harsh Himalayan conditions.

History:
An ancient landrace developed by nomadic shepherds, particularly the Gaddi tribe, in the Himalayan regions of India and Nepal. Bred to be a versatile and resilient guardian capable of protecting flocks from large predators like leopards and wolves at high altitudes.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, independent, intelligent, alert, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Himalayan region of India and Nepal
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 41-64; Female: 36-54 kg
Height: Male: 71-86; Female: 66-81 cm
