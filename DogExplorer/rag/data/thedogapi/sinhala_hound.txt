Breed: Sinhala Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient landrace sighthound from Sri Lanka with a lean, athletic build and short coat. This medium-sized primitive dog is known for its keen hunting instincts, alert expression, and reserved yet loyal nature.

History:
Ancient pariah-type landrace native to Sri Lanka, traditionally used by indigenous Vedda people for hunting small game, tracking, and village guarding. Efforts are underway by the Kennel Association of Sri Lanka to standardize and preserve this primitive breed with strong survival instincts.

Temperament: Alert, independent, loyal, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Sighthound & Pariah
Origin: Sri Lanka
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-20; Female: 13-18 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 43-53 cm
