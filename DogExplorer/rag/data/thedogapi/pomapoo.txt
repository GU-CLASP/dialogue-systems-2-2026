Breed: Pomapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small designer crossbreed between a Pomeranian and Toy or Miniature Poodle, featuring a soft, fluffy coat and expressive face. This intelligent, affectionate companion dog often inherits the Poodle's low-shedding coat with a lively, adaptable personality.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States by crossing Pomeranian with Toy or Miniature Poodle. Primary goal was to create a small, intelligent companion dog with reduced-shedding coat suitable for allergy sufferers.

Temperament: Intelligent, affectionate, playful, alert, adaptable
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 2-7 kg
Height: 20-25 cm
