Breed: Australian Cattle Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, muscular working dog with distinctive coat markings and exceptional endurance for cattle herding; known as the Heeler

History:
Developed in 19th century Australia by crossing British herding dogs including blue merle Collies with native Dingo, with later infusions of Dalmatian and Black and Tan Kelpie to create a hardy cattle herding dog for harsh Australian conditions

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, loyal, energetic, courageous
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Australia
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-51; Female: 43-48 cm
