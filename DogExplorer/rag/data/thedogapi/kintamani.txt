Breed: Kintamani
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type dog native to Bali, Indonesia, known for its thick double coat, erect ears, plumed curled tail, and alert, intelligent disposition. This indigenous breed serves as both village guardian and companion dog.

History:
Indigenous national breed of Indonesia that originated in the Kintamani region of Bali. Evolved from local free-roaming dogs and is genetically distinct. Traditionally used as village guardian and companion dog. Officially recognized by the FCI in 2019.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, independent, protective, devoted
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Kintamani, Bali, Indonesia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 13-18; Female: 12-17 kg
Height: Male: 43-56; Female: 41-51 cm
