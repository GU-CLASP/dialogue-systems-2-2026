Breed: Greater Swiss Mountain Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and striking tricolor working dog with distinctive tri-colored coat and gentle, dependable temperament. Known for strength and robust build as a draft dog.

History:
One of Switzerland's oldest breeds, likely descended from Roman Mastiffs, used as an all-purpose draft, herding, and farm guardian dog in the Swiss Alps. Nearly extinct until rediscovered and revived in early 1900s by Dr. Albert Heim.

Temperament: Gentle, loyal, confident, alert, friendly
Breed group: Working
Origin: Switzerland
Life span: 8-11 years
Weight: Male: 52-64; Female: 39-50 kg
Height: Male: 65-72; Female: 60-69 cm
