Breed: Basset Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Low-slung, heavy-boned scent hound with distinctive long, velvety ears, mournful expression, and loose skin. Known for powerful tracking ability and calm demeanor.

History:
Developed by French aristocrats from hounds bred at the Abbey of St. Hubert for hunting small game in dense cover. Low-slung body perfected for trailing rabbits and hare, later refined in England for pack hunting.

Temperament: Friendly, calm, gentle, loyal, independent, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 18-30 kg
