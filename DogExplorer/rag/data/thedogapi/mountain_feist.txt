Breed: Mountain Feist
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized hunting dog with compact, muscular build and short coat, developed in the southern United States for treeing small game. Known for exceptional agility, alertness, and tenacious hunting ability.

History:
Developed by pioneers and settlers in the rural mountainous regions of the southern United States for hunting small game. Descended from various terriers and hunting dogs brought by European immigrants, bred for tenacity, intelligence, and treeing ability.

Temperament: Energetic, alert, intelligent, courageous, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Southern United States (Appalachian and Ozark Mountains)
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 5-14 kg
Height: 30-46 cm
