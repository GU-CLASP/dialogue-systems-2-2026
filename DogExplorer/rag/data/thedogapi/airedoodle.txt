Breed: Airedoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, intelligent crossbreed resulting from crossing an Airedale Terrier with a Standard Poodle, combining the terrier's confident, courageous nature with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat qualities

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the US by crossing the versatile, courageous Airedale Terrier with the intelligent Standard Poodle. The goal was to combine the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat with the Airedale's confidence and sturdiness. Not recognized by major kennel clubs

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, courageous, loyal, friendly, alert
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
