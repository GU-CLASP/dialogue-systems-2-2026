Breed: Smooth Collie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large graceful herding dog with short, dense, smooth coat and elegant build. Distinguished by its intelligent, gentle expression and lithe, active build, the Smooth Collie shares the same chassis and sweet temperament as its rough-coated counterpart but is an easier-to-groom variety

History:
Developed in the Scottish Highlands and Northern England as a sheep-herding dog, sharing ancestry with the Rough Collie but selected for shorter coat favored in milder climates. Popularity surged after Queen Victoria became an enthusiast in the 19th century

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, gentle, friendly, devoted
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Scotland and Northern England
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-34; Female: 23-29 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
