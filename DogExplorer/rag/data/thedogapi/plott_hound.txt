Breed: Plott Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Athletic, streamlined, and powerful scent hound with distinctive brindle coat, known for its intelligence, courage, and exceptional tracking ability. Originally bred for big-game hunting including bear and boar.

History:
Developed in the mountains of North Carolina by German immigrant Johannes Georg Plott from German Hanoverian Schweisshunds brought in the 1750s. Perfected over generations for big-game hunting in the Appalachian Mountains. The only coonhound breed not descended from foxhounds and official state dog of North Carolina.

Temperament: Loyal, alert, courageous, intelligent, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: North Carolina, United States
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-27; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 51-64; Female: 51-58 cm
