Breed: Jindo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type hunting dog native to Jindo Island in South Korea with erect ears, curled tail, and strong hunting instincts, prized for loyalty, intelligence, and bravery

History:
Ancient breed that developed naturally on Jindo Island over 1500 years ago, prized for hunting abilities, courage, and fierce loyalty. Designated as Natural Treasure #53 by Korean government in 1962 to preserve breed purity.

Temperament: Loyal, independent, intelligent, alert, courageous, protective
Breed group: Foundation Stock Service
Origin: Jindo Island, South Korea
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-23; Female: 14-19 kg
Height: Male: 50-56; Female: 46-53 cm
