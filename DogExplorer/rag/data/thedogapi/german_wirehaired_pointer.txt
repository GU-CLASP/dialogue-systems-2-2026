Breed: German Wirehaired Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large versatile hunting dog with distinctive dense wiry double coat, bushy beard, and facial furnishings, known for endurance and weather resistance

History:
Developed in late 19th-century Germany by crossing German Shorthaired Pointers, Foxhounds, Pudelpointers, and Wirehaired Pointing Griffons to create versatile, rugged, weather-resistant gundog capable of working on land and water

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, affectionate, alert, eager to please
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
