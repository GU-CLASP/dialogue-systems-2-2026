Breed: Doberman Pinscher
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-large, sleek, athletic and powerful dog known for exceptional loyalty and intelligence. This elegant working breed combines strength with agility and has a confident, alert demeanor.

History:
Created in late 19th century by Karl Friedrich Louis Dobermann using breeds such as the Rottweiler, German Pinscher, and others for protection and companionship

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, loyal, energetic, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Germany
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 34-45; Female: 27-41 kg
Height: Male: 66-71; Female: 61-66 cm
