Breed: Bucovina Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog from the Carpathian Mountains with a thick, dense weather-resistant double coat. Known for its calm demeanor, courage, and devotion to family and flock.

History:
Ancient natural breed developed over centuries in the Carpathian Mountains of Romania by shepherds to guard livestock and property against large predators like bears and wolves. First breed standard written in 1982, officially recognized by FCI in 2019.

Temperament: Calm, protective, courageous, loyal, independent, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Bucovina region, Carpathian Mountains, Romania
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 50-70; Female: 45-60 kg
Height: Male: 68-78; Female: 64-72 cm
