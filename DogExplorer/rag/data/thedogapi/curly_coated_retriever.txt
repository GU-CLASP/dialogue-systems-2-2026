Breed: Curly-Coated Retriever
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, elegant retriever and one of the oldest retriever breeds, distinguished by its distinctive tight, water-resistant curly coat and confident, athletic temperament

History:
One of the oldest retriever breeds, developed in England during the 18th-19th century from English Water Spaniel, St. John's Newfoundland, and Poodle ancestry, prized by gamekeepers for waterfowl hunting versatility

Temperament: Confident, intelligent, independent, loyal, energetic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 27-43 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 58-64 cm
