Breed: Maltshi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed resulting from crossing a Maltese and Shih Tzu, known for their affectionate nature, low-shedding silky coat, and charming companion temperament

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States in the late 20th century by crossing the Maltese and Shih Tzu breeds. The goal was to create a small, affectionate companion dog with a low-shedding coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, friendly, gentle, outgoing, intelligent
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-16 years
Weight: 2.7-6.8 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
