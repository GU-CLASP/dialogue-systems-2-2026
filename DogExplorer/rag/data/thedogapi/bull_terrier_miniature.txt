Breed: Bull Terrier (Miniature)
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, upstanding, and muscular terrier with a distinctive egg-shaped head and triangular eyes. This compact version of the Bull Terrier is known for being comical, mischievous, and lively with a squarely built frame.

History:
Developed in 19th-century England as a scaled-down version of the standard Bull Terrier, which was standardized by James Hinks. Created for fanciers who wanted a smaller companion version while maintaining the same characteristics and recognized as a separate breed by the AKC in 1991.

Temperament: Playful, energetic, courageous, confident, affectionate, outgoing
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: 9-15 kg
Height: 25-36 cm
