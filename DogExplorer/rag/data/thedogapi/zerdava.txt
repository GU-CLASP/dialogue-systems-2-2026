Breed: Zerdava
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, spitz-type hunting dog native to the Black Sea region of Turkey with a wedge-shaped head and prick ears, known for its agility, intelligence, and courageous nature in hunting game such as wild boar

History:
Ancient landrace breed from the eastern Black Sea region of Turkey, primarily the Trabzon and Rize provinces. Developed as a versatile and fearless hunter, traditionally used to track and corner prey like wild boar, bear, and fox. Now being preserved and standardized by the Turkish Kennel Club (KIF).

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, loyal, energetic, alert
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Eastern Black Sea region of Turkey
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 8-20; Female: 8-19 kg
Height: Male: 43-53; Female: 41-51 cm
