Breed: Taigan
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient, elegant sighthound from the high-altitude Tian Shan mountains of Kyrgyzstan with long, silky coat and feathered ears, adapted for hunting in mountainous terrain

History:
Ancient aboriginal sighthound breed from the mountainous Tian Shan region of Kyrgyzstan, developed by nomadic tribes as a versatile hunter capable of working at high altitudes in harsh conditions. Integral part of Kyrgyz national culture and recognized by FCI as breed No. 337

Temperament: Independent, calm, intelligent, loyal, courageous, reserved
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Kyrgyzstan
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 65-71; Female: 60-66 cm
