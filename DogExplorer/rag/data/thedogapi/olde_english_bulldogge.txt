Breed: Olde English Bulldogge
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Athletic and muscular, medium-sized bulldog breed developed to recreate the healthier, more athletic Regency-era English Bulldog with improved health, stable temperament, and well-balanced features without exaggeration

History:
Developed in the 1970s by David Leavitt to recreate the healthier, more athletic bulldog of the 18th and 19th centuries (Regency Period) using crosses of English Bulldog, American Bulldog, American Pit Bull Terrier, and Bullmastiff. Recognized by UKC but not AKC

Temperament: Confident, courageous, alert, friendly, loyal
Breed group: Foundation Stock Service
Origin: United States
Life span: 9-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-36; Female: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 43-51; Female: 41-48 cm
