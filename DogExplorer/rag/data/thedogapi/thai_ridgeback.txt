Breed: Thai Ridgeback
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient, medium-sized hound known for the distinctive ridge of hair running along its back, with a muscular, athletic build and an independent, protective nature

History:
An ancient, primitive breed native to eastern Thailand, developed for hunting small game, vermin control, and serving as a guard dog. Its isolation in the region preserved its unique characteristics, including the dorsal ridge, for centuries. One of only three breeds with hair ridge.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, independent, protective, athletic, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Thailand
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: Male: 23-34; Female: 16-25 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 51-56 cm
