Breed: Bohemian Spotted Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spotted companion dog known for its distinctive tricolor coat pattern and gentle, friendly nature. Officially known as Český strakatý pes, this adaptable breed combines intelligence with a calm temperament.

History:
Created in the 1950s in Prague by cynologist František Horák for laboratory purposes. The breed's stable temperament and manageable size made them ideal subjects. After the research concluded, the dogs were made available to the public and developed into a beloved companion breed.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, adaptable, gentle, energetic
Breed group: Companion
Origin: Czech Republic
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 15-20 kg
Height: Male: 45-53; Female: 43-53 cm
