Breed: Irish Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized terrier with a distinctive wiry, fiery red coat and bold, spirited temperament. Known for its courage and loyalty, it is one of the oldest terrier breeds.

History:
One of the oldest terrier breeds, developed in County Cork, Ireland as all-purpose farm dogs for hunting vermin and guarding homes. Their courage was proven in World War I where they served as messenger dogs and sentinels.

Temperament: Courageous, loyal, intelligent, energetic, spirited
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Ireland
Weight: Male: 11-13; Female: 11-12 kg
Height: Male: 46-48; Female: 43-48 cm
