Breed: Aksaray Malaklisi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A giant and powerful livestock guardian dog from the Aksaray region of Turkey, characterized by its massive frame, muscular build, and pendulous lips from which it gets its name ('Malakli' meaning 'with lips'). Known for exceptional size and protective instincts.

History:
Ancient Turkish breed developed as a landrace in the Aksaray region of central Turkey for livestock protection against large predators such as wolves and bears. Closely related to Turkish mastiff and guardian breeds, recognized by Turkish cynology organizations as distinct from the Kangal Shepherd Dog

Temperament: Protective, loyal, independent, calm, alert, courageous, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Aksaray Province, Central Anatolia, Turkey
Life span: 8-12 years
Weight: Male: 55-90; Female: 50-70 kg
Height: Male: 76-86; Female: 70-81 cm
