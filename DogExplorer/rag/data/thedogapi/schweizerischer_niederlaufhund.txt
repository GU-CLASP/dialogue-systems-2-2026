Breed: Schweizerischer Niederlaufhund
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A smaller version of the Swiss Hound, the Schweizerischer Niederlaufhund (Small Swiss Hound) is a sturdy, energetic scenthound with short legs bred for hunting in the mountainous terrain of Switzerland. This medium-small scent hound comes in four distinct coat varieties and possesses keen hunting instincts ideal for tracking game in confined territories.

History:
Developed in Switzerland around 1905 when hunting regulations restricted hunting to specific districts, favoring smaller, slower hounds. Created by selectively breeding smaller specimens of existing Swiss Hounds to create dogs well-suited for hunting in confined mountainous territories.

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, energetic, friendly, loyal, independent
Breed group: Scenthound
Origin: Switzerland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 15-18 kg
Height: 33-43 cm
