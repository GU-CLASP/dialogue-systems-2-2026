Breed: Ca de Bou
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, powerful Molosser-type breed with distinctive muscular build and alert expression, also known as the Perro de Presa Mallorquin. Originally bred for bull-baiting with a calm yet courageous demeanor.

History:
Ancient breed descended from Iberian Mastiff and English Bulldogs brought to the Balearic Islands. Originally used for bull-baiting and as a farm guard dog, the breed nearly faced extinction after blood sports were banned but was revived by dedicated Spanish breeders in the 20th century.

Temperament: Calm, courageous, loyal, confident, protective
Breed group: Working
Origin: Majorca, Balearic Islands, Spain
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 35-38; Female: 30-34 kg
Height: Male: 55-58; Female: 52-55 cm
