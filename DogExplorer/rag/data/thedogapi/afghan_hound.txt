Breed: Afghan Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient, elegant sighthound with dramatic, flowing silky coat and aristocratic bearing, bred for hunting large prey in mountainous terrain

History:
Ancient sighthound originating in the mountainous regions of Afghanistan, where they were prized for hunting large prey like gazelles and leopards. Valued for their speed, stamina, and problem-solving intelligence over rugged terrain. Introduced to the Western world by British officers in the early 20th century.

Temperament: Independent, dignified, aloof, loyal, confident
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Afghanistan
Life span: 12-15 years

Height: Male: 69-74; Female: 63-69 cm
