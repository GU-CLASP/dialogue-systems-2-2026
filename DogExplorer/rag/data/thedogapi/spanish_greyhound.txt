Breed: Spanish Greyhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient sighthound breed from Spain known for its lean, athletic build, speed, and gentle, calm temperament. Also called Galgo Español, they come in both smooth and wire-coated varieties and are bred for grace and reserved demeanor.

History:
Ancient breed with roots tracing back over 2000 years to Celtic hounds in the Iberian Peninsula. Used by Spanish nobility and rural hunters for coursing hare across open plains and fields. Recognized by FCI and national Spanish kennel club.

Temperament: Gentle, calm, intelligent, affectionate, independent, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Spain
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-30; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 60-70; Female: 58-68 cm
