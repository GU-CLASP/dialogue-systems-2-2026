Breed: Faroese Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type herding dog from the Faroe Islands with dense, weather-resistant double coat adapted to harsh Nordic climate. This landrace breed is known for its hardiness, intelligence, and ability to work sheep in rugged terrain.

History:
Ancient landrace breed descended from Norse dogs brought to the Faroe Islands by Vikings over a thousand years ago. Related to Icelandic Sheepdog, evolved in isolation to work sheep in challenging climate. Nearly extinct by 1960s but restored through preservation efforts managed by Danish Kennel Club and local enthusiasts.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, work-focused, independent
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Faroe Islands
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: Male: 11-20; Female: 10-18 kg
Height: Male: 43-51; Female: 41-48 cm
