Breed: Japanese Spitz
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized spitz-type dog with profuse, pure-white double coat, fox-like face with pointed muzzle, and plumed tail carried over its back. This lively companion dog has an alert, friendly disposition.

History:
Developed in Japan in the 1920s-1930s from various spitz breeds including large white German Spitz dogs brought to Japan around 1920. The breed was developed through selective breeding for friendly temperament and white coat, first exhibited in Tokyo in 1921. After World War II, the breed standard was unified by the Japan Kennel Club.

Temperament: Intelligent, playful, alert, loyal, affectionate, friendly
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Japan
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 5-11; Female: 4.5-9 kg
Height: 30-39 cm
