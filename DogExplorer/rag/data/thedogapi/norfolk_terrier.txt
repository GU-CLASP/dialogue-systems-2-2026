Breed: Norfolk Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy, and fearless terrier with a wiry, weather-resistant coat and characteristic drop ears. Known for its spirited, affectionate nature and bred for ratting and companionship.

History:
Developed in Norfolk and East Anglia, England in the 1880s-early 20th century as farm dogs to control vermin and hunt foxes. Originally classified as the drop-eared variety of the Norwich Terrier, separated as distinct breed in 1964 by The Kennel Club (UK) and 1979 by the AKC based on ear carriage.

Temperament: Alert, courageous, affectionate, energetic, playful
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Norfolk, England
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 5-5.5 kg
Height: 23-25 cm
