Breed: Treeing Tennessee Brindle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic scent hound known for its distinctive brindle coat pattern, intelligence, and persistent treeing ability. Fast and agile with a loud, distinctive bark, this breed excels at tracking and treeing small game.

History:
Developed in the mid-20th century (early 1960s) primarily by Rev. Earl Phillips in the Appalachian and Ozark Mountains from brindle-patterned cur dogs and various hounds to create a pure-breeding scent hound with strong treeing instincts for hunting small game

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, courageous, energetic, friendly
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States (Tennessee, Appalachian Mountains)
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 14-23; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: Male: 46-61; Female: 41-56 cm
