Breed: Chesapeake Bay Retriever
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A powerful, muscular gundog with distinctive wavy, waterproof double coat and amber eyes, bred for retrieving waterfowl in the harsh conditions of the Chesapeake Bay

History:
Developed in the 19th century along the Chesapeake Bay from two shipwrecked Newfoundland-type puppies (Sailor and Canton) bred with local retrievers to create a water gundog capable of withstanding icy conditions and retrieving waterfowl in harsh environments

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, loyal, protective, confident
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Maryland, United States
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 29-36; Female: 25-32 kg
Height: Male: 58-66; Female: 53-61 cm
