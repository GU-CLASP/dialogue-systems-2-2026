Breed: Poodle (Miniature)
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant, athletic, and intelligent smaller version of the Standard Poodle with a curly, hypoallergenic coat and proud carriage

History:
Originally developed as water retrievers in Germany, the name 'Poodle' comes from German 'pudel' meaning 'to splash in water'. Miniature variety was bred down from Standard Poodles for companionship, circus performance, and truffle hunting

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, playful, confident, eager to please
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 4.5-6.8 kg
Height: 25-38 cm
