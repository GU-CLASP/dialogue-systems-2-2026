Breed: Gończy Polski
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Polish scent hound with muscular, compact build and strong hunting instincts, known for its courage, intelligence, and distinctive sonorous voice

History:
Ancient Polish hunting hound with documented history since the 13th century. Nearly lost after WWII but reconstructed through efforts of Colonel Józef Pawłusiewicz. Officially recognized by FCI in 2006.

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, loyal, alert, confident
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Poland
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 25-30; Female: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 55-59; Female: 50-55 cm
