Breed: Komondor
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, muscular Hungarian livestock guardian dog with distinctive heavy coat of white cords resembling a mop. Built for endurance and protection, this imposing breed possesses a dignified, courageous demeanor.

History:
Ancient breed descended from Tibetan dogs brought to Hungary by nomadic Magyar tribes over a thousand years ago around 1000 AD. Developed to be formidable independent livestock guardian protecting sheep and cattle from wolves bears and harsh weather.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, independent, courageous, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-59; Female: 36-50 kg
Height: Male: 70-80; Female: 65-70 cm
