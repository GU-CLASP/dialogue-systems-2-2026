Breed: Bordoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An intelligent and energetic medium-to-large crossbreed combining the herding instincts and work ethic of the Border Collie with the low-shedding coat and athleticism of the Poodle, creating a highly trainable companion

History:
Developed in the late 20th/early 21st century by crossing Border Collies with Standard Poodles to create a highly intelligent, low-shedding companion. Part of the popular 'doodle' designer breed trend

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, playful, eager to please
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-29; Female: 14-25 kg
Height: Male: 46-64; Female: 43-56 cm
