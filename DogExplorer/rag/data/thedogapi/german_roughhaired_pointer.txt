Breed: German Roughhaired Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large versatile gundog with dense, harsh, bristly (stichelhaarig) weatherproof coat and distinctive beard. Known for strong pointing instincts and calm, even temperament, this noble hunting companion is considered the oldest of the German rough-coated pointing breeds.

History:
The oldest of the German rough-coated pointing breeds, developed in the late 19th century from remnants of old German rough-coated bird dogs (Hühnerhunde) crossed with Pudelpointer and Griffon breeds. Standardized with focus on functional hunting ability as a versatile rough-coated hunting dog.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, confident, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 26-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-64 cm
