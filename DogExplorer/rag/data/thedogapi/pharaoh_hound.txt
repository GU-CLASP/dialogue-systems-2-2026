Breed: Pharaoh Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized elegant sighthound with short tan coat, alert expression, and distinctive ability to 'blush' on nose and ears when excited. A graceful, powerful, and swift breed of ancient origins.

History:
National dog of Malta and ancient breed believed descended from Egyptian hunting dogs brought by Phoenician traders over 2,000 years ago. Known as 'Kelb tal-Fenek' (rabbit dog) in Malta, used for rabbit hunting. One of oldest domesticated breeds with depictions found in ancient Egyptian tombs. Recognized by AKC in 1983.

Temperament: Intelligent, friendly, playful, alert, affectionate
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Malta
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 58-64; Female: 53-61 cm
