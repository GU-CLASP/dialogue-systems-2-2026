Breed: Small Münsterländer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized versatile hunting dog of German origin with distinctive brown and white or brown roan feathered coat, known for excellent pointing and retrieving abilities and elegant appearance

History:
Developed in the Münsterland region of Germany in the early 1900s from crossing German Longhaired Pointers with Brittany-type dogs and long-haired German Spaniels to create a versatile, all-purpose hunting companion

Temperament: Intelligent, eager to please, energetic, loyal, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Münster region, Germany
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 18-30 kg
Height: Male: 52-56; Female: 50-53 cm
