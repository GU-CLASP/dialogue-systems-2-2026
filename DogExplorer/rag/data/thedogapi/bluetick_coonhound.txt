Breed: Bluetick Coonhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic, and muscular scenthound with distinctive blue-ticked coat pattern and renowned 'cold nose' trailing ability, bred specifically for tracking and treeing raccoons

History:
Developed in Louisiana from French Grand Bleu de Gascogne hounds crossed with English Foxhounds, American Foxhounds, and other local coonhound breeds, perfected for raccoon hunting in American terrain

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, loyal, devoted, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-12 years
Weight: Male: 25-36; Female: 20-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 53-64 cm
