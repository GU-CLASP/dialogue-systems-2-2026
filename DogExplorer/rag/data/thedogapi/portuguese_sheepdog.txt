Breed: Portuguese Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized herding dog with long, shaggy wavy coat and lively intelligent expression, known for its monkey-like gait and rustic appearance

History:
Developed in the Serra de Aires region of Portugal, likely from Briards brought in the early 1900s. Used extensively for herding sheep, goats, and cattle. Known locally as 'cão macaco' (monkey dog) for its lively antics.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, alert, devoted
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Serra de Aires, Portugal
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 17-27; Female: 16-25 kg
Height: Male: 43-55; Female: 41-52 cm
