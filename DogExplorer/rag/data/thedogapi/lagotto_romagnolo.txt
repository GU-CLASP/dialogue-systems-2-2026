Breed: Lagotto Romagnolo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rustic water retriever with dense, curly, waterproof coat and exceptional sense of smell, specialized as the world's premier truffle-hunting dog

History:
Ancient breed from the Romagna region of Italy, originally used as a water retriever in the marshlands. When the marshes were drained, the breed was adapted and became the world's premier truffle-hunting dog.

Temperament: Affectionate, intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Romagna region, Italy
Weight: Male: 13-16; Female: 11-14 kg
Height: Male: 43-48; Female: 41-46 cm
