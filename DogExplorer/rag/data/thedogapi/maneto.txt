Breed: Maneto
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, short-legged (achondroplastic) sighthound from Andalusia, Spain, specifically developed from the Podenco Andaluz for hunting rabbits in dense undergrowth. Known for agility in hunting small game with a low-set build and smooth coat.

History:
The Maneto is a variety of the Podenco Andaluz, characterized by its short legs (achondroplasia). This ancient Spanish breed was selectively bred in the provinces of Málaga and Cádiz to create a dog adept at navigating dense scrub and brambles to hunt rabbits and partridges. It was officially recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE) in 2004, with FCI provisional recognition.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, alert, loyal, affectionate, devoted, independent
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Andalusia, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 8-11.5 kg
Height: Male: 29-33; Female: 28-32 cm
