Breed: Keeshond
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized compact spitz with plush silver-gray double coat, fox-like face, distinctive spectacles markings around the eyes, and plumed tail curled over back

History:
Ancient Dutch breed known as the Dutch Barge Dog, popular companion and watchdog on barges and riverboats along the Rhine River. Became symbol of Dutch Patriots Party in 18th century, nearly extinct after WWII but restored from dogs exported to England and America

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, outgoing, alert, eager to please
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Netherlands
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 16-20 kg
Height: Male: 43-48; Female: 41-46 cm
