Breed: Istrian Coarse-haired Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, noble-looking scent hound with distinctive harsh, wiry white coat and orange markings, bred for hunting in the mountainous terrain of Istria. Known for its calm, gentle demeanor and tenacious hunting ability with a deep, resonant voice.

History:
Ancient breed from the Istrian peninsula with depictions dating back to the 15th century. Developed for centuries to hunt fox, hare, and wild boar in mountainous terrain, prized for its weather-resistant coat and endurance. Formally standardized by FCI in the mid-20th century.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, gentle, loyal, independent, intelligent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Istria Peninsula, Croatia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-24; Female: 15-21 kg
Height: Male: 46-58; Female: 44-56 cm
