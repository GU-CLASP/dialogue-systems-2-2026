Breed: Pastore della Lessinia e del Lagorai
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, rustic sheepdog with a wolfish appearance and agile build, characterized by its endurance and semi-long, often merle coat. Developed for herding livestock in the mountainous regions of Northeast Italy.

History:
An ancient sheepdog breed from northeastern Italy, used for centuries by shepherds in the Lessinia and Lagorai mountain regions for herding and transhumance. Nearly extinct, the breed was formally recognized through recovery efforts starting in the 1990s, with FCI granting provisional recognition in 2018.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Lessinia and Lagorai regions, Triveneto, Northern Italy
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-33; Female: 19-29 kg
Height: Male: 48-58; Female: 46-54 cm
