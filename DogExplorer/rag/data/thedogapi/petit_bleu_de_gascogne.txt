Breed: Petit Bleu de Gascogne
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, noble French scent hound distinguished by its distinctive mottled 'blue' coat, long drooping ears, and exceptional sense of smell. Originally developed as a smaller version of the Grand Bleu de Gascogne, this diligent and determined hunter excels at tracking small game.

History:
Originating from the Gascogne province of southwestern France, the Petit Bleu de Gascogne is a direct descendant of the Grand Bleu de Gascogne. Developed as a smaller version specifically for hunting small game such as hare and rabbit, recognized and standardized by the FCI.

Temperament: Gentle, calm, intelligent, loyal, affectionate, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Gascony, France
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 18-25; Female: 16-22 kg
Height: Male: 50-58; Female: 50-56 cm
