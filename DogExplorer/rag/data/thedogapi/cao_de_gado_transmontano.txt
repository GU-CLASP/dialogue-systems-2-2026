Breed: Cão de Gado Transmontano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Very large, powerful molossoid-type livestock guardian dog with thick double coat, bred in the mountainous regions of northern Portugal to protect flocks from predators with a calm yet imposing and rustic demeanor

History:
Ancient Iberian mastiff-type breed developed in the remote Trás-os-Montes region of northern Portugal, used for centuries by shepherds to guard flocks against wolves and other predators in harsh mountain conditions. Recognized by the FCI for its courage, loyalty, and ability to work independently.

Temperament: Protective, independent, calm, loyal, confident, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Trás-os-Montes region, Portugal
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 50-75; Female: 40-60 kg
Height: Male: 75-84; Female: 68-78 cm
