Breed: Posavac Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Croatian scenthound of robust, noble build with distinctive reddish coat and white markings. Known for exceptional stamina, scenting ability, and sonorous voice when tracking.

History:
Ancient Croatian scenthound from the Posavina region with ancestry depicted in 15th-century frescoes. Formally documented in 18th century with first FCI standard published in 1955.

Temperament: Calm, devoted, energetic, intelligent, independent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Sava River Valley, Croatia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 16-25 kg
Height: 46-58 cm
