Breed: Villanuco de Las Encartaciones
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, primitive-type Spanish ratter dog from the Basque Country, known for its agile, alert, and rustic nature, traditionally used for vermin control

History:
Traditional primitive breed from the Las Encartaciones region of Biscay province, used on farms as guard dog and adept hunter of rats and vermin. The breed faced near-extinction but has been revived through dedicated conservation efforts. Recognized by regional Spanish breed registries but not by major international kennel clubs

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, courageous, energetic, loyal
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: Las Encartaciones, Basque Country, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 8-10; Female: 7-9 kg
Height: Male: 40-45; Female: 38-43 cm
