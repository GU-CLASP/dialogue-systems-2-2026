Breed: Cantabrian Water Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rustic, medium-sized, and athletic water dog from the Cantabria region of northern Spain, characterized by a curly, woolly, waterproof coat. Originally bred for fishing and water work, this ancient breed is known for its loyalty, work-focused nature, and exceptional aptitude for swimming and retrieving.

History:
An ancient landrace breed from the coastal Cantabrian region of northern Spain, historically used by fishermen for water work, retrieving fish and gear, guarding boats, and assisting with nets. Nearly extinct but being revived through conservation efforts, officially recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE) in 2011.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, work-focused, energetic, alert, devoted
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Cantabria, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 15-23; Female: 12-20 kg
Height: Male: 42-50; Female: 40-47 cm
