Breed: Bully Kutta
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Very large, powerful mastiff-type landrace breed from the Indian subcontinent with ancient origins, known for its immense strength, muscular build, and use as a guardian dog

History:
Ancient landrace breed with origins in the Punjab region of the Indian subcontinent. Descended from mastiff-type dogs, historically used by royalty and landowners for hunting large game and later bred for guarding, fighting, and estate protection. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs but standards exist in Pakistani and Indian breed registries

Temperament: Protective, loyal, courageous, independent, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Indian subcontinent (Pakistan/India)
Life span: 8-10 years
Weight: Male: 68-91; Female: 59-77 kg
Height: Male: 76-91; Female: 71-81 cm
