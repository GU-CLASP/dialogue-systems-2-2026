Breed: Cirneco dell'Etna
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient medium-sized sighthound with short coat, elegant slender build, and distinctive large ears. Bred for hunting on the rugged volcanic terrain of Mount Etna, this athletic breed combines endurance with graceful movement.

History:
Ancient Sicilian breed developed for rabbit hunting on Mount Etna's rugged volcanic terrain for over 2,500 years. Nearly extinct, the breed was revived in the 20th century and is now recognized by FCI and AKC Foundation Stock Service.

Temperament: Affectionate, independent, alert, gentle, intelligent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Sicily, Italy
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 10-12; Female: 8-10 kg
Height: Male: 46-50; Female: 42-46 cm
