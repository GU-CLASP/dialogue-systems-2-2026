Breed: Indian Pariah Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized primitive landrace breed native to the Indian subcontinent with erect ears, short coat, and naturally hardy constitution developed through thousands of years of natural selection

History:
Ancient aboriginal landrace breed native to the Indian subcontinent, among the world's oldest and most natural dog breeds. Developed as a free-ranging village dog over thousands of years, adapted to local climate and conditions through natural selection.
