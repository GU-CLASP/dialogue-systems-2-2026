Breed: Smooth Fox Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, energetic terrier with a predominantly white, short, smooth coat and keen, alert expression. This sturdy, medium-sized breed combines intelligence with the bold, lively spirit characteristic of terriers.

History:
Developed in England in the 18th-19th century for fox hunting, with their primary role being to 'bolt' foxes from dens. Refined from various terrier breeds and hounds, standardized by English foxhunters. Smooth and Wire Fox Terriers were considered one breed until 1985.

Temperament: Energetic, alert, intelligent, friendly, courageous, playful
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 8-8.5; Female: 7-7.5 kg
Height: Male: 38-39; Female: 36-38 cm
