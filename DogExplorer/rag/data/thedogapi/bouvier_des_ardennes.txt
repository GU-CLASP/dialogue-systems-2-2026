Breed: Bouvier des Ardennes
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, hardy and rustic herding dog from the Belgian Ardennes, characterized by a harsh, shaggy double coat, robust build, and impressive endurance with a courageous temperament

History:
Ancient Belgian herding breed from the Ardennes region, traditionally used to drove cattle and pigs. Nearly extinct after the World Wars due to mechanization of farming but successfully revived by enthusiasts in the late 1980s

Temperament: Courageous, loyal, intelligent, energetic, work-focused, adaptable
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Ardennes region, Belgium
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 27-34; Female: 23-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 52-56 cm
