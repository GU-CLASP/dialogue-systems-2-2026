Breed: Kaikadi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized sighthound from Maharashtra, India, with a slender, athletic build and short, fine coat. Named after the Kaikadi nomadic tribe, this ancient landrace breed is known for its agility and exceptional hunting abilities.

History:
Ancient landrace sighthound from the Maharashtra region of India, traditionally used by the Kaikadi nomadic tribe for hunting small game, vermin, and as companion dogs. Considered one of the aboriginal hound breeds of India and recognized by the Indian National Kennel Club.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, loyal, independent, courageous
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Maharashtra, India
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-20; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: Male: 53-58; Female: 51-56 cm
