Breed: Biewer Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, elegant toy terrier with long, silky tri-colored coat and compact build. Known for their whimsical, lively personality and charming attitude that persists well into adulthood.

History:
Developed in Germany in 1984 by Werner and Gertrud Biewer from a piebald genetic mutation in their Yorkshire Terrier breeding program. Through selective breeding, they established this distinct tri-colored toy breed. AKC recognized in 2021.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, playful, devoted, affectionate
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Hunsruck, Germany
Weight: 1.8-3.6 kg
Height: 18-28 cm
