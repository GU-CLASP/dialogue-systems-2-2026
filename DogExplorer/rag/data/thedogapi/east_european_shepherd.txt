Breed: East European Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful working dog developed in Soviet Union from German Shepherds and local breeds. Similar to German Shepherd but larger and more cold-resistant, with a slightly elongated body built for strength and endurance in harsh climates. Possesses a noble, confident demeanor.

History:
Developed in Soviet Union during 1930s-1950s from German Shepherds and local breeds for military, police, and border patrol duties in harsh climates

Temperament: Loyal, protective, confident, intelligent, work-focused
Breed group: Working
Origin: Soviet Union
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 34-50; Female: 29-45 kg
Height: Male: 66-76; Female: 61-71 cm
