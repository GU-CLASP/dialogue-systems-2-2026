Breed: Argentine Pila
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An elegant and slender hairless dog native to Argentina, known for its soft, warm skin and lively, alert temperament. The breed comes in three size varieties and is prized as a companion dog.

History:
An ancient hairless breed native to Argentina, believed to have descended from dogs brought by pre-Incan peoples. Related to other South American hairless breeds and traditionally kept as a companion for perceived therapeutic warmth among indigenous peoples.

Temperament: Affectionate, alert, playful, gentle, intelligent
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Argentina
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 4-25 kg
Height: 25-51 cm
