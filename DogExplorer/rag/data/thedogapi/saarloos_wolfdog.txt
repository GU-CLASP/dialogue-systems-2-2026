Breed: Saarloos Wolfdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, strongly built wolf-like dog with reserved temperament and strong pack instincts, originally created by crossing a German Shepherd with a Eurasian wolf

History:
Created in the 1930s by Dutch breeder Leendert Saarloos by crossing a German Shepherd Dog with a female Eurasian wolf, aiming to create a more natural, robust working dog. Officially recognized by the Dutch Kennel Club in 1975.

Temperament: Independent, alert, loyal, reserved, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Netherlands
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 36-41; Female: 30-35 kg
Height: Male: 65-75; Female: 60-70 cm
