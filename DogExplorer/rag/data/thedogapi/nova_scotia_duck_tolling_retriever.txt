Breed: Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
The smallest of the retrievers, this medium-sized, athletic sporting dog is known for its distinctive reddish coat with white markings and enthusiastic personality. Originally bred for luring and retrieving waterfowl by playing along the shoreline.

History:
Developed in early 19th century Nova Scotia, specifically in the Little River Harbour area of Yarmouth County, to 'toll' (lure) ducks within gunshot range by playing along the shoreline, then retrieve shot birds. Recognized by AKC in 2003.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, playful, outgoing, alert, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Nova Scotia, Canada
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-53; Female: 43-51 cm
