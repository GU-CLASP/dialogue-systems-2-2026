Breed: Villano de Las Encartaciones
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, robust Spanish Molossian-type working dog with a short, dense brindle or black coat, traditionally used for cattle herding, property protection, and big game hunting

History:
Ancient breed from the Las Encartaciones region of Basque Country, descended from old Alano dogs. Traditionally used for centuries for handling semi-wild Monchina cattle, property protection, and big game hunting particularly wild boar. Recognized by Spanish kennel organizations and valued for working ability.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, protective, intelligent, confident, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Las Encartaciones, Basque Country, Spain
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 33-45; Female: 28-38 kg
Height: Male: 60-65; Female: 58-63 cm
