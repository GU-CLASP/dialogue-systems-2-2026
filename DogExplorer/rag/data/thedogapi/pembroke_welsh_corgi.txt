Breed: Pembroke Welsh Corgi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A low-set, strong, and sturdily built herding dog with short legs, fox-like face, alert expression, and naturally bobbed tail. Known for their intelligence, sturdy build, and distinctive appearance.

History:
Ancient herding breed from Pembrokeshire, Wales, brought by Flemish weavers in 1107. Developed as a cattle herding dog, their low stature allowed them to nip at cattle's heels and avoid being kicked. Closely associated with British royalty.

Temperament: Intelligent, affectionate, alert, outgoing, playful
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Pembrokeshire, Wales
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: Male: 12-14; Female: 11-13 kg
Height: 25-30 cm
