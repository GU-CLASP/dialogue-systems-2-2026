Breed: Sarail Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare, tall, and athletic sighthound native to Bangladesh, renowned for its speed, endurance, and hunting ability. Known for being courageous, loyal, and protective, this breed was historically prized for hunting and guarding estates.

History:
An ancient and rare sighthound breed originating from the Sarail region of Bangladesh. Believed to have been developed by Moghul zamindars by crossing Arabian Salukis with local dog breeds. Historically used by village households and officials, valued for speed, loyalty, and guarding ability in hunting and coursing game.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, intelligent, protective, energetic, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Sarail, Bangladesh
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 58-64 cm
