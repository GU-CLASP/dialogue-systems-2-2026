Breed: Xiasi Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A lean, muscular, medium-sized hunting dog from the Guizhou province of China, known for its short wiry coat, loyalty, and athletic ability. This agile breed was prized as a versatile hunting and guard dog, believed to bring wealth and good fortune to families.

History:
Ancient breed developed for centuries in and around the village of Xiasi in Guizhou province. Prized by the Miao ethnic region as a versatile hunting and guard dog, believed to bring wealth and good fortune to families. Recognized in China and the AKC's Foundation Stock Service.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, courageous, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Guizhou Province, China
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-30; Female: 15-25 kg
Height: Male: 43-53; Female: 41-51 cm
