Breed: Ryukyu
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, primitive hunting dog from the Ryukyu Islands of Japan with an athletic build, triangular ears, and a distinctive brindle coat. Known for its loyalty to family and traditional use in wild boar hunting. Also, known as 'Ryukyu Inu'.

History:
An indigenous primitive dog native to the Ryukyu Islands, traditionally used for hunting wild boar in dense subtropical forests. The breed faced near extinction but was revived through dedicated preservation efforts starting in the 1980s by the Ryukyu Inu Hozonkai (Ryukyu Dog Preservation Society). Rare breed recognized by the Nihon Ken Hozonkai but not by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, independent, courageous, alert, energetic
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: Ryukyu Islands (Okinawa), Japan
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 15-20; Female: 12-18 kg
Height: Male: 46-52; Female: 43-49 cm
