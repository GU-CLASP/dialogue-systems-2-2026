Breed: Rat Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, energetic American terrier with smooth coat and athletic build, developed as a versatile all-purpose farm dog and vermin hunter

History:
Developed in early 20th century United States from European terriers brought by British migrants, including Fox Terrier and Manchester Terrier, for farm vermin control and companionship. President Theodore Roosevelt was a notable owner and credited with naming the breed. AKC recognition in 2013.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, energetic, friendly, playful, loyal
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-18 years
Weight: 4-11 kg
Height: 25-46 cm
