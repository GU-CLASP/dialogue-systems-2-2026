Breed: Berger Picard
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized herding dog with distinctive shaggy fawn or brindle coat, expressive upright ears, and lively expression. An ancient French breed known for its rustic appearance and intelligent, observant nature.

History:
Ancient French herding breed from Picardy region, considered one of the oldest French sheepdogs. Nearly extinct after World Wars but restored by dedicated breeders.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, alert, affectionate
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Picardy, France
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 60-65; Female: 55-60 cm
