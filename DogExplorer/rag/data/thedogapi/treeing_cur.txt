Breed: Treeing Cur
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic, and versatile hunting dog developed in the rural United States, bred specifically for treeing game and working in rural environments. Known for its intelligence, courage, and strong working ability.

History:
Developed in the rural Southern United States by pioneers needing an all-purpose dog for hunting, treeing game, and guarding the homestead. Bred from various cur-type and hound crosses, with breeders prioritizing working ability over physical appearance.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, courageous, loyal, protective
Breed group: Scenthound
Origin: Southern United States
Life span: 10-16 years
Weight: 14-27 kg
Height: Male: 46-61; Female: 41-61 cm
