Breed: Bernese Mountain Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, strong, and sturdy working dog with a distinctive long tricolored coat and calm, gentle temperament. One of four types of Swiss Mountain Dogs, known for being good-natured, loyal, and an excellent family companion.

History:
One of four types of Swiss Mountain Dogs originating in the canton of Bern. Served as all-purpose farm dogs for droving cattle, pulling carts to market, and watchdogs. Nearly extinct by early 1900s but revived through careful breeding programs

Temperament: Calm, gentle, loyal, intelligent, affectionate, good-natured
Breed group: Working
Origin: Switzerland
Life span: 7-10 years
Weight: Male: 36-52; Female: 32-43 kg
Height: Male: 64-70; Female: 58-66 cm
