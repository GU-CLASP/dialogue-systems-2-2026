Breed: Boston Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, short-tailed dog with distinctive flat face, erect ears, and smooth 'tuxedo' coat markings. Known as 'The American Gentleman' for its friendly and intelligent demeanor.

History:
Developed in Boston in the 1870s from crossing English Bulldog with the now-extinct White English Terrier. Refined in the late 19th century and became one of the first American-developed breeds recognized by the AKC.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, outgoing, playful
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Boston, Massachusetts, United States
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: 5-11 kg
Height: 38-43 cm
