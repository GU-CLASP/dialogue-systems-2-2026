Breed: Romanian Raven Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog from the Carpathian Mountains, distinguished by its impressive size and entirely black coat. Known as 'Corb' (meaning 'Raven' in Romanian), this robust and courageous breed was developed for protecting sheep flocks from wolves, bears, and lynx, valued for its calm demeanor and devotion to its flock and family.

History:
Ancient native breed from the Carpathian and Sub-Carpathian regions of Romania, used for centuries as guardian of livestock against large predators. Officially recognized by the FCI on provisional basis in 2019, valued for courage, loyalty and ability to deter wolves, bears and lynx

Temperament: Protective, loyal, calm, courageous, intelligent, independent, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Romania, Carpathian Mountains
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 45-63; Female: 40-55 kg
Height: Male: 70-78; Female: 65-73 cm
