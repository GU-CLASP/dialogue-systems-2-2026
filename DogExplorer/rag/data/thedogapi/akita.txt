Breed: Akita
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A large, powerful spitz-type dog of Japanese origin with a bear-like head, dense double coat, and full curled tail. Known for its dignified presence, imposing stature, and courageous, loyal temperament.

History:
Ancient Japanese breed developed in the mountainous Akita prefecture for hunting large game including wild boar and bear. Revered in Japanese culture as a symbol of health, happiness, and good fortune. The breed was nearly lost after World War II but was preserved by dedicated breeders and designated as a national treasure in Japan.

Temperament: Courageous, loyal, dignified, independent, protective, alert
Breed group: Working
Origin: Japan
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 45-59; Female: 32-45 kg
Height: Male: 66-71; Female: 61-66 cm
