Breed: Shetland Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, agile herding dog from the Shetland Islands, resembling a miniature Rough Collie with a dense double coat, intelligent expression, and alert demeanor. A distinct breed known for its bright, playful nature and collie-like appearance.

History:
Developed in the rugged Shetland Islands of Scotland as a small, hardy herding dog for sheep and ponies in harsh conditions. Created by crossing native dogs with Border Collies, Scotch Collies and possibly Spitz breeds. Recognized by AKC in 1911.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, alert, playful, gentle, eager to please
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Shetland Islands, Scotland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 7-11 kg
Height: 33-41 cm
