Breed: Podenco Canario
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient, athletic sighthound of primitive type from the Canary Islands, characterized by its lean, elegant build, large erect ears, and remarkable agility. This medium-sized hunting dog has a smooth short coat and slender build optimized for speed and hunting rabbits in rocky volcanic terrain.

History:
Ancient breed originating in the Canary Islands, believed descended from primitive dogs brought by early Mediterranean settlers like the Phoenicians. Adapted over centuries to the volcanic terrain, becoming a highly specialized rabbit hunter recognized by the FCI as a primitive-type hunting breed.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, independent, courageous, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Canary Islands, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 17-22 kg
Height: Male: 55-64; Female: 53-60 cm
