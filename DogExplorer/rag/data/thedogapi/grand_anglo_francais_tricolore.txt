Breed: Grand Anglo-Français Tricolore
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and elegant French scent hound with a distinctive tricolor coat, specifically bred for pack hunting large game like deer. This athletic breed combines French hound heritage with English Foxhound influences.

History:
Developed in late 19th century France by crossing native French scent hounds like the Poitevin with English Foxhounds to create faster, more powerful hounds capable of hunting roe deer. Recognized by FCI as typical French scenthound for traditional pack hunting.

Temperament: Energetic, courageous, loyal, work-focused, independent, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 30-35 kg
Height: 60-72 cm
