Breed: Chihuahua
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
World's smallest dog breed with long, flowing coat and bold, confident personality despite tiny size

History:
Ancient breed descended from Techichi dogs of the Toltec civilization, refined in Mexican state of Chihuahua and standardized in Mexico and the US. Recognized by AKC since 1904

Temperament: Alert, devoted, intelligent, confident, courageous, lively
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Mexico
Weight: 1-3 kg
Height: 13-20 cm
