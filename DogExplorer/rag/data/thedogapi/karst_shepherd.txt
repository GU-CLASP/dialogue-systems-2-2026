Breed: Karst Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A robust, medium-large livestock guardian dog with a thick iron-grey double coat and calm, self-confident disposition. Ancient breed from the Karst Plateau region developed for centuries to guard livestock from predators including wolves and bears.

History:
Ancient livestock guardian breed native to the Karst Massif region of Slovenia, used for centuries to guard livestock from wolves and bears. Originally recognized by FCI in 1939 as the Illyrian Shepherd Dog, renamed to Karst Shepherd in 1968.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, courageous, confident, calm, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Slovenia
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 30-40; Female: 25-35 kg
Height: Male: 57-63; Female: 54-60 cm
