Breed: Schnoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A crossbreed between a Poodle and a Schnauzer, combining intelligence, charming personality, and low-shedding coat. This mixed breed companion dog is known for its playful nature, with size and appearance varying significantly depending on the parent breeds used.

History:
Developed in the United States starting in the 1980s as a designer crossbreed created to combine the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat with the Schnauzer's sturdiness and alert nature for companion and family purposes

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, playful, alert, affectionate, devoted, energetic
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-16 years
Weight: 4.5-36 kg
Height: 25-66 cm
