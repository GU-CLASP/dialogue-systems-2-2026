Breed: Tosa Ken
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Japanese mastiff-type breed developed for dog fighting, known for its imposing presence, calm and dignified temperament, and loyalty to family

History:
Developed in Japan during the latter half of the 19th century by crossing native Shikoku dogs with Western breeds including Mastiffs, Great Danes, Bulldogs, and German Pointers to create a larger, more powerful fighting dog. Later bred for calmness and self-control.

Temperament: Calm, courageous, loyal, protective, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Tosa Province, Shikoku Island, Japan
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-91; Female: 41-73 kg
Height: Male: 55-69; Female: 55-64 cm
