Breed: Kars
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, rugged livestock guardian dog with dense weather-resistant coat and protective nature

History:
An ancient Turkish livestock guardian from the Kars region, developed to protect flocks from predators in harsh climates; closely related to other regional shepherd breeds. Recognized by Turkish registries and FCI Group 2 as a regional variant.

Temperament: Protective, independent, courageous, loyal
Breed group: Working
Origin: Eastern Anatolia, Turkey
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 40-60; Female: 35-50 kg
Height: Male: 71-81; Female: 69-76 cm
