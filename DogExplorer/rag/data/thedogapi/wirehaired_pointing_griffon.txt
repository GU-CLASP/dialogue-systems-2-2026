Breed: Wirehaired Pointing Griffon
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized versatile gundog with distinctive harsh, wiry coat and characteristic beard and eyebrows. Known as the 'supreme gundog' with excellent pointing and retrieving abilities and an intelligent, friendly disposition.

History:
Developed in the late 19th century by Dutch breeder Eduard Korthals, who worked in both Germany and France. His goal was to create the ultimate versatile walking-hunter's gun dog by crossing various pointing breeds, spaniels, and retrievers. Often called 'Korthals Griffon' in his honor.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, eager to please, loyal, energetic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Netherlands/France
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 16-27 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 51-56 cm
