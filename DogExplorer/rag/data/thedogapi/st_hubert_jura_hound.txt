Description:
A medium-sized Swiss scent hound of the Jura variety, distinguished by its noble appearance, long ears, and more substantial head structure indicating St. Hubert Hound influence. Known for exceptional stamina, tracking ability, and endurance in mountainous terrain.

History:
A variety of the Swiss Hound originating in the Jura Mountains, descended from ancient St. Hubert Hounds. Developed for tracking hare, roe deer, and fox in mountainous terrain. The 'St. Hubert' type shows heavier build and head structure indicating French St. Hubert Hound influence.
