Breed: Old Danish Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, strongly-built pointer of noble appearance with distinctive white coat and brown markings, bred for endurance hunting in Danish terrain and known for its calm, stable temperament

History:
Ancient Danish hunting breed also known as Gammel Dansk Hønsehund, dating back to around 1710. Developed by crossing local Danish farm dogs with Spanish Pointers and scenthounds to create a versatile, steady gundog with endurance for varied terrain. Nearly extinct after WWII but preserved by dedicated breeders.

Temperament: Calm, gentle, intelligent, loyal, confident, courageous
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Denmark
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 30-36; Female: 26-35 kg
Height: Male: 56-62; Female: 54-60 cm
