Breed: Russian Tsvetnaya Bolonka
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy toy dog with a sweet, loving temperament and a hypoallergenic wavy or curly coat. Known for its playful, affectionate nature and ability to bond closely with families.

History:
Developed in the Soviet Union (Russia) after WWII when dog imports were restricted. Bred from small dogs like the Bichon Frise, Shih Tzu, and Pekingese to create a perfect apartment-sized companion. Officially recognized by the AKC in 2026.

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, friendly, intelligent, loyal, sweet-tempered
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Russia
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 2-5 kg
Height: 24-26 cm
