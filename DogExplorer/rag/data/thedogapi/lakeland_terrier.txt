Breed: Lakeland Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, sturdy terrier from the Lake District of England with a dense, weather-resistant wiry coat and lively expression. Known for its confident 'big dog' demeanor in a compact body and bold, courageous temperament.

History:
Developed in the rugged Lake District by farmers to hunt foxes that preyed on lambs and sheep. Ancestry includes the old English Black and Tan Terrier and Bedlington Terrier. Recognized by The Kennel Club in 1921 and AKC in 1934.

Temperament: Confident, alert, courageous, intelligent, friendly, energetic
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Lake District, England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 7-8; Female: 6-7 kg
Height: Male: 34-38; Female: 33-37 cm
