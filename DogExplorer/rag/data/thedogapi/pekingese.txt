Breed: Pekingese
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact toy dog of Chinese nobility with a long, profuse double coat, distinctive flat-faced expression, rolling gait, and regal bearing originally bred for Chinese royalty

History:
Ancient breed developed in Chinese imperial court and held sacred in the Imperial Palace in Peking (now Beijing). Exclusively owned by royalty for over 1000 years until Western dogs were brought to England after British troops entered the Forbidden City in 1860 during the Opium Wars.

Temperament: Loyal, independent, confident, courageous, affectionate, alert
Breed group: Toy
Origin: China
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 3.2-6.4 kg
Height: 15-23 cm
