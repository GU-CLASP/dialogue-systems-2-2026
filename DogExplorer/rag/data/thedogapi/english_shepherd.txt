Breed: English Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Exceptionally intelligent and versatile medium-sized herding dog with a dense double coat and strong work ethic, developed as an all-purpose farm dog with devotion to family and adaptable temperament

History:
Developed in the United States from British and Scottish collie-type farm dogs brought by early settlers, bred as versatile all-purpose farm dogs for herding livestock, guarding property, and hunting vermin

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, alert, devoted, adaptable
Breed group: Herding
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-27; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 48-58; Female: 46-56 cm
