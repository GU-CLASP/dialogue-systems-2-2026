Breed: Kurdish Mastiff
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and ancient landrace livestock guardian dog originating from the Kurdistan region, known for its protective instincts, independent nature, and robust build. Also known as the Pshdar Dog, this breed was developed for strength, courage, and resilience in harsh mountainous conditions.

History:
An ancient landrace breed developed over centuries by Kurdish shepherds in the mountainous Kurdistan region primarily to protect livestock from large predators like wolves and bears. Valued for strength, courage, and resilience in harsh conditions, but not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, independent, courageous, alert, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Kurdistan region
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 50-82; Female: 40-70 kg
Height: Male: 71-84; Female: 66-79 cm
