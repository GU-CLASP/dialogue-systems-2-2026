Breed: Smithfield
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large historical herding dog with thick, shaggy coat originally used for droving cattle to London's Smithfield Market. Considered an ancestor of the Old English Sheepdog, this hardy working breed is now extinct in its original form, though similar types persist in Australia as landrace breeds.

History:
Historic droving breed originating in England, named after London's Smithfield Market where these dogs drove and managed cattle. Known for hardiness and intelligence, they were brought to Australia and Tasmania by early settlers. The original English Smithfield is now extinct, but similar types persist as landrace breeds in Australia. Considered a key ancestor to modern Old English Sheepdogs and Bearded Collies.
