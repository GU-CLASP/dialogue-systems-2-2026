Breed: Chukotka Sled Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Arctic spitz-type sled dog from northeastern Siberia, developed by the Chukchi people for sledding, endurance, and transportation in extreme cold conditions

History:
Ancient breed developed by the indigenous Chukchi people of northeastern Siberia for sledding, hunting, and survival in Arctic conditions. Traditional sled dog bred for centuries for transportation and endurance, considered the foundation breed for the Siberian Husky.

Temperament: Hardy, energetic, independent, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Northern
Origin: Chukotka Peninsula, Siberia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-22 kg
Height: Male: 53-60; Female: 51-57 cm
