Breed: Catahoula Leopard Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, athletic herding and hunting breed known for its distinctive spotted or merle coat patterns, striking heterochromatic (glass) eyes, and strong work ethic. This versatile working dog combines intelligence with independence.

History:
Developed in Northern Louisiana from Native American dogs crossed with Spanish and French settlers' dogs for hunting feral hogs and herding cattle. Named after Catahoula Parish and designated as the official state dog of Louisiana.

Temperament: Independent, intelligent, loyal, protective, energetic, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Louisiana, United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 29-41; Female: 23-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-66; Female: 51-61 cm
