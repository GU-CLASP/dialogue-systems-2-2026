Breed: Braque Saint-Germain
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant and athletic medium-sized French pointing dog with distinctive white coat and orange markings, known for its gentle temperament and versatile hunting abilities

History:
Developed in the 1830s at the royal kennels of Compiègne, France, from crossings between English Pointers and French Braque breeds, believed to involve an English Pointer gifted to King Charles X. Named after the Saint-Germain-en-Laye region and recognized by the FCI as a versatile gundog

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, affectionate, eager to please, loyal
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 18-26 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 53-58 cm
