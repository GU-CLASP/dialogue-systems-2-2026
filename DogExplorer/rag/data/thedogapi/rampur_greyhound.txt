Breed: Rampur Greyhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerfully-built sighthound from India with a lean, athletic build and short coat, known for incredible speed, endurance, and deep loyalty, originally developed for hunting large game in the Indian subcontinent

History:
Developed in the princely state of Rampur during the 18th-20th century by royalty including Ahmad Ali Khan, the Nawab of Rampur, by crossing Afghan Hounds, Persian Greyhounds (Salukis), and English Greyhounds for hunting large and dangerous game

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, independent, courageous, protective
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Rampur, Uttar Pradesh, India
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-34; Female: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-76; Female: 56-71 cm
