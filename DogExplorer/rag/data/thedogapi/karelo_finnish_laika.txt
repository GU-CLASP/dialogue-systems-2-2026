Breed: Karelo-Finnish Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, fox-like spitz-type hunting dog with a vibrant red-gold double coat, erect ears, and curled tail. Known for its intelligence, courage, and distinctive 'bark-pointing' hunting style used for tracking game birds and small mammals.

History:
Ancient spitz breed developed in the Karelia region from native spitz-type hunting dogs for hunting game birds, particularly capercaillie and black grouse. Standardized in late 19th century and recognized as Finland's national dog. In 2006, FCI merged the similar Russian Karelo-Finnish Laika into the Finnish Spitz breed standard.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, courageous, alert, independent
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Finland/Karelia region
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 12-16; Female: 11-14 kg
Height: Male: 44-51; Female: 41-48 cm
