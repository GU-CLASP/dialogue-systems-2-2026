Breed: Silken Windhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized graceful sighthound with a silky, moderately long coat and elegant athletic build, combining the speed of a sighthound with a trainable, affectionate temperament and gentle nature

History:
Developed in the 1980s by Francie Stull crossing Borzoi, Whippets, and small amount of Shetland Sheepdog to create a medium-sized sighthound with longer coat and biddable temperament

Temperament: Friendly, gentle, affectionate, intelligent, playful, calm
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-18 years
Weight: Male: 15-25; Female: 9-20 kg
Height: 46-60 cm
