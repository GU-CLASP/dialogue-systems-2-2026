Breed: Rajapalayam
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, muscular Indian sighthound with pristine white coat and distinctive pink nose. This powerful breed has a loyal, protective nature and was historically used for hunting wild boar and as a royal companion.

History:
Ancient breed named after the town of Rajapalayam in Tamil Nadu, developed as hunting and guard dogs by royalty and aristocracy in Southern India. Traditionally used for hunting wild boar and recognized by the Kennel Club of India.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, independent, alert, courageous, intelligent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Rajapalayam, Tamil Nadu, India
Life span: 9-12 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 65-75; Female: 58-68 cm
