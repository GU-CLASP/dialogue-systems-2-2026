Breed: German Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, muscular hunting spaniel known as the Deutscher Wachtelhund in its native Germany, with a dense wavy double coat featuring feathering and strong tracking abilities developed for versatile forest hunting

History:
Developed in Germany in the 1890s from older German landrace dogs known as Stöberhund, crossing various spaniels to create a versatile hunting dog capable of tracking, flushing, and retrieving game in dense forests and water. FCI-recognized and bred primarily for hunting ability and versatility rather than appearance.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, adaptable, confident, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 47-55; Female: 44-52 cm
