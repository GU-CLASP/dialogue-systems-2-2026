Breed: Styrian Coarse-haired Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, rugged Austrian scent hound with a distinctive coarse, wiry red coat, known for its perseverance and stamina as a hunter in mountainous terrain. Bred specifically for tracking wounded game over difficult, high-altitude terrain.

History:
Developed in the 1870s in the Austrian province of Styria by Carl Peintinger by crossing a Hanoverian Scenthound with an Istrian Coarse-haired Hound to create a resilient hound capable of tracking boar and deer in high-altitude, rugged mountainous terrain

Temperament: Work-focused, loyal, courageous, independent, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Styria, Austria
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 15-18; Female: 15-17 kg
Height: Male: 47-53; Female: 45-51 cm
