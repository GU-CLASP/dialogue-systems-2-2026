Breed: Eurasier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Spitz-type dog with balanced proportions and thick, plush double coat that comes in a variety of colors. Known for its calm, devoted nature and confident temperament.

History:
Developed in Germany in the 1960s by Julius Wipfel by crossing Chow Chow, Wolfspitz (Keeshond), and Samoyed to create an ideal Spitz-type family companion dog. Recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Calm, confident, loyal, intelligent, gentle, reserved
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 18-26 kg
Height: Male: 52-60; Female: 48-56 cm
