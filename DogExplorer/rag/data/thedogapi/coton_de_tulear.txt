Breed: Coton de Tulear
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small companion dog with soft cotton-like white coat, charming personality and cheerful temperament

History:
Originated as royal dog and companion to nobles in Madagascar, developed from small white dogs brought by traders. Named for city of Tuléar and recognized by FCI and AKC as beloved adaptable companion breed

Temperament: Happy, playful, affectionate, alert, friendly, intelligent, adaptable
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Madagascar
Weight: Male: 4-7; Female: 3.5-6 kg
Height: Male: 25-28; Female: 23-25 cm
