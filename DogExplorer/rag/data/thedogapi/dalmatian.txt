Breed: Dalmatian
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic dog with distinctive black or liver spotted white coat and elegant build, originally bred as a carriage dog with exceptional endurance

History:
Ancient breed with roots in Dalmatia, Croatia, originally bred as carriage dogs to run for miles alongside horse-drawn coaches, guarding horses and coaches. Also served as circus performers and iconic firehouse mascots, particularly associated with American fire departments. Recognized by AKC in 1888.

Temperament: Energetic, outgoing, intelligent, alert, athletic
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Dalmatia, Croatia
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 58-61; Female: 56-58 cm
