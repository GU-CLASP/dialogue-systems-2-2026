Breed: Chug
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small companion dog resulting from a cross between a Pug and a Chihuahua, known for its compact build, expressive face, and spirited yet affectionate nature

History:
A modern crossbreed developed in North America in the late 20th century by crossing a Pug with a Chihuahua. The goal was to create a small companion dog combining the affectionate nature of the Pug with the spirited personality of the Chihuahua. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Playful, affectionate, loyal, alert, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: 4.5-9 kg
Height: 25-36 cm
