Breed: Levriero Sardo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Rare, ancient sighthound landrace from Sardinia, Italy, known for its lean, athletic build and speed. This slender breed has strong hunting instincts and a reserved, independent temperament, traditionally used for hunting hares and small game across rugged terrain.

History:
The Levriero Sardo, or Sardinian Greyhound, is an ancient landrace sighthound native to the island of Sardinia. Believed to have descended from sighthounds brought to the region by ancient civilizations such as the Phoenicians, it has been prized by local hunters for centuries for its speed and stamina. The breed is rare and not recognized by major international kennel clubs but is documented in regional breed registries and is the focus of preservation efforts by local enthusiasts.

Temperament: Independent, alert, loyal, intelligent, reserved
