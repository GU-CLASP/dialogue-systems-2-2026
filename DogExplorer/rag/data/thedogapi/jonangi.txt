Breed: Jonangi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized agile Indian spitz-type dog with short smooth coat, webbed feet, and pricked ears. Known for its unique yodel-like vocalization and traditionally used for duck herding, hunting, and fishing activities.

History:
Ancient landrace from coastal Andhra Pradesh traditionally used by fishermen and shepherding communities for duck herding, hunting small game, and fishing activities. Faced near-extinction but recent preservation efforts are underway.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, loyal, alert, independent
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: Andhra Pradesh, India
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 13-23; Female: 13-20 kg
Height: Male: 43-53; Female: 41-51 cm
