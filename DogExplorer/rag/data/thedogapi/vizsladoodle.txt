Breed: Vizsladoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large active crossbreed combining the athletic ability and affectionate nature of the Hungarian Vizsla with the intelligence and low-shedding, curly coat of the Standard Poodle

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States by crossing the athletic Hungarian Vizsla with the Standard Poodle to create a versatile, friendly companion with the temperament and athleticism of the Vizsla and the low-shedding, hypoallergenic coat qualities of the Poodle. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, affectionate, athletic, loyal, playful
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 18-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-66; Female: 53-64 cm
