Breed: Tonya Finosu
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, spitz-type landrace dog from the Black Sea region of Turkey with a dense double coat, pricked ears, and curled tail. Known for alert watchdog capabilities, hardiness, and intelligent, friendly temperament.

History:
Native landrace from the Tonya district in Trabzon province, Turkey. Used for centuries as a multi-purpose farm dog, primarily for watchdog duties and hunting small vermin. Hardy breed adapted to the Black Sea region climate, recognized by regional Turkish organizations but not major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, energetic, loyal, friendly, courageous
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Tonya, Trabzon, Turkey
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 7-11; Female: 6-10 kg
Height: Male: 28-32; Female: 26-30 cm
