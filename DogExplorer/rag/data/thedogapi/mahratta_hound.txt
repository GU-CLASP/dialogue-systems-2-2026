Breed: Mahratta Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare, medium-sized sighthound from the Maharashtra region of India, known for its lean build, speed, stamina, and keen eyesight. Closely related to the Caravan Hound, this ancient breed is prized for its hunting prowess.

History:
An ancient sighthound breed originating from the Deccan Plateau in the state of Maharashtra, India. Traditionally used by local nobility and villagers for hunting hare, deer, and other small game. Maintained in rural communities as a rare landrace breed with no formal international breed standard.

Temperament: Independent, loyal, intelligent, alert, athletic

Origin: Maharashtra, India
Life span: 10-14 years

Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 53-66 cm
