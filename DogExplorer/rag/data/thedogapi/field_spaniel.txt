Breed: Field Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized sporting dog with silky, glossy coat in black, liver, or roan colors, built for endurance and agility in the field with an adaptable, noble temperament

History:
Developed in England during the late 19th century from Cocker Spaniels and other spaniel varieties, initially for show but later redeveloped for field work. The breed faced near extinction after World War II but was revived by dedicated breeders.

Temperament: Docile, intelligent, affectionate, gentle, sensitive
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 16-23 kg
Height: 43-46 cm
