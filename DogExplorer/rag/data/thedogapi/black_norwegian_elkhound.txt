Breed: Black Norwegian Elkhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type hunting dog with solid black double coat and robust, compact build. A bold, energetic breed smaller than its grey counterpart, built for endurance and agility as an all-purpose hunter with alert expression and confident demeanor.

History:
Ancient Norwegian spitz breed developed over centuries as a versatile hunter of large game such as elk, moose, and bear in rugged Scandinavian terrain. Smaller and less common than the Gray Norwegian Elkhound, also served as a watchdog. Recognized by the FCI but not the AKC as of 2024.

Temperament: Energetic, courageous, alert, independent, loyal, confident
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Norway
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-50; Female: 43-47 cm
