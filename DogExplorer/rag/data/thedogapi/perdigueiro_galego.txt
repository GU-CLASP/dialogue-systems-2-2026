Breed: Perdigueiro Galego
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, noble Spanish pointing dog from the Galicia region with a refined build, short dense coat, and excellent hunting instincts. Known for its calm temperament and versatile abilities as an upland game retriever.

History:
Ancient Iberian pointing breed native to northwestern Spain's Galicia region, developed for hunting upland game birds in rugged terrain. Nearly extinct but recovered through dedicated preservation efforts, formally recognized by Spain's Ministry of Agriculture in 2001 and Real Sociedad Canina de España in 2002.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, docile, energetic, alert
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Galicia, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years

Height: Male: 52-60; Female: 50-55 cm
