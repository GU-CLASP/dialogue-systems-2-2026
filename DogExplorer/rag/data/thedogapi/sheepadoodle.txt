Breed: Sheepadoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large crossbreed combining the Old English Sheepdog's gentle herding nature with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. Known for a shaggy coat and family-friendly disposition, with characteristics varying depending on genetic inheritance from parent breeds

History:
Designer crossbreed developed in the United States in the 1980s, gaining popularity in the late 20th century. Created by crossing Old English Sheepdog with Standard Poodle for a large, family-oriented dog with low-shedding coat qualities

Temperament: Intelligent, playful, gentle, energetic, friendly, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 29-39; Female: 20-29 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 53-64 cm
