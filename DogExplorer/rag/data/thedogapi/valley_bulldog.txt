Breed: Valley Bulldog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, muscular crossbreed with a broad head and compact body, combining the stockiness of the English Bulldog with the athleticism of the Boxer. Features a brachycephalic (short-nosed) face and is known for its loyal, courageous temperament.

History:
Developed in the Annapolis Valley of Nova Scotia, Canada, in the mid-20th century by crossing Boxers and English Bulldogs to create a healthier, more athletic companion with the Bulldog's temperament but fewer breathing and health issues

Temperament: Friendly, loyal, playful, courageous, intelligent, affectionate
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Annapolis Valley, Nova Scotia
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-36; Female: 18-32 kg
Height: Male: 43-53; Female: 41-51 cm
