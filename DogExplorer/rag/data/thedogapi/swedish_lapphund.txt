Breed: Swedish Lapphund
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type herding dog with dense black double coat and curled tail, bred by the Sami people for reindeer herding. Known for its lively expression, intelligence, and versatile working ability as both herder and guardian.

History:
Ancient Nordic spitz breed developed by the Sami people for herding and guarding reindeer in Arctic regions of Sweden. One of Sweden's three national breeds and oldest spitz breeds, officially recognized by Swedish Kennel Club in 1944.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, affectionate, friendly, energetic, loyal
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Sweden
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 19-30; Female: 17-25 kg
Height: Male: 43-51; Female: 40-47 cm
