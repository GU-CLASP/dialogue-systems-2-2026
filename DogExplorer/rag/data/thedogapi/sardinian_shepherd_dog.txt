Breed: Sardinian Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rustic livestock guardian native to the island of Sardinia, known for its robust build, weather-resistant goat-like coat, and strong protective instincts developed through centuries of guarding livestock in rugged terrain

History:
Ancient landrace breed native to the island of Sardinia, traditionally used by local shepherds for guarding livestock against predators for centuries. Developed through natural selection in relative isolation, preserving strong working instincts and hardiness. Also known as Pastore Fonnese or Cane da Pastore Fonnese, officially recognized by Italian Kennel Club (ENCI) in 2013

Temperament: Protective, loyal, independent, intelligent, alert, courageous
Breed group: Pastoral/Herding
Origin: Sardinia, Italy
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 29-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 56-60; Female: 52-56 cm
