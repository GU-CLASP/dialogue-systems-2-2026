Breed: Boerboel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful mastiff-type guardian breed from South Africa with a muscular build, short dense coat, and confident temperament. Known as a family guardian, the name 'Boerboel' translates to 'farmer's dog' in Afrikaans.

History:
Developed by Dutch and other European settlers in South Africa starting in the 1600s from European mastiff breeds and native dogs. Bred as all-purpose farm dogs for protecting homesteads from predators.

Temperament: Confident, intelligent, loyal, protective, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: South Africa
Life span: 9-11 years
Weight: Male: 68-91; Female: 50-68 kg
Height: Male: 61-69; Female: 56-64 cm
