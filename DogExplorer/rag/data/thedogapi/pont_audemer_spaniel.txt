Breed: Pont-Audemer Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, rustic French water spaniel with distinctive curly, water-resistant coat, topknot, and gentle, clownish expression. Specialized for wetland game retrieval with excellent swimming abilities.

History:
Developed in 19th century Normandy, France by crossing local water spaniels with Irish Water Spaniels and potentially Poodles for waterfowl hunting in marshlands. Numbers dwindled after World War II and was revived through careful breeding.

Temperament: Affectionate, intelligent, gentle, athletic, devoted, playful, loyal
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Pont-Audemer, Normandy, France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-22 kg
Height: Male: 52-58; Female: 50-55 cm
