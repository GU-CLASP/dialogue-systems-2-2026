Breed: Indian Spitz
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized spitz-type companion dog with pointed ears, curled tail, and dense white or cream double coat, developed in India from German Spitz ancestors and adapted for warm Indian climate conditions

History:
Descended from German Spitz dogs introduced to India by the British, developed and promoted by the Kennel Club of India through selective breeding for smaller size and adaptation to Indian climate conditions, becoming a popular family companion breed in the late 20th century

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, playful, loyal, friendly, adaptable
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: India
Life span: 10-16 years
Weight: Male: 12-18; Female: 10-16 kg
Height: Male: 35-41; Female: 33-38 cm
