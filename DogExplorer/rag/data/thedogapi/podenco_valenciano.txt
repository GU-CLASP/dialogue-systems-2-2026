Breed: Podenco Valenciano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, primitive-type sighthound from the Valencian region of Spain with lean, athletic build and large ears. Known for its keen hunting senses using sight, smell, and hearing, this agile breed was traditionally used for rabbit hunting across varied terrain.

History:
Ancient, native breed from the Valencian region of Spain, traditionally used by hunters for finding and catching rabbits. A rustic, resilient dog that is standardized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE) and recognized by FCI as a regional variety related to the Podenco Ibicenco.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, independent, intelligent, loyal, athletic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Valencia, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 18-24; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 51-61; Female: 48-57 cm
