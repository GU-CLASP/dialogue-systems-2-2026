Breed: French Bulldog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact muscular companion dog known for its distinctive large bat-like ears, flat smooshed face, and short smooth coat. Originally developed as a beloved companion breed with a playful, alert disposition.

History:
Developed in 19th century France when English lacemakers brought Toy Bulldogs to France and crossed them with local French ratters becoming highly fashionable in Parisian society

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, alert, adaptable, smart
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 7-13 kg
Height: 28-33 cm
