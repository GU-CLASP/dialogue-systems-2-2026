Breed: Kerry Blue Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, muscular Irish terrier with distinctive soft, wavy blue-gray coat and bearded face, known for its spirited, intelligent personality

History:
Originating in County Kerry, Ireland, developed as a versatile all-purpose farm dog for herding cattle, hunting vermin and small game, and guarding. Became a symbol of Irish patriotism with breed records dating to the 19th century; recognized by the AKC in 1922.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, playful, energetic, courageous
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Ireland
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-18; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: Male: 46-50; Female: 44-48 cm
