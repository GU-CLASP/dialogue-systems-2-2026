Breed: Segugio Italiano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient Italian scenthound of noble appearance with medium-sized, athletic build and distinctive hound features. Bred for speed and endurance in hunting, the breed comes in two coat varieties: shorthaired (pelo raso) and wirehaired (pelo forte), renowned for exceptional scenting ability.

History:
Descended from ancient hounds of Egypt and Phoenicia, the Segugio Italiano has existed in Italy for centuries. Refined during the Renaissance, this ancient breed has long been prized by Italian hunters for stamina, speed, and exceptional scenting ability in hunting hare and wild boar.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, determined, eager to please
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Italy
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 18-28 kg
Height: Male: 52-60; Female: 48-57 cm
