Breed: Dunker
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Norwegian scenthound with athletic build and distinctive harlequin, blue-merle, or marbled coat pattern, known for endurance and bred specifically for hare hunting

History:
Developed in Norway during the early 19th century by Captain Wilhelm Dunker, crossing Russian Harlequin Hounds with reliable local Norwegian scenthounds to create a superior hound for hunting hare on foot in challenging terrain

Temperament: Friendly, calm, gentle, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Norway
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 16-22 kg
Height: Male: 50-55; Female: 47-52 cm
