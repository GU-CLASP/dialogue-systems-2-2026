Breed: Briquet de Provence
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, elegant French scent hound with short coat and distinctive tri-color markings, well-proportioned with lean muscle built for endurance and hunting small game in Mediterranean terrain

History:
Ancient French scent hound from Provence region developed for hunting hare and small game. Faced near extinction after World War II but was reconstructed by M. Le Comte de Ravel and others, leading to breed club establishment in 1983 and official FCI recognition

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, alert, friendly, devoted, adaptable
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Provence, France
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-22 kg
Height: Male: 48-56; Female: 47-53 cm
