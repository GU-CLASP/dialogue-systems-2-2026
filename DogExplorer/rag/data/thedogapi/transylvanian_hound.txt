Breed: Transylvanian Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient Hungarian scent hound of medium-to-large size, known for its athletic build, endurance, and distinctive short black-and-tan coat. This courageous and determined hunter was specifically adapted for mountainous terrain and varied landscapes.

History:
Ancient Hungarian breed developed in the Carpathian Mountains region of Transylvania for hunting wild boar and other game in mountainous terrain. Prized by nobility for hunting prowess, the breed faced near extinction after World War II but was preserved by dedicated breeders and officially recognized by FCI.

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, loyal, energetic, independent, calm, adaptable
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Transylvania, Hungary
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 55-66; Female: 51-62 cm
