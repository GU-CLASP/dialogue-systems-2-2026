Breed: Poitevin
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, elegant French scent hound with distinctive tricolor coat and lean, muscular build. Bred for endurance and stamina in pack hunting, originally developed for hunting wolves and large game with exceptional scenting ability and melodic voice.

History:
Created in 1692 by Marquis François de Larrye from remnants of Haut-Poitou hound packs, developed for hunting wolves and large game. Nearly extinct after WWII but restored using English Foxhound crosses. Recognized by the FCI in the Scenthound Group.

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, independent, gentle, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Poitou region, France
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 62-72; Female: 60-70 cm
