Breed: Porcelaine
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant, medium-sized French scenthound with a distinctive glossy white coat that gives the breed its name, resembling porcelain. Known for its refined build, endurance, speed, and exceptional nose for hunting.

History:
One of the oldest French scent hounds, descended from white St. Hubert hounds in the Franche-Comté region. Dating back to the 18th century, the breed nearly disappeared after the French Revolution and WWII but was carefully reconstructed by enthusiasts in the mid-19th century using Swiss specimens.

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, energetic, friendly, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 25-28 kg
Height: Male: 55-58; Female: 53-56 cm
