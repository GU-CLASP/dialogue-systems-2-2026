Breed: Bullmastiff
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful guardian breed with short coat and calm, confident demeanor, originally bred to guard estates and known as 'The Gamekeeper's Night-Dog'

History:
Developed in mid-19th century England by gamekeepers crossing English Mastiff with Old English Bulldog to create an effective estate guard dog for tracking poachers

Temperament: Loyal, protective, calm, courageous, affectionate, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: England
Life span: 7-9 years
Weight: Male: 50-59; Female: 45-54 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 61-66 cm
