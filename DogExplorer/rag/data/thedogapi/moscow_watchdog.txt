Breed: Moscow Watchdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerfully built guardian breed developed in Soviet Russia with a pronounced guarding instinct, combining Saint Bernard and Caucasian Shepherd traits. Known for its balanced temperament, self-confidence, and devotion to family while maintaining strong protective instincts.

History:
Developed in the 1950s at Soviet 'Red Star' military kennels in Moscow post-WWII by crossing Saint Bernard, Caucasian Shepherd Dog, and Russian Spotted Hound. Goal was to create ideal military guard dog adapted to harsh Russian climate.

Temperament: Protective, confident, loyal, alert, intelligent, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Russia
Life span: 9-11 years
Weight: Male: 50-68; Female: 45-60 kg
Height: Male: 66-71; Female: 64-69 cm
