Breed: Bull Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A muscular, well-built terrier best known for its distinctive long, egg-shaped head that slopes down to the nose. This confident breed combines a playful, mischievous personality with a courageous temperament.

History:
Developed in 19th-century England by crossing old English Bulldogs with various terriers, originally for bull-baiting and dog fighting. James Hinks is credited with standardizing the breed and creating the distinctive egg-shaped head, earning them the nickname 'The White Cavalier'.

Temperament: Playful, courageous, energetic, loyal, mischievous
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 23-32 kg
Height: 53-56 cm
