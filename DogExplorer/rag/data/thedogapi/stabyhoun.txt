Description:
Medium-sized versatile Dutch pointing dog from Friesland with a distinctive sleek, longish black and white coat and gentle, calm temperament. One of the rarest breeds in the world and considered a Dutch national treasure.

History:
Developed in the Friesland province of the Netherlands in the 1800s as a versatile farm dog for pointing, retrieving game, and controlling vermin. One of the rarest breeds in the world and considered a Dutch national treasure.
