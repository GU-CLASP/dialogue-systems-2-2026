Breed: Lhasapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed between Lhasa Apso and Poodle, combining the intelligence and low-shedding coat of the Poodle with the loyal, alert nature of the Lhasa Apso. Features a fluffy, wavy or curly coat and affectionate, friendly temperament.

History:
Developed in the United States during the designer dog trend of the late 20th century by crossing the Lhasa Apso and Poodle. The goal was to create a small, intelligent companion dog with a low-shedding coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, affectionate, playful, alert, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 4.5-9 kg
Height: 23-33 cm
