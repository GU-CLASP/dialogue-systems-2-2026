Breed: Gascon Saintongeois
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, noble and powerfully built French scenthound with distinctive tricolor coat and elegant appearance. Known for its superb nose, melodious voice, and pack-hunting endurance, this athletic breed was developed for hunting large game in southwestern France.

History:
Developed in the mid-19th century by Comte Joseph de Carayon-Latour, who crossed the last remaining Saintonge Hounds with the Grand Bleu de Gascogne. Originally used for hunting wolves, now used for hunting deer and wild boar. FCI recognized.

Temperament: Gentle, calm, affectionate, loyal, intelligent, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Saintonge, France
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 26-30 kg
Height: Male: 65-71; Female: 61-67 cm
