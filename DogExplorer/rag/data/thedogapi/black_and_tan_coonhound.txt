Breed: Black and Tan Coonhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful American-bred scent hound with distinctive black and tan coat pattern and long ears, known for its cold nose, loud bawling bark, and determined trailing ability

History:
Developed in the American South by frontiersmen in the 18th century, descending from English Foxhound, Bloodhound, and Talbot Hound crosses for raccoon hunting and tracking over rugged Appalachian terrain

Temperament: Friendly, outgoing, intelligent, alert, adaptable, easygoing
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 29-50 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 58-64 cm
