Breed: Bakharwal Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful mountain dog bred for guarding livestock in the Himalayas, featuring a dense double coat and strong protective instincts

History:
Ancient livestock guardian of the Bakharwal and Gujjar tribes, used for centuries in the Himalayas for flock protection

Temperament: Loyal, protective, intelligent, courageous, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Jammu and Kashmir, India
Life span: 9-13 years
Weight: 35-60 kg
Height: Male: 61-76; Female: 56-71 cm
