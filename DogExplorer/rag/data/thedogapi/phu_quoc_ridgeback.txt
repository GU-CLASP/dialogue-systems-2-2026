Breed: Phu Quoc Ridgeback
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic primitive hunting dog from Vietnam, distinguished by a characteristic ridge of hair running along its back in the opposite direction from the rest of the coat. One of only three ridge-backed breeds in the world, this agile and intelligent dog originated on Phu Quoc Island.

History:
Ancient and primitive breed native to Phu Quoc Island in Vietnam's Kien Giang Province. For centuries used by local people as loyal companions for hunting, guarding property, and carting. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs; standards maintained by regional breed organizations.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, alert, independent, courageous
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: Phu Quoc Island, Vietnam
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-23; Female: 15-20 kg
Height: Male: 48-56; Female: 46-53 cm
