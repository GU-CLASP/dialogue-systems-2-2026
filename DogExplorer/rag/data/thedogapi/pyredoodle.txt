Breed: Pyredoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large mixed breed created by crossing a Great Pyrenees and Standard Poodle, combining the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat with the Great Pyrenees' calm, protective nature. Features a sturdy build with wavy or curly coat, though traits can vary significantly.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States to combine the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat with the Great Pyrenees' calm temperament and protective instincts. Recognized by designer dog registries but not major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, gentle, protective, calm, affectionate
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 39-54; Female: 34-48 kg
Height: Male: 64-81; Female: 56-74 cm
