Breed: Cretan Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient, primitive Mediterranean sighthound from Greece with slender, athletic build, erect ears, and curled tail. Known for exceptional speed, agility, and keen hunting instincts over harsh, rocky terrain.

History:
Ancient Hellenic breed native to the Greek island of Crete, with depictions dating back over 4,000 years. Developed for hunting hare and wild rabbits over harsh, rocky terrain. Regarded as one of the oldest European hounds and recognized provisionally by the FCI.

Temperament: Intelligent, independent, alert, energetic, athletic, gentle, affectionate
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Crete, Greece
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-25; Female: 15-23 kg
Height: Male: 52-60; Female: 50-58 cm
