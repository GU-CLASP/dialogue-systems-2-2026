Breed: Poodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An exceptionally intelligent, proud, and elegant dog with athletic bearing and curly hypoallergenic coat. Originally bred as a water retriever, this athletic breed excels as a companion and in various dog sports.

History:
Originated in Germany as a water retrieving dog, with name derived from German 'pudelin' meaning 'to splash in the water.' Later standardized and refined in France where it became a popular companion. One of the oldest hunting dogs recognized globally for intelligence and versatility.

Temperament: Intelligent, athletic, alert, confident, loyal, eager to please
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Germany, France
Life span: 10-18 years
Weight: Male: 20-32; Female: 18-27 kg
Height: 45-61 cm
