Breed: Eskapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium designer breed combining the fluffy coat and friendly nature of the American Eskimo Dog with the intelligence and low-shedding curly coat of the Miniature Poodle

History:
Modern designer breed developed by crossing American Eskimo Dogs with Miniature Poodles to create an intelligent companion dog with a low-shedding coat. Part of the popular Poodle-mix trend that began in the late 20th century. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, friendly, playful, affectionate, alert, energetic
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 7-11; Female: 6-10 kg
Height: 25-38 cm
