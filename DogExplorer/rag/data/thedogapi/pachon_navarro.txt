Breed: Pachón Navarro
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large-sized, robust Spanish pointing dog with distinctive split or 'double' nose, renowned for its hunting abilities as a versatile and athletic worker. This resilient and tireless breed is a prized hunting companion with excellent scenting ability.

History:
Ancient Spanish pointing dog with origins tracing back to the Middle Ages in the Navarre region. Famed for its scenting ability and distinctive double nose, it was a prized hunting companion for centuries. The breed faced near extinction in the 20th century but was revived through dedicated conservation efforts and breeding programs. Recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España and provisionally by the FCI.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, calm, work-focused, docile, alert, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Navarre, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-33; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 55-64; Female: 52-60 cm
