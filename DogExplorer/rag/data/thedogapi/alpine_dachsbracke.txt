Breed: Alpine Dachsbracke
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Sturdy, short-legged Austrian scent hound with dense double coat, originally bred for tracking wounded game in high-altitude Alpine terrain. Built low-to-the-ground with a robust, weather-resistant build designed for rugged mountainous conditions.

History:
Developed in the Austrian Alps during the mid-19th century as a durable scent hound for tracking wounded deer, boar, and hare over rugged terrain. Descended from larger Austrian hounds crossed with Dachshunds, it was a favorite of Crown Prince Rudolf of Habsburg and is officially recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, loyal, work-focused, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Austria
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 15-18 kg
Height: 34-42 cm
