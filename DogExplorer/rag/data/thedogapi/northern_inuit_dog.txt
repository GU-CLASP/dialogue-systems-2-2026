Breed: Northern Inuit Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic, wolf-like dog developed in the UK to resemble wolves while maintaining a domestic temperament suitable for companionship. Known for intelligence and loyalty but also independent nature.

History:
Developed in the UK from the late 1980s by Eddie Harrison (Edwina Harrison) crossing German Shepherds, Siberian Huskies, Alaskan Malamutes, and selected Inuit-type dogs to create a companion dog with wolf-like appearance. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs but governed by the Northern Inuit Society.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, loyal, independent, gentle, alert
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United Kingdom
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 36-50; Female: 25-38 kg
Height: Male: 58-76; Female: 58-71 cm
