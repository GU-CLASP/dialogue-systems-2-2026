Breed: Dogue Brasileiro
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, powerful mastiff-type breed developed in Brazil with strong guarding instincts, athletic build, and short coat. Known for its devotion to family and protective temperament.

History:
Developed in Brazil in the 1970s and 1980s by crossing various breeds including Boxers, Bullmastiffs, and local breeds to create a versatile family guardian and protection dog. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs but has official breed standard in Brazil.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, intelligent, courageous, calm, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Brazil
Life span: 9-12 years
Weight: Male: 36-50; Female: 31-45 kg
Height: Male: 56-66; Female: 54-64 cm
