Breed: Finnish Lapphund
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, sturdy spitz-type dog with a thick profuse double coat and fox-like features, originally bred for herding reindeer in arctic conditions by the Sami people

History:
Ancient spitz breed developed by the indigenous Sami people of Lapland to herd and guard reindeer in Arctic conditions. Nearly extinct after WWII but successfully restored by dedicated Finnish breeders, officially recognized by Finnish Kennel Club in 1945

Temperament: Friendly, alert, intelligent, calm, courageous, loyal, adaptable
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Lapland (Finland)
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 15-24 kg
Height: Male: 46-53; Female: 41-49 cm
