Description:
An intelligent and athletic mixed breed cross between Dalmatian and Standard Poodle, combining the Dalmatian's distinctive spotted appearance and dignified nature with the Poodle's keen intelligence and potentially low-shedding coat

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed by mixing a Dalmatian with a Standard Poodle to combine the Dalmatian's distinctive appearance and athletic ability with the Poodle's intelligence and potentially hypoallergenic coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.
