Breed: English Toy Terrier (Black & Tan)

Description:
Small, elegant black and tan terrier with fine-boned build and distinctive pointed, candle-flame ears

History:
Descended from the Old English Black and Tan Terrier, bred as a ratter in Victorian England and now a rare companion breed. Recognized by The Kennel Club (UK).

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, playful
Breed group: Toy
Origin: England
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 2.7-3.6 kg
Height: 25-30 cm
