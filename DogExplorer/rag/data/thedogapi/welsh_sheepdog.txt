Breed: Welsh Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized agile herding dog with weather-resistant double coat, bred as a traditional landrace working dog in Wales. Prized for stamina, intelligence and working ability over standardized appearance, this athletic breed was developed for herding sheep in rugged Welsh terrain.

History:
Traditional landrace herding breed developed over centuries by Welsh farmers for herding sheep in mountainous terrain. The Welsh Sheepdog Society was formed in 1997 to preserve this working breed valued for natural ability over appearance, maintained in working registries rather than major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, work-focused, independent
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Wales
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-25; Female: 14-20 kg
Height: Male: 43-56; Female: 41-53 cm
