Breed: Dandie Dinmont Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, long, low-bodied terrier with distinctive topknot of silky hair, large soulful eyes, and unique pepper or mustard colored crisp double coat. Originally bred for hunting badgers and otters with a gentle expression despite their tenacious nature.

History:
Developed in the 18th century in the border region between Scotland and England for hunting badgers and otters. Named after a character in Sir Walter Scott's 1815 novel 'Guy Mannering'. Recognized by The Kennel Club (UK) in 1875 and AKC in 1886.

Temperament: Independent, intelligent, loyal, affectionate, confident, gentle
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Scottish Borders
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 8-11 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
