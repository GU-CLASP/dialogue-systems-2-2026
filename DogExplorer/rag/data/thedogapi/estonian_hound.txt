Breed: Estonian Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strong-boned scent hound with short, smooth tricolor coat and sturdy build, known for its tenacity in hunting small game and stamina in Estonia's terrain

History:
Developed in Estonia between 1947-1954 after Soviet decree mandating each republic establish a national breed. Created by crossing local hunting dogs with Beagles, Swiss hounds, and English Foxhounds to create versatile hunting dog suited to Estonian terrain under restrictive hunting laws. Officially recognized by FCI in 2019.

Temperament: Friendly, energetic, alert, intelligent, calm
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Estonia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 20-30 kg
Height: Male: 45-52; Female: 42-49 cm
