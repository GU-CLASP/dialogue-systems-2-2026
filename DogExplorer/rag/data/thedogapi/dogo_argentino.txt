Breed: Dogo Argentino
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, all-white mastiff-type dog with massive head and athletic build, originally developed for big-game hunting with exceptional courage and strength

History:
Developed in Argentina in the 1920s by Dr. Antonio Nores Martinez who systematically crossed the now-extinct Cordoba Fighting Dog with Great Dane, Boxer, Bull Terrier, and Pointer to create a versatile big-game hunter for wild boar and puma

Temperament: Courageous, loyal, protective, confident, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Working
Origin: Argentina
Life span: 9-15 years
Weight: Male: 40-45; Female: 40-43 kg
Height: Male: 61-67; Female: 61-65 cm
