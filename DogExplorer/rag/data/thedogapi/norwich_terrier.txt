Breed: Norwich Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy, and compact terrier with distinctive prick ears, wiry double coat, and lively disposition. Known for its hardy constitution and fearless but affectionate nature.

History:
Developed in East Anglia, England in the 1880s as a rugged earth-dog for ratting and fox bolting. Originally grouped with Norfolk Terrier until official separation in 1964 based on ear carriage (Norwich has prick ears). Recognized by AKC in 1979.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, affectionate, courageous, outgoing
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: East Anglia, England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 5-5.5 kg
Height: 23-25 cm
