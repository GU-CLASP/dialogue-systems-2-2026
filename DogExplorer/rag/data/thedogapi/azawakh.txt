Breed: Azawakh
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant, tall sighthound from West Africa with a lean, muscular build, fine coat, and long legs built for speed and endurance in desert conditions of the Sahel region

History:
Ancient sighthound breed developed by nomadic Tuareg peoples of the Sahel region for hunting gazelle and other game, protecting livestock, and serving as companions. Bred for speed and stamina in Saharan and Sahelian conditions

Temperament: Independent, loyal, alert, protective, affectionate, aloof
Breed group: Hound
Origin: West Africa (Sahel region: Mali, Niger, Burkina Faso)
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 15-20 kg
Height: Male: 63-74; Female: 60-70 cm
