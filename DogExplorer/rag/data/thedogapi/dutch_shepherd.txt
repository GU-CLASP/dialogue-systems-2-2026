Breed: Dutch Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized herding dog with athletic, muscular build and distinctive brindle coat pattern. An intelligent, versatile, and work-focused breed of Dutch origin that served as an all-purpose farm dog.

History:
Developed in the Netherlands as an all-purpose farm dog for herding sheep and cattle, cart-pulling, and guarding. Nearly extinct after WWII but preserved through dedicated breeding efforts. Known for its brindle coat in three varieties: short, long, and wire-haired.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, alert, energetic, athletic, work-focused, independent
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Netherlands
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-34; Female: 19-30 kg
Height: Male: 57-64; Female: 55-61 cm
