Breed: Schipperke
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact, tailless spitz-type dog with a distinctive fox-like face, thick double coat, and abundant ruff around the neck. Known as the 'little captain', this agile black dog has a confident demeanor and alert expression.

History:
Developed in the Flemish regions of Belgium as a canal barge watchdog and ratter. The name means 'little captain' and the breed served as an adept guardian and vermin hunter on Belgian waterways for centuries.

Temperament: Alert, confident, energetic, intelligent, independent, curious
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Belgium
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 4.5-7 kg
Height: 25-33 cm
