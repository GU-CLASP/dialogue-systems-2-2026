Breed: Westiepoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small crossbreed combining the spirited West Highland White Terrier with the intelligent Miniature Poodle, featuring a curly or wavy low-shedding coat, compact body, and lively temperament

History:
Designer crossbreed that gained popularity in the United States starting in the 1990s, developed by crossing West Highland White Terriers with Miniature Poodles to create a small, intelligent companion with reduced shedding and the sturdy, confident nature of the Westie

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, playful, alert, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 7-14; Female: 6-13 kg
Height: Male: 25-43; Female: 25-41 cm
