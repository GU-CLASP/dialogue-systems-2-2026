Breed: Alapaha Blue Blood Bulldog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A powerful, athletic bulldog breed with distinctive blue merle or patched coat patterns and muscular build. Originally developed as a 'catch dog' for farm work, livestock protection, and family guardian with strong protective instincts.

History:
Developed in the Alapaha River region of southern Georgia as a multi-purpose catch dog for working cattle and hogs, and as a family guardian. Descended from Old English Bulldogs and recreated the original plantation bulldogs of the 1800s, maintained by the Lane family.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, intelligent, confident, alert
Breed group: Working
Origin: Alapaha River region of southern Georgia, United States
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: Male: 32-41; Female: 25-34 kg
Height: Male: 51-61; Female: 46-56 cm
