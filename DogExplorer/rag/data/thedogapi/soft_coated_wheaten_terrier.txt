Breed: Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, hardy and agile terrier distinguished by its soft, silky, gently waving coat of warm wheaten color and lively expression. Known for their joyful, affectionate nature with a steady, confident temperament.

History:
Ancient Irish breed developed over 200 years as versatile farm dog for herding, guarding property, and vermin control. Known as the 'poor man's Wolfhound,' gained official recognition from Irish Kennel Club in 1937 and AKC in 1973.

Temperament: Friendly, happy, intelligent, devoted, energetic
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Ireland
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-18; Female: 14-16 kg
Height: Male: 46-48; Female: 43-46 cm
