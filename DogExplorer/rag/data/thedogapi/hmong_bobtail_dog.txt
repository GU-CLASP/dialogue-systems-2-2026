Breed: Hmong Bobtail Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type dog with characteristic naturally short tail and thick double coat, developed by Hmong people as versatile working companion

History:
Ancient indigenous breed of Vietnam's Hmong people, traditionally developed for hunting, guarding, and companionship over many generations. Recognized by the Vietnam Kennel Association, not by FCI or AKC.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, alert, protective, affectionate
Breed group: Spitz-type
Origin: Vietnam
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-25; Female: 14-22 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 41-51 cm
