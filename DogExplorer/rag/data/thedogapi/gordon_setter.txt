Breed: Gordon Setter
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic bird dog with distinctive glistening black-and-tan coat. Built sturdily for work in rugged terrain, it is the heaviest and most substantial of the setter breeds, known for endurance and loyalty with an alert expression.

History:
Developed in Scotland by Alexander the fourth Duke of Gordon in the late 18th and early 19th centuries from black and tan setters. Established as a durable one-man bird dog capable of working long days in rugged Scottish Highland terrain.

Temperament: Confident, loyal, affectionate, intelligent, eager to please, alert
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Scotland
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: Male: 25-36; Female: 20-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-69; Female: 58-66 cm
