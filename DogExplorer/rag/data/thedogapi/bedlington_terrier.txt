Breed: Bedlington Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Distinctive lamb-like terrier with graceful, lithe build, arched back, pear-shaped head, and crisp curly coat that gives it a unique appearance among terrier breeds

History:
Developed in Bedlington, Northumberland in the early 19th century for hunting vermin in mines and farms. Known as the 'Gypsy Dog' due to use by poachers. Lineage includes Dandie Dinmont Terrier, Whippet, and Otterhound crosses. Recognized by The Kennel Club in 1877 and AKC in 1886.

Temperament: Intelligent, affectionate, energetic, playful, courageous, alert
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England (Northumberland)
Life span: 11-16 years
Weight: 8-10 kg
Height: Male: 41-44; Female: 38-42 cm
