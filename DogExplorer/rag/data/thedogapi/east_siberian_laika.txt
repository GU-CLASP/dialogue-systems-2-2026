Breed: East Siberian Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large rugged spitz-type hunting dog from the Siberian taiga with erect ears, curled tail, and thick double coat. Built for harsh climates with strong working drive and balanced temperament.

History:
Developed from aboriginal spitz-type dogs by indigenous peoples of the East Siberian taiga region for hunting big game including bear and wild boar. Standardized in mid-20th century for versatile hunting abilities and adaptability to harsh climates.

Temperament: Independent, alert, intelligent, courageous, loyal, work-focused
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Eastern Siberia, Russia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 18-23; Female: 16-20 kg
Height: Male: 56-64; Female: 53-61 cm
