Description:
A large, powerful wolf-dog hybrid developed in Russia for military and border patrol duties, combining the physical attributes and enhanced senses of a wolf with the working ability of a German Shepherd. This experimental crossbreed features a robust build designed for specialized working applications.

History:
Developed in the early 2000s at the Perm Institute of Internal Troops in Russia through experimental crossbreeding program between a female Carpathian grey wolf named Naida and specially selected German Shepherds. Bred for superior sense of smell, stamina, and strength for military and border security roles.
