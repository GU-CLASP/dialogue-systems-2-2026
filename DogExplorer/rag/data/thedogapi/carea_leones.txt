Breed: Carea Leonés
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, rustic Spanish sheepdog of the lupoid type with a dense, weather-resistant coat and strong herding instincts. Known for intelligence, agility, and alert, expressive demeanor in mountainous terrain.

History:
Ancient traditional herding dog from León Province in northwest Spain, used for centuries to manage and guide flocks, particularly Merino sheep. Highly valued for hardiness, intelligence, and unwavering work ethic in mountainous terrain and rural environments.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, work-focused, energetic, alert, athletic
Breed group: Herding
Origin: León Province, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 18-27 kg
Height: Male: 51-61; Female: 46-56 cm
