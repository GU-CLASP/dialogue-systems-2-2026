Breed: Pomsky
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Designer crossbreed between Pomeranian and Siberian Husky with striking wolf-like appearance in a compact, manageable size. Features thick plush coat, fox-like face resembling a miniature Husky, with varied appearance and size depending on individual inheritance.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed that gained popularity in the early 2010s through internet fame. Breeders aimed to create smaller apartment-friendly dog with Husky appearance by crossing with Pomeranian. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Playful, intelligent, energetic, alert, affectionate, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 7-14 kg
Height: 25-38 cm
