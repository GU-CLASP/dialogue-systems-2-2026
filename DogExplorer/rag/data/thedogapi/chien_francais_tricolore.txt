Breed: Chien Français Tricolore
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, elegant, and powerful French scenthound with distinctive tricolor coat, built for speed and endurance while pack hunting large game, particularly deer

History:
Created in the mid-20th century by Henri de Falandre by crossing the Poitevin and the Gascon-Saintongeois for large game hunting particularly deer. Officially recognized by the FCI in 1957

Temperament: Work-focused, intelligent, energetic, loyal, athletic, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 28-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 62-72; Female: 60-68 cm
