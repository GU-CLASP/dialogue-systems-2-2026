Breed: Black Russian Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and robust working dog with a distinctive dense black double coat and confident demeanor. Bred for military and guard work, this hardy breed combines courage with intelligence.

History:
Developed in the Soviet Union in the late 1940s by the state-run Red Star Kennel for military use. Created by crossing Giant Schnauzer, Airedale Terrier, Rottweiler, and other breeds to produce a large, trainable, and hardy guard dog.

Temperament: Confident, courageous, intelligent, protective, calm, loyal
Breed group: Working
Origin: Russia
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 36-59 kg
Height: Male: 68-76; Female: 66-74 cm
