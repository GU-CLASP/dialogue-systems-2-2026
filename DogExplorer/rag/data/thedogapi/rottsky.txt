Breed: Rottsky
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic mixed breed with well-muscled build and thick double coat, combining the strength and loyalty of the Rottweiler with the endurance and independent spirit of the Siberian Husky. Physical appearance and temperament can vary significantly.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed created by breeding Rottweiler with Siberian Husky, aiming to blend guarding instincts and loyalty with stamina and intelligence. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, protective, athletic, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-61; Female: 23-50 kg
Height: Male: 53-69; Female: 51-64 cm
