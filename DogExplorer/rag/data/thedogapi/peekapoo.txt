Breed: Peekapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small companion mixed breed with soft curled or wavy coat combining the Pekingese and Toy Poodle

History:
Peekapoo originated in the United States in the 1950s as a deliberate cross between a Pekingese and Toy Poodle, valued for companionship and hypoallergenic potential. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Affectionate, intelligent, alert, playful
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 4-8 kg
Height: 20-30 cm
