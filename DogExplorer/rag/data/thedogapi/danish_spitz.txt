Breed: Danish Spitz
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, compact spitz-type dog with a dense white double coat, pricked ears, and curled tail carried over the back. Known for its friendly, lively disposition and wedge-shaped head with alert expression.

History:
An old Danish landrace breed used on farms as an all-purpose companion, watchdog, and vermin catcher. The breed faced near extinction in the 20th century but was revived by dedicated breeders and officially recognized by the Danish Kennel Club in 1988

Temperament: Friendly, alert, intelligent, energetic, loyal
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Denmark
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 12-18; Female: 10-15 kg
Height: Male: 40-49; Female: 39-46 cm
