Breed: Gerberian Shepsky
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large athletic mixed breed combining the loyalty and intelligence of the German Shepherd Dog with the endurance and striking appearance of the Siberian Husky. A powerful crossbreed with thick double coat and high energy, though characteristics can vary significantly depending on genetic inheritance.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in North America in the late 20th or early 21st century, intentionally combining the German Shepherd Dog and Siberian Husky to blend intelligence, loyalty, working drive, and endurance. Not recognized by major kennel clubs as it is not a standardized breed.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, alert, protective, independent
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 20-41; Female: 16-32 kg
Height: Male: 53-66; Female: 51-64 cm
