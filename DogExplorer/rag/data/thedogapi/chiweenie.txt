Breed: Chiweenie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, spirited crossbreed resulting from a Chihuahua and Dachshund with a compact elongated body, short legs, and confident personality combining traits from both parent breeds

History:
Designer crossbreed that gained popularity in the late 1990s in North America, developed by crossing Chihuahua and Dachshund breeds to create a small companion dog

Temperament: Loyal, alert, playful, confident, energetic, affectionate
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 2-5 kg
Height: 15-25 cm
