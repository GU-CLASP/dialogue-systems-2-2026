Breed: Bracco Italiano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, noble Italian pointing breed with sculpted head, distinctive orange and white or chestnut and white coat, and pendulous ears. A powerful and athletic dog of ancient Italian lineage, known for its endurance and gentle, intelligent nature.

History:
One of the oldest pointing breeds in Europe with ancient origins dating back to the 4th-5th century BC. Developed as a versatile hunting dog for bird hunting in Italian terrain, adept at pointing and retrieving. The modern breed is thought to be a cross of the Piedmontese Pointer and the Lombard Pointer.

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, affectionate, loyal, calm, docile, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Italy
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 25-40 kg
Height: Male: 58-67; Female: 55-62 cm
