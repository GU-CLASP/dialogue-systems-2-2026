Breed: Tosa
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful mastiff-type breed originally developed for dog fighting, characterized by a calm, dignified demeanor and imposing physical presence. Known for its stately manner, patience, and courage with a naturally quiet temperament.

History:
Developed in mid-to-late 19th century Japan by crossing native Shikoku dogs with Western breeds including Mastiffs, Bulldogs, Bull Terriers, and Great Danes to create a formidable fighting dog with increased size and power.

Temperament: Calm, courageous, loyal, protective, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Tosa Province, Japan
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-91; Female: 36-73 kg
Height: Male: 60-66; Female: 55-61 cm
