Breed: Bergamasco Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-large herding dog with a distinctive matted coat consisting of natural 'flocks' that provide protection from weather and predators, combined with an intelligent, independent temperament rooted in ancient pastoral heritage

History:
Ancient shepherding breed from the Italian Alps near Bergamo, valued for intelligence and protective flocked coat that developed to shield from harsh mountain weather and predators while herding sheep, recognized by AKC in 2015

Temperament: Intelligent, independent, loyal, alert, gentle, calm
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Italian Alps
Weight: Male: 32-38; Female: 26-32 kg
Height: Male: 60-62; Female: 56-60 cm
