Breed: Romanian Mioritic Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog native to the Carpathian Mountains of Romania, covered in an abundant, long, shaggy double coat that is typically white or grey. Known for its imposing presence, calm demeanor, and strong protective instincts developed for guarding sheep flocks against predators like bears, wolves, and lynx.

History:
Ancient breed developed as a premier livestock guardian dog in the Carpathian Mountains of Romania, selected for courage and ability to protect flocks from large predators. First breed standard written by Romanian Cynological Association in 1981, provisionally recognized by FCI in 2005.

Temperament: Calm, protective, independent, loyal, intelligent, courageous, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Romania
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 45-65; Female: 36-60 kg
Height: Male: 68-75; Female: 65-72 cm
