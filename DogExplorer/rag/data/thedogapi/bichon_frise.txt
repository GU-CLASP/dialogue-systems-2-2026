Breed: Bichon Frisé
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, sturdy, and cheerful companion dog with a soft, curly white double coat, black eyes, and a lively, playful disposition

History:
Descended from the Barbet, a Mediterranean water spaniel. The breed was developed in France and became a popular companion dog for European nobility. The modern breed standard was established in France after World War I.

Temperament: Happy, playful, affectionate, intelligent, gentle, energetic
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: France / Belgium
Weight: 5-8 kg
Height: 24-29 cm
