Breed: Dogo Sardesco
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Molossian-type guardian breed from Sardinia with a sturdy, muscular build and strong protective instincts. Also known as the Pastore Fonnese, this ancient landrace breed was traditionally used for livestock and property protection.

History:
Ancient landrace breed from Sardinia used for centuries as a fierce guardian of flocks and property against predators and thieves. Provisionally recognized by the FCI as the Pastore Fonnese, though not recognized by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, courageous, intelligent, confident, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Sardinia, Italy
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 35-50; Female: 30-45 kg
Height: Male: 60-69; Female: 56-64 cm
