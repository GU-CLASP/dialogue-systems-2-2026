Breed: Miniature Schnauzer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy terrier-type dog with distinctive wiry coat, bushy eyebrows, and characteristic beard, known for its alert, friendly nature and square build

History:
Developed in late 19th century Germany by crossing Standard Schnauzers with smaller breeds like Affenpinschers and Poodles to create an all-purpose farm dog and efficient ratter for vermin control and companionship

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, alert, outgoing, energetic
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 5-9 kg
Height: Male: 30-36; Female: 28-36 cm
