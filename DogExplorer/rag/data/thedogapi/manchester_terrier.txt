Breed: Manchester Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Sleek, elegant terrier with distinctive black-and-tan coat and keen, alert expression. Known as the 'Gentleman's Terrier' in Victorian England, this racy, athletic breed exists in both Standard and Toy varieties with a clean-cut, spirited appearance.

History:
Developed in 19th-century Manchester by crossing Black and Tan Terriers with Whippets. Created as proficient rat-killer and rabbit-courser for working men of the industrial era. Recognized by AKC since 1886.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, energetic, spirited, independent
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Manchester, England
Weight: 5-10 kg
Height: 38-41 cm
