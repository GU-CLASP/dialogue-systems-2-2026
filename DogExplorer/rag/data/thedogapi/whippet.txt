Breed: Whippet
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, sleek sighthound known for its elegant athletic build with distinctive 'inverted S' shape, explosive bursts of speed, and gentle nature. Originally known as the 'poor man's racehorse'.

History:
Developed in Northern England during the 18th-19th centuries by working-class families, crossing small Greyhounds with fast terriers for rabbit coursing and racing. Recognized by AKC and The Kennel Club.

Temperament: Gentle, affectionate, calm, intelligent, playful
Breed group: Hound
Origin: England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 11-18 kg
Height: Male: 48-56; Female: 46-53 cm
