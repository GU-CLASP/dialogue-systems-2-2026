Breed: Barak Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rough-coated scent hound with distinctive tricolor coat, bred for hunting in mountainous terrain across the Balkans

History:
Descended from local hounds in the Balkans, developed for hunting wild boar and game in mountainous terrain. Recognized by FCI as the Bosnian Coarse-haired Hound (Bosanski Oštrodlaki Gonič – Barak)

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, independent, courageous
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Bosnia and Herzegovina
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 17-25 kg
Height: Male: 51-56; Female: 48-53 cm
