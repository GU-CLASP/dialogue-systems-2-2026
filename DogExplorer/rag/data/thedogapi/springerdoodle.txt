Breed: Springerdoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large crossbreed combining the English Springer Spaniel's athleticism and friendly sporting nature with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. This energetic, versatile designer breed often has a curly coat and makes an excellent family companion.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed primarily in the United States in recent decades to combine the English Springer Spaniel's friendly, athletic sporting nature with the Poodle's intelligence and hypoallergenic coat qualities. Also seen in the UK but not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, friendly, energetic, playful, affectionate, eager to please, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 16-27 kg
Height: Male: 46-64; Female: 43-58 cm
