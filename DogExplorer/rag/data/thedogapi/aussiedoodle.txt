Breed: Aussiedoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Designer crossbreed combining Australian Shepherd and Poodle traits, known for high intelligence, energetic nature, and often a low-shedding coat with varied coat types

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the late 20th century/1990s in the United States by crossing Australian Shepherd and Poodle to create a highly intelligent low-shedding companion dog

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, friendly, loyal, affectionate, playful
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-32; Female: 16-27 kg
Height: Male: 51-64; Female: 46-58 cm
