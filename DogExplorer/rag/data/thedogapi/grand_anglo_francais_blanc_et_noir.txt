Breed: Grand Anglo-Français Blanc et Noir
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and elegant French scent hound with distinctive black and white coat markings, bred for pack hunting large game with exceptional stamina and fine nose

History:
Developed in mid-19th century France by Comte de Chabot, crossing French scenthounds including old Saintongeois with English Foxhounds to create stamina, scenting ability, and pack hunting cooperation for large game

Temperament: Energetic, courageous, intelligent, independent, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 35-40 kg
Height: Male: 65-72; Female: 62-70 cm
