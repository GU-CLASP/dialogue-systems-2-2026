Breed: Icelandic Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type herding dog with thick weatherproof double coat, prick ears, and curved bushy tail, known for its cheerful and inquisitive nature

History:
Iceland's only native dog breed, brought by Viking settlers around 874-930 AD and bred in isolation for centuries. Essential for herding sheep and livestock in harsh Icelandic conditions

Temperament: Friendly, alert, energetic, intelligent, playful, confident, hardy
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Iceland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 14-16; Female: 11-14 kg
Height: Male: 46-51; Female: 42-47 cm
