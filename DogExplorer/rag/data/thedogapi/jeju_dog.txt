Breed: Jeju Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type landrace breed from Jeju Island, South Korea, with erect ears, curled tail, and keen alert expression. Known for brindle coat, strong hunting instincts, and courageous nature. Historically used for hunting badgers and roe deer.

History:
Ancient landrace native to Jeju Island, traditionally used for hunting and guarding. Nearly became extinct during Japanese colonial period and Korean War. Restoration project began in 1986, designated Natural Monument of South Korea (No. 540) in 2010. Very rare outside homeland, not recognized by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Alert, loyal, intelligent, courageous, independent, energetic
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Jeju Island, South Korea
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 15-25; Female: 13-20 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 43-52 cm
