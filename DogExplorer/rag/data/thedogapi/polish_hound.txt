Breed: Polish Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strongly built scent hound with robust build and distinctive black and tan coat, bred for stamina and tracking large game in dense forests. Known for its powerful, melodious voice and calm yet alert temperament.

History:
Ancient Polish hunting breed dating back to the Middle Ages, with origins documented in 14th century literature. Developed by crossing local hounds with imported St. Hubert Hounds for tracking large game in forests. Colonel Józef Pawłusiewicz was instrumental in re-establishing the breed after World War II. Recognized by FCI as national breed of Poland.

Temperament: Loyal, calm, intelligent, alert, gentle
