Breed: German Longhaired Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Noble, versatile medium to large gundog with elegant build and distinctive long, silky, weather-resistant coat with feathering. Developed as an all-purpose German hunting breed known for calm intelligence and athletic ability.

History:
Developed in 19th-century Germany from various pointer breeds, setters, bird dogs, water dogs, and scent hounds to create a versatile and noble gundog for work in field, forest, and water terrain

Temperament: Intelligent, gentle, calm, loyal, eager to please, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-36; Female: 20-32 kg
Height: Male: 61-71; Female: 58-66 cm
