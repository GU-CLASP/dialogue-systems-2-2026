Breed: Basset Bleu de Gascogne
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, low-slung French scent hound with distinctive blue-mottled coat, long drooping ears, and exceptional tracking abilities. Known for its noble expression and remarkable sense of smell.

History:
Descended from the larger Grand Bleu de Gascogne developed in medieval Gascony region for hunting small game. Nearly extinct but revived in early 20th century by breeder Alain Bourbon

Temperament: Gentle, friendly, alert, loyal, independent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Gascony, France
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 16-18 kg
Height: 33-38 cm
