Breed: German Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized scent hound with athletic build and tricolor short coat, bred for hunting in packs with elegant, resilient structure and muscular build

History:
The German Hound (Deutsche Bracke) was developed in the Westphalia region of Germany from various local Bracke types for hunting wild boar and deer. The official breed standard was established in 1900 after breed clubs merged in 1896 to unify the type.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, calm, confident
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Germany
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 48-53; Female: 46-51 cm
