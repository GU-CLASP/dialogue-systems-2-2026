Breed: Pumi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, agile Hungarian herding dog known for its whimsical, terrier-like expression, distinctive corkscrew-curled coat, and high-set semi-erect ears with an alert, lively attitude

History:
Developed in Hungary during the 17th-18th centuries from crossbreeding the native Puli with German and French herding dogs and terriers. Used as versatile farm dogs for herding cattle, sheep, and swine

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, alert, playful, loyal, confident
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 10-13 kg
Height: Male: 41-47; Female: 38-44 cm
