Breed: Segugio dell'Appennino
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, rugged Italian scenthound with a short coat (smooth or wire-haired varieties), specifically developed for hunting hare in the challenging mountainous terrain of the Apennines. This athletic, work-focused breed combines intelligence with the independent determination typical of scenthounds.

History:
Descended from ancient hounds of the Italian Apennines, likely influenced by dogs brought by Phoenician merchants. Traditional Italian scenthound developed over centuries specifically for hunting hare in the region's challenging mountainous terrain. Recognized by FCI provisionally in 2019 with both smooth and wire-haired varieties.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, courageous, work-focused, independent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Apennine Mountains, Italy
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 10-20 kg
Height: Male: 43-52; Female: 40-50 cm
