Breed: Can de Palleiro
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-to-large, rustic herding and guarding dog of lupoid (wolf-like) appearance, native to the Galicia region of Spain. Known for its sturdy build, weather-resistant double coat, balanced temperament, and versatility as an all-purpose farm dog.

History:
Ancient Iberian landrace breed from northwestern Spain, traditionally used as an all-purpose farm dog for herding cattle, guarding property, and livestock management. Maintained genetic purity through geographic isolation but faced near extinction by late 20th century. Subject of successful recovery program since late 1990s and recognized by Spain's royal kennel club (RSCE).

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, protective, work-focused, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Galicia, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 25-30 kg
Height: Male: 58-65; Female: 53-61 cm
