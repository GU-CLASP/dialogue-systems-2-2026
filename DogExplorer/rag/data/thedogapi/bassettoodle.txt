Breed: Bassettoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized mixed breed with long ears, short legs, and a wavy or curly coat, combining the Basset Hound's laid-back nature with Poodle intelligence and low-shedding coat

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States by combining Basset Hound and Standard Poodle, aiming to create a companion dog with the Poodle's intelligence and hypoallergenic coat combined with the Basset's friendly, easygoing temperament

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, gentle, playful, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 11-32; Female: 9-32 kg
Height: Male: 30-41; Female: 30-38 cm
