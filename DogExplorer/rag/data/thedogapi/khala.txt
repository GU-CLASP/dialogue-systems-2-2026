Breed: Khala
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare, primitive hairless landrace breed from Bolivia existing in two size varieties (Medio and Grande), with a distinctive sighthound-like build, hairless body, and tufts of hair on feet and tail. Also has a coated variety.

History:
A primitive landrace breed from the highlands of Bolivia, believed to have existed for centuries. Traditionally used as a companion and sight-hunter, closely related to other South American hairless breeds like the Peruvian Inca Orchid and Xoloitzcuintli. Recognized by FCI as Bolivian Hairless Dog (Khala).

Temperament: Loyal, independent, intelligent, alert, affectionate
Breed group: Sighthound
Origin: Bolivia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 7-15 kg
Height: 36-51 cm
