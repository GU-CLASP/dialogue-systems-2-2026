Breed: Croatian Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, agile herding dog with a distinctive wavy or curly black coat and athletic build. This intelligent and lively breed displays an alert expression and was developed for livestock work in Croatia.

History:
Ancient herding breed believed to have descended from dogs brought by the Croats in the 7th century, developed in eastern regions of Slavonia as versatile farm and herding dog. Referenced in historical documents from 1374, systematic breeding program began in 1935 with FCI recognition granted in 1969.

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, energetic, loyal, work-focused, eager to please
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Croatia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 13-20 kg
Height: 40-50 cm
