Breed: Denmark Feist
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium agile hunting dog of the Treeing Feist type, specifically developed for treeing small game, especially squirrels. Known for its tenacity, high energy, and alert nature with strong hunting instincts.

History:
The Denmark Feist is a specific line of Treeing Feist developed by the Slade family in Denmark, Tennessee, originating from dogs brought to the area in 1917. Developed from terrier and hound crosses, these historic American hunting dogs were prized by early settlers for their vermin-hunting and treeing abilities.

Temperament: Energetic, alert, intelligent, courageous, loyal
Breed group: Scenthound
Origin: Denmark, Tennessee, United States
Life span: 12-18 years
Weight: Male: 9-16; Female: 7-11 kg
Height: 38-46 cm
