Breed: Boykin Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, energetic, and versatile flushing and retrieving spaniel with rich liver or chocolate brown coat and enthusiastic work ethic, especially in water. Known for their amiable disposition and compact build ideal for hunting from small boats.

History:
Developed in early 1900s in South Carolina by L. Whitaker Boykin. Foundation sire was a small stray spaniel-type dog named 'Dumpy' who showed great hunting aptitude. Bred by crossing Chesapeake Bay Retrievers, Springer Spaniels, Cocker Spaniels, and American Water Spaniels for hunting in South Carolina swamps, particularly the Wateree River Swamp. Recognized as the state dog of South Carolina.

Temperament: Friendly, energetic, eager to please, intelligent, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: South Carolina, United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 14-18; Female: 11-16 kg
Height: Male: 39-46; Female: 36-42 cm
