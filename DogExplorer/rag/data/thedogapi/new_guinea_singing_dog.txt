Breed: New Guinea Singing Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Primitive medium-small wild dog known for distinctive howling vocalizations, fox-like appearance, and exceptional agility. Closely related to the dingo, this rare breed retains wild characteristics and is considered semi-domesticated.

History:
Ancient primitive breed native to New Guinea highlands and forests, believed to be one of the oldest dog breeds with limited human domestication. Very rare and considered wild or semi-domesticated, not recognized by major kennel clubs but noted for distinctive melodious vocalizations.

Temperament: Independent, alert, intelligent, energetic, cautious
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: New Guinea
Weight: Male: 9-14; Female: 8-11 kg
Height: Male: 36-46; Female: 33-41 cm
