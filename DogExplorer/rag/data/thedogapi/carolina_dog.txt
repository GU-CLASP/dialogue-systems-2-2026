Breed: Carolina Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic primitive breed with sand-colored coat and erect ears, resembling a dingo or small wolf with ancient pariah dog characteristics

History:
Primitive landrace breed descended from ancient dogs that accompanied early human migrations across the Bering land bridge. Discovered living as free-roaming population in cypress swamps of South Carolina and Georgia by Dr. I. Lehr Brisbin Jr. Recognized by UKC and seeking AKC recognition.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, independent, loyal, adaptable, gentle
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Southeastern United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 14-25 kg
Height: Male: 46-51; Female: 43-48 cm
