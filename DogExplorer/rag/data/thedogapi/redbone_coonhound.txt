Breed: Redbone Coonhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large athletic hound with a striking solid red coat, known for its speed, agility, and powerful scenting ability. Sleek and muscular, this breed was developed specifically for hunting raccoons and other game.

History:
Developed in the American South, primarily in Georgia, during the late 18th and early 19th centuries by Scottish immigrants. Created from red-colored Irish Foxhounds and Bloodhounds to produce a fast, hot-nosed hound for trailing and treeing raccoons and larger game. The breed name is attributed to early breeder Peter Redbone. Recognized by the AKC in 2009.

Temperament: Friendly, loyal, eager to please, energetic, affectionate
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 20-32 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 53-66 cm
