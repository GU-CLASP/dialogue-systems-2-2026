Breed: Segugio Maremmano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Italian scent hound with athletic build and strong hunting instincts, developed for wild boar hunting in the Maremma region of Tuscany. Known for its endurance, determination, and sturdy rustic build designed for challenging terrain.

History:
Ancient Italian breed developed in the coastal Maremma region of Tuscany for hunting wild boar and hare. Descended from ancient Roman hunting hounds and perfected by local hunters over generations for tenacity and skill in challenging terrain. Recognized by the FCI and Italian Kennel Club.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, courageous, independent

Origin: Maremma region, Tuscany, Italy
