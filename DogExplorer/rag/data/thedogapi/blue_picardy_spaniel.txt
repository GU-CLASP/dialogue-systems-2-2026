Breed: Blue Picardy Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, strongly built gundog with distinctive blue-gray roan speckled coat that gives it a bluish appearance. Known for its gentle hunting temperament and versatile field abilities.

History:
Developed in the Picardy region of France in the early 20th century by crossing Picardy Spaniels with English Setters (likely Gordon Setters) to produce a versatile, hardy pointing dog with the distinctive blue-speckled coat. Recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Gentle, intelligent, calm, devoted, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Picardy, France
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 19.5-22.7 kg
Height: Male: 57-61; Female: 55-60 cm
