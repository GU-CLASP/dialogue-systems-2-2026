Breed: Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An athletic and graceful medium-large sporting dog renowned for its speed, stamina, and keen nose. Known for its characteristic pointing stance and aristocratic demeanor, the Pointer is a hard-driving hunting companion with exceptional field abilities.

History:
The Pointer's ancestors are believed to have originated in Spain and were brought to England in the early 1700s. Developed by crossing Spanish Pointers with local scent and setter-type dogs, the breed was refined by British sportsmen into the premier bird-hunting gundog known for its iconic pointing stance.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, affectionate, even-tempered, adaptable
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: England
Life span: 12-17 years
Weight: Male: 25-34; Female: 20-30 kg
Height: Male: 64-71; Female: 58-66 cm
