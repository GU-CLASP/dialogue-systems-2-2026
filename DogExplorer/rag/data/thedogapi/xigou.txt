Breed: Xigou
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient Chinese sighthound breed, sometimes called the 'Chinese Greyhound', with a slender build, long legs, and elegant appearance. Known for speed, endurance, and calm demeanor, it comes in both smooth and long-haired varieties with several regional variations.

History:
An ancient sighthound breed from China, sometimes called the 'Chinese Greyhound', utilized for thousands of years in hunting and coursing wild game including hares. Has several regional varieties and played an important part in Chinese aristocratic and rural life. Its lineage is documented in Chinese art and literature dating back thousands of years and is recognized by the FCI and AKC Foundation Stock Service.
