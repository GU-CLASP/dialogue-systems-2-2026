Breed: Griffon Bruxellois
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, intelligent toy dog from Belgium with a distinctive bearded, almost human-like expressive face and alert demeanor. Available in rough and smooth coat varieties, known for their big personality despite their small size.

History:
Developed in Brussels as stable dogs and ratters from local rough-coated terriers in the 1800s, refined by crossing with Pug and English Toy Spaniel, became popular with Belgian nobility and coachmen

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, confident
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Brussels, Belgium
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 4-5 kg
Height: 18-25 cm
