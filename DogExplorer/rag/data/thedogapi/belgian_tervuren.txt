Breed: Belgian Tervuren
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant, athletic medium-sized herding dog with a long double coat ranging from rich fawn to mahogany with black overlay, known for intelligence, versatility and strong work drive

History:
One of four distinct Belgian sheepdog varieties developed in the late 1800s, named after the village of Tervuren. Originally used as a versatile herding and guard dog for flocks, prized for intelligence, trainability and work ethic

Temperament: Intelligent, courageous, alert, loyal, energetic
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Belgium
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-34; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
