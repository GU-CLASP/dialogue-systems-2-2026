Breed: Český fousek
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, wire-haired versatile pointing dog of noble appearance with distinctive beard, known for endurance, adaptability, and devotion as a hunting companion

History:
One of the oldest Czech hunting breeds, the Český fousek nearly became extinct after the World Wars but was restored in the 1960s through careful breeding programs using remaining typical specimens

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, energetic, devoted, eager to please, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Czech Republic
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 28-34; Female: 22-28 kg
Height: Male: 60-66; Female: 57-62 cm
