Breed: Rottle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic and intelligent mixed breed crossing the Rottweiler and Standard Poodle, combining the Rottweiler's protective nature and strength with the Poodle's intelligence and potentially low-shedding curly or wavy coat

History:
Modern designer crossbreed intentionally developed by mixing Rottweiler and Standard Poodle to combine the Rottweiler's loyal guardian nature with the Poodle's high intelligence and low-shedding coat qualities. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, protective, confident, affectionate, energetic
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 9-14 years
Weight: Male: 27-57; Female: 23-45 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 51-64 cm
