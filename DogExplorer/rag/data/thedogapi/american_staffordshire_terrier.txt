Breed: American Staffordshire Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A muscular, stocky, but agile medium-sized terrier of remarkable strength with a broad head, strong build, smooth coat and confident, good-natured personality

History:
Developed in the 19th century from crosses of bulldogs and terriers brought to America in the 1800s. Originally bred for bull-baiting and pit fighting, later refined to be larger than English Staffordshire Bull Terriers and developed into all-purpose farm dogs and family companions. Recognized by the AKC in 1936.

Temperament: Confident, courageous, loyal, intelligent, smart
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 46-48; Female: 43-46 cm
