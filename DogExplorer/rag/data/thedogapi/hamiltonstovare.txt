Breed: Hamiltonstövare
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Swedish tricolor scent hound with athletic build and noble appearance, bred for endurance and skill in hunting fox and hare over challenging terrain

History:
Developed in the late 19th century by Count Adolf Patrick Hamilton using English Foxhounds, Harriers, and German hounds including Holsteiner Hounds to create a dedicated Swedish hunting breed

Temperament: Friendly, energetic, intelligent, alert, independent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Sweden
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 23-34 kg
Height: Male: 52-61; Female: 48-57 cm
