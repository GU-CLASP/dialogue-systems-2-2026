Breed: Alaskan Klee Kai
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium spitz-type dog resembling a miniature Alaskan Husky with striking masked face, erect ears, curled tail, and lively, alert personality

History:
Developed in the 1970s by Linda S. Spurlin in Alaska to create a companion-sized version of the Alaskan Husky. Foundation stock included Siberian and Alaskan Huskies, with potential crosses of Schipperke and American Eskimo Dog to reduce size. Recognized by UKC but not currently AKC recognized.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, energetic, loyal, curious, playful
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Wasilla, Alaska, United States
Weight: Male: 7-10.4; Female: 4.5-8.2 kg
Height: Male: 38-43; Female: 33-38 cm
