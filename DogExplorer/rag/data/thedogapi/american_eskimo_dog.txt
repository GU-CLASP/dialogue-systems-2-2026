Breed: American Eskimo Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact spitz-type companion dog with distinctive dense white double coat, black points on lips, nose, and eye rims, wedge-shaped head, and plumed tail carried over the back. Available in three sizes (toy, miniature, standard) with an alert expression and striking appearance.

History:
Descended from white German Spitz dogs brought to America by German immigrants. Name changed from German Spitz to American Eskimo Dog during World War I due to anti-German sentiment. Became popular circus performers in the 19th and early 20th centuries, recognized by AKC in 1994

Temperament: Intelligent, alert, friendly, playful, energetic, eager to please
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: United States
Weight: 3-16 kg
Height: 23-48 cm
