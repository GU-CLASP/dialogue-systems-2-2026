Breed: Schillerstövare
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, noble Swedish scenthound with a distinctive tan coat and black mantle, known for its athletic nature and exceptional ability as a hunter of fox and hare. This energetic Scandinavian breed combines endurance with keen scenting abilities.

History:
Developed in the late 19th century by Swedish farmer Per Schiller, who crossed Swiss and German scenthounds with local hounds and English Harrier-type hounds. Created to be an exceptional hunter of fox and hare, first exhibited in 1907.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, loyal, courageous, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Sweden
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 55-61; Female: 52-59 cm
