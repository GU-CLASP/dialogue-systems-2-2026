Breed: Welsh Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large-sized, powerful and athletic scent hound with strong pack hunting instincts, built for stamina and endurance over rough Welsh terrain. Known for its distinct, ringing voice and rugged, weatherproof coat.

History:
Ancient breed descended from Celtic hounds, developed over centuries in Wales for fox hunting over difficult terrain. A distinct strain of British Foxhound adapted for Welsh conditions, officially recognized by the Kennel Club in 1922.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, independent, alert, loyal, gentle
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Wales
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 23-29 kg
Height: Male: 58-61; Female: 56-58 cm
