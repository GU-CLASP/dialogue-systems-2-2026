Breed: Chinese Shar-Pei
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient Chinese breed known for its distinctive deep wrinkles, hippopotamus-like muzzle, blue-black tongue, and compact, medium-sized build with a calm, independent temperament

History:
Ancient Chinese breed from southern provinces dating back to the Han Dynasty, originally used as all-purpose farm dog for hunting, herding, guarding, and farm work. Nearly extinct in mid-20th century but revived from very small population by dedicated breeders in 1970s

Temperament: Loyal, independent, calm, alert, protective, intelligent
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: China
Life span: 8-12 years
Weight: 20-27 kg
Height: 46-51 cm
