Breed: Bruno Jura Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, noble Swiss scent hound with tan to black coat and distinctive black saddle, known for its keen nose, endurance, and strong tracking instincts. Features long, dropped ears and a gentle, sometimes melancholic expression, bred for hunting in mountainous terrain.

History:
Ancient Swiss breed and variety of the Schweizer Laufhund, developed over centuries in the Jura Mountains for hunting hare, roe deer, and fox in challenging terrain. One of four Swiss running hound varieties recognized by FCI, with modern standard established in early 20th century.

Temperament: Independent, alert, intelligent, gentle, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Jura Mountains, Switzerland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 15-22; Female: 15-20 kg
Height: Male: 46-59; Female: 43-57 cm
