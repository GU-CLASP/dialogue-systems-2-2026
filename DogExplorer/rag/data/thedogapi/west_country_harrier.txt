Breed: West Country Harrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to substantial scent hound with a broad head, dense short weather-resistant coat, and powerful frame developed for pack hunting hare across the varied rough terrain of Southwest England. This rare or extinct regional variant of the Harrier retains stronger Foxhound influence.

History:
Traditional regional pack hound developed in Devon, Cornwall and Somerset for hunting hares over rough terrain, combining local harrier bloodlines with stronger Foxhound influence. Now considered extinct by most authorities, having been absorbed into other Harrier and modern English Foxhound lines over the 20th century. No major kennel clubs officially recognize the breed.

Temperament: Energetic, friendly, alert, independent, outgoing, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: West Country, England (Devon, Cornwall, Somerset)
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-34; Female: 22-31 kg
Height: Male: 51-57; Female: 48-55 cm
