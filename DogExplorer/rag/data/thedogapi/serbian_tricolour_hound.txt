Breed: Serbian Tricolour Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, robust scenthound with distinctive tricolor coat and solid construction, known for its tenacity, keen tracking ability, and lively temperament. Bred for hunting hare, fox, and larger game like wild boar in mountainous terrain.

History:
Developed in the Balkan region by crossing local Balkan hounds in early 20th century. First official standard drawn up in 1959 as 'Yugoslavian Tricolour Hound', officially recognized by FCI in 1961, renamed Serbian Tricolour Hound in 2006 after Yugoslavia's dissolution.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, loyal, alert, devoted, gentle
Breed group: Scenthound
Origin: Serbia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-30; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 44-54 cm
