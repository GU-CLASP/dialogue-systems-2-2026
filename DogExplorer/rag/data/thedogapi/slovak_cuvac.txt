Breed: Slovak Cuvac
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, robust white livestock guardian dog of the mountain type with thick double coat, known for its imposing stature and courageous, protective nature developed for livestock protection in Slovakia

History:
Ancient breed developed in Slovak mountainous regions for guarding livestock for centuries. After World War II, the breed was nearly extinct until Dr. Antonin Hruza established a breeding program to restore numbers. Officially standardized in 20th century and recognized by FCI in 1964.

Temperament: Alert, courageous, loyal, protective, independent, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Slovakia
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 35-45; Female: 30-40 kg
Height: Male: 62-70; Female: 59-66 cm
