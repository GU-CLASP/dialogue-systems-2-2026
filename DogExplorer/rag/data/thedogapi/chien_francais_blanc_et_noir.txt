Breed: Chien Français Blanc et Noir
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and elegant French scent hound distinguished by its distinctive black and white coat, bred for hunting large game in packs with endurance and persistence

History:
Developed in 19th century France from crosses of Gascon-Saintongeois and the now-extinct Montaimboeuf hounds, with English Foxhounds. Meticulously created as a powerful and enduring pack hound specialized for hunting roe deer and large game

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, work-focused, independent, loyal
Breed group: Hound
Origin: France
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 30-35; Female: 28-33 kg
Height: Male: 65-72; Female: 62-68 cm
