Breed: Spinone Italiano
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, robust, and muscular hunting dog with a distinctive dense wiry coat, expressive beard and eyebrows, and patient gentle expression. Built for all-terrain hunting with a rugged yet versatile build and methodical hunting style.

History:
Ancient Italian breed from the Piedmont region dating back to 500 BC, developed as a versatile gundog for hunting in dense, thorny terrain. Known since at least the Renaissance and valued for tracking, pointing and retrieving. The breed faced near extinction after World War II but was revived by dedicated enthusiasts.

Temperament: Gentle, patient, loyal, intelligent, docile, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Italy
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 32-37; Female: 28-32 kg
Height: Male: 58-69; Female: 56-64 cm
