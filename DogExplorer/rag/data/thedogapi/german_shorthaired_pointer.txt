Breed: German Shorthaired Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A versatile, athletic, and noble all-purpose hunting dog with a distinctive short, dense liver and white coat, known for its intelligence, energy, and friendly disposition

History:
Developed in 19th-century Germany by crossing Spanish Pointers, Bloodhound, and local German hunting dogs to create a versatile pointer-retriever capable of working on land and water. Recognized by AKC in 1930.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, energetic, eager to please, athletic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Germany
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 25-32; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 58-64; Female: 53-58 cm
