Breed: Smaland Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Swedish scenthound and the oldest native Swedish scent hound, featuring a compact, sturdy build with a tricolor coat and often naturally bobbed tail. Known for keen hunting ability and alert, energetic temperament.

History:
Sweden's oldest native scent hound with origins dating back to the 16th century, developed in Småland province for hunting hare and fox. Modern breed established in early 20th century from local scenthounds and imported European hounds, bred for natural bobtail trait and versatility in dense southern Swedish forests.

Temperament: Calm, intelligent, loyal, alert, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Småland, Sweden
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 13.6-20.4 kg
Height: Male: 45.7-53.5; Female: 43.2-50.8 cm
