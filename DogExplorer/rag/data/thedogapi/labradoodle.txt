Breed: Labradoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Energetic medium-to-large crossbreed between Labrador Retriever and Poodle with curly or wavy coat, originally developed as a hypoallergenic companion and guide dog. Size and coat type can vary significantly based on generation and Poodle parent size.

History:
First intentionally developed in Australia in the late 1980s by Wally Conron for the Royal Guide Dog Association of Australia. Created by crossing Labrador Retrievers with Poodles to produce a hypoallergenic guide dog combining the low-shedding coat of the Poodle with the temperament and trainability of the Labrador Retriever. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, energetic, gentle, outgoing, affectionate
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Australia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-29; Female: 20-29 kg
Height: 53-61 cm
