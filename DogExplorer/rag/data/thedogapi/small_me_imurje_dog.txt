Breed: Small Međimurje Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small Croatian hunting dog with compact build and keen scenting abilities, historically known as 'Međimurski ftičar' (little bird hunter). This rare, energetic breed combines primitive-type characteristics with scenthound abilities and makes a loyal companion and courageous watchdog.

History:
Developed in the Međimurje region of northwestern Croatia as a hunting companion for small game, traditionally known as 'Međimurski ftičar' (little bird hunter). Known locally for generations as both hunter and companion, provisionally recognized by the FCI in 2022.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, loyal, affectionate
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Međimurje, Croatia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 8-16; Female: 7-15 kg
Height: Male: 35-43; Female: 32-42 cm
