Breed: Rafeiro do Alentejo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog from the Alentejo region of Portugal with an imposing presence, dense coat, and calm, sober temperament developed for protecting herds against predators

History:
Ancient Iberian mastiff-type breed descended from dogs of the Tibetan plateau, used for centuries by Portuguese shepherds to guard flocks against predators in the Alentejo plains; recognized by FCI as breed 96

Temperament: Calm, confident, protective, loyal, independent, alert
Breed group: Working
Origin: Alentejo region, Portugal
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 45-60; Female: 40-50 kg
Height: Male: 66-74; Female: 64-70 cm
