Breed: Skye Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Long, low-built terrier with flowing double coat that parts down the middle and distinctive feathered ears. Despite its elegant, stylish appearance, this is a hardy and courageous working dog with a dignified demeanor, originally bred for hunting vermin on the Isle of Skye.

History:
Developed on the Isle of Skye in Scotland for hunting fox, otter, and vermin in rocky terrain. Gained popularity in the 19th century when Queen Victoria became a fancier. Made famous by Greyfriars Bobby in Edinburgh. Recognized by AKC in 1887.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, intelligent, calm, confident
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Isle of Skye, Scotland
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 16-20; Female: 11-18 kg
Height: Male: 24-27; Female: 23-25.5 cm
