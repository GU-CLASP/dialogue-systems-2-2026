Breed: Treeing Walker Coonhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large athletic scent hound with distinctive tricolor coat, bred for tracking and treeing raccoons and other game in rugged terrain. Known for swift endurance, intelligence, and distinctive baying voice.

History:
Developed from English Foxhounds brought to colonial America in 1742, specifically refined by Tennessee breeder Thomas Walker and the Walker family in the 1800s for speed, endurance, and ability to track and tree raccoons in American terrain.

Temperament: Intelligent, confident, friendly, energetic, courageous, alert
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 56-69; Female: 51-64 cm
