Breed: Chinook
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic American sled dog with distinctive tawny double coat, known for combining strength, endurance, and a calm, gentle temperament. This versatile working breed was developed for drafting and sled-dog racing in New England conditions.

History:
Developed in the early 20th century by polar explorer Arthur Treadwell Walden by crossing Mastiff-type dogs with Huskies and Belgian Shepherds. Named after the foundation male 'Chinook', bred to be an ideal sled dog for New England conditions. Recognized by the AKC in 2013.

Temperament: Intelligent, calm, devoted, friendly, eager to please
Breed group: Working
Origin: New Hampshire, United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 25-41; Female: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 58-66; Female: 53-64 cm
