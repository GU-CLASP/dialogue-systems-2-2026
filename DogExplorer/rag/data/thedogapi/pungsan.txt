Breed: Pungsan
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large spitz-type hunting dog from North Korea with thick white double coat and wolf-like appearance, known for strength, stamina, and resilience in harsh mountain climates

History:
Developed in the mountainous Kaema Plateau of North Korea as a versatile and resilient hunter of large game including wild boar. Considered a national treasure of North Korea. In 2000, a pair was given as a state gift to South Korea, increasing international recognition.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, intelligent, alert, independent
Breed group: Northern
Origin: Pungsan County, North Korea (Kaema Plateau region)
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 25-36; Female: 20-30 kg
Height: Male: 55-62; Female: 51-57 cm
