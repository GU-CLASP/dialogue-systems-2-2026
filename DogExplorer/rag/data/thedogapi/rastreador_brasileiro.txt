Breed: Rastreador Brasileiro
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic Brazilian scenthound with a short, dense coat, developed for tracking jaguar and other large game with exceptional scenting ability, known for perseverance and courage in hunting

History:
Developed in Brazil from European scenthounds and local dogs for tracking jaguar and large game in Brazilian terrain. Declared extinct by FCI in 1973 following disease outbreak but successfully reconstituted through dedicated breeding project and granted definitive FCI recognition again in 2019

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, loyal, calm, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Brazil
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 20-27 kg
Height: Male: 61-69; Female: 58-66 cm
