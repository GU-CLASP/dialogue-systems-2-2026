Breed: Cane Corso
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Italian mastiff-type dog with muscular, athletic build and confident demeanor. This intelligent and majestic breed was historically used as a guardian and hunter, known for its loyalty to family.

History:
Ancient Italian breed descended from Roman Molossian war dogs, developed as a versatile farmhand, guardian, and hunter of large game. The breed nearly faced extinction but was revived by enthusiasts in the 1970s.

Temperament: Confident, protective, intelligent, loyal, affectionate
Breed group: Working
Origin: Italy
Life span: 9-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-50; Female: 40-45 kg
Height: Male: 64-70; Female: 60-66 cm
