Breed: Wirehaired Vizsla
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Hungarian sporting dog with a distinctive dense, wiry coat and golden rust coloring. Features a robust yet lean build and versatile hunting ability, bred for pointing and retrieving in harsh terrain.

History:
Developed in Hungary in the 1930s by crossing the smooth-coated Vizsla with German Wirehaired Pointers to create a wire-coated hunting dog with weather-resistant coat suitable for harsh terrain and cold water

Temperament: Energetic, loyal, intelligent, affectionate, gentle
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-29; Female: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 58-64; Female: 55-58 cm
