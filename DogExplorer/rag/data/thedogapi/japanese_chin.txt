Breed: Japanese Chin
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, elegant toy breed with distinctive flat face, large wide-set eyes, and luxurious silky coat. Known for their aristocratic bearing and unique cat-like demeanor, often cleaning their faces and perching in high places.

History:
Ancient breed believed to have originated in the Chinese imperial court, then refined in Japan by nobility as cherished lap dogs and companions. Often given as gifts to diplomats and royalty, including to Commodore Matthew Perry who brought them to the West in 1853.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, affectionate, independent, playful, calm
Breed group: Toy
Origin: China/Japan
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 1.8-5 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
