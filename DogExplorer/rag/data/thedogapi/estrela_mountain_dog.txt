Breed: Estrela Mountain Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and imposing livestock guardian dog from the Estrela Mountains of Portugal with a dense double coat and protective temperament. Known for its intelligence, loyalty, and calm yet fearless demeanor, this athletic breed combines strength with a naturally protective nature.

History:
One of the oldest breeds in Portugal, developed in the Estrela Mountains as a livestock guardian to protect flocks from wolves and other predators. Ancient breed with lineage tied to Mastiff-type dogs of the region, recognized by FCI.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, intelligent, calm, courageous, independent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Serra da Estrela Mountains, Portugal
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 40-60; Female: 30-50 kg
Height: Male: 65-72; Female: 62-69 cm
