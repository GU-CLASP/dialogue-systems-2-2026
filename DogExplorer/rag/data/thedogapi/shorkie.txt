Breed: Shorkie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed combining Shih Tzu and Yorkshire Terrier traits, known for its big personality, silky coat, and spirited yet affectionate nature in a compact companion dog

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States during the 21st century by crossing Shih Tzu and Yorkshire Terrier to create a small companion dog with low-shedding coat and lively, affectionate temperament. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, intelligent, alert, loyal, confident
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-16 years
Weight: 3-7 kg
Height: 18-28 cm
