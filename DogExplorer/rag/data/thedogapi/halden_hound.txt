Breed: Halden Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, rectangular Norwegian scenthound with distinctive short, dense coat, built for speed and endurance as a hare hunter in challenging terrain. Features a proud yet gentle expression with the stamina and resilience needed for Norwegian hunting conditions.

History:
Developed in Halden, Norway in the late 19th and early 20th centuries by crossing local scenthounds with English, Swedish, and German hounds to create a swift, resilient hare hunter suited for Norwegian terrain. Recognized by FCI.

Temperament: Gentle, alert, intelligent, confident, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Halden, Norway
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-29; Female: 20-26 kg
Height: Male: 51-60; Female: 48-58 cm
