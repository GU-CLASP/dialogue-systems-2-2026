Breed: American Water Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized versatile sporting dog with distinctive curly or wavy chocolate or liver coat, bred for hunting waterfowl in the icy waters and marshy banks of the Great Lakes region

History:
Developed in the 19th century in the Fox and Wolf River valleys of Wisconsin as a compact, versatile hunter that could work from a skiff or boat. Ancestry likely includes Irish Water Spaniel and Curly-Coated Retriever. Named the state dog of Wisconsin in 1985 and foundation breed for the Boykin Spaniel, recognized by AKC in 1940.

Temperament: Eager to please, intelligent, energetic, alert, happy
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Wisconsin, United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 14-20; Female: 11-18 kg
Height: 38-46 cm
