Breed: Mudi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, versatile Hungarian herding dog with a wavy to curly coat, known for its intelligence, agility, and energetic nature. This all-purpose farm dog excels in various dog sports and herding activities.

History:
Developed in Hungary during the 19th and early 20th centuries as a versatile all-purpose herding and farm dog, likely from spontaneous crosses of Puli, Pumi, and German Spitz-type dogs. Nearly extinct after WWII but preserved by dedicated breeders. Officially recognized by FCI and AKC.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, loyal, adaptable, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 8-13 kg
Height: Male: 41-47; Female: 38-44 cm
