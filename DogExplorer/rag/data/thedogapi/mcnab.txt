Breed: McNab
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, agile herding dog with athletic build and exceptional working ability, known for intelligence, stamina, and versatility. Developed for ranch work in Northern California

History:
Developed in the late 1800s by Scottish immigrant Alexander McNab on his Mendocino County ranch, crossing Scottish Collies and local working collies with other herding dogs to create a versatile ranch dog suited to California terrain

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: California, United States
Weight: Male: 16-29; Female: 14-27 kg
Height: Male: 46-64; Female: 41-58 cm
