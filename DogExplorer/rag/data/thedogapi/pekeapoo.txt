Breed: Pekeapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small designer crossbreed combining Pekingese and Poodle traits, creating an intelligent and affectionate companion dog with a compact size and low-shedding coat

History:
Designer crossbreed developed in the United States in the 1950s-1960s as one of the earliest intentional hybrids, crossing Pekingese with Toy or Miniature Poodle to create a small companion dog with reduced shedding and playful temperament

Temperament: Affectionate, intelligent, alert, playful, loyal
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 4-9 kg
Height: 20-28 cm
