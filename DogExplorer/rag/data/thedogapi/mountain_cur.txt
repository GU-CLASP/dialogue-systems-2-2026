Breed: Mountain Cur
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rugged treeing hound and farm dog with strong, muscular build, bred as a pioneer's all-purpose hunting companion known for courage, athleticism, and excellent treeing abilities in mountainous terrain

History:
Developed by early settlers in the rugged Southern Mountains from European cur-type dogs, serving as invaluable all-purpose pioneer dogs for treeing game, farm work, and homestead protection. Formal breed registry established in 1956

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, loyal, energetic, protective
Breed group: Foundation Stock Service
Origin: Southern United States (Appalachian and Ozark Mountains)
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 14-27 kg
Height: Male: 46-66; Female: 41-61 cm
