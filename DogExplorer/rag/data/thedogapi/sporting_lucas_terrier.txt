Breed: Sporting Lucas Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy, and active working terrier developed for hunting ability with a harsh weatherproof double coat and compact build, known for its friendly and game nature

History:
Developed in the late 1940s by Sir Jocelyn Lucas in England, crossing Sealyham Terriers with Norfolk Terriers to create a smaller, more agile working terrier capable of accompanying hounds

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, friendly, energetic, courageous
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 6.5-8; Female: 5-6.5 kg
Height: Male: 28-33; Female: 25-30 cm
