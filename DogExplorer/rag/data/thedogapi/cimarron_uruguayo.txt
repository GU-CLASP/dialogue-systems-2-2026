Breed: Cimarrón Uruguayo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful molossoid breed with robust bone structure and athletic build, known for its balanced temperament, courage, and protective instincts. This rugged working breed features a short brindle or fawn coat and strong working heritage.

History:
Descended from dogs brought by Spanish and Portuguese colonizers, developed as a feral breed then shaped by natural selection in the wild. Used for herding cattle, guarding property, and hunting large game. Officially recognized in Uruguay in 1989 and recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, loyal, protective, calm, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Uruguay
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 38-45; Female: 33-40 kg
Height: Male: 58-61; Female: 55-58 cm
