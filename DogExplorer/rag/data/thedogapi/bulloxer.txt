Breed: Bulloxer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A powerful and muscular mixed breed combining the sturdy, stocky build of the English Bulldog with the athleticism of the Boxer. Appearance and temperament can vary, often possessing the Boxer's athletic frame and playful nature with the Bulldog's loyal, courageous disposition.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in recent decades in the United States to combine the athletic, playful nature of the Boxer with the loyal, courageous temperament of the English Bulldog, while potentially reducing breathing issues common in English Bulldogs. Not recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Loyal, protective, playful, affectionate, courageous, energetic
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 9-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-36; Female: 23-32 kg
Height: Male: 46-64; Female: 43-61 cm
