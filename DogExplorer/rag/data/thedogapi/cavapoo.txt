Breed: Cavapoo
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small companion crossbreed combining Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and Poodle traits, known for its soft wavy or curly coat, friendly nature, and affectionate temperament

History:
Modern designer crossbreed first intentionally developed in Australia in the 1990s by crossing Cavalier King Charles Spaniel with Poodle, aiming to create a friendly family companion with low-shedding coat suitable for allergy sufferers

Temperament: Affectionate, intelligent, friendly, playful, gentle, outgoing
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: Australia
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 5-11 kg
Height: 23-36 cm
