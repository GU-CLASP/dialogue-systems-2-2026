Breed: Kishu Ken
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, well-balanced spitz-type hunting dog of ancient Japanese lineage, known for its nobility, endurance, and dignified temperament. Features erect ears, curled tail, and typically solid white coat, though sesame and red are also found.

History:
Ancient Japanese hunting breed from the mountainous Kishu region of the Kii Peninsula, prized for hunting wild boar and deer. Designated as a Natural Monument of Japan in 1934 to preserve its purity and recognized by the FCI in the Spitz and Primitive Types group.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, alert, courageous, independent, reserved
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: Kii Peninsula, Japan
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 16-27; Female: 14-23 kg
Height: Male: 48-56; Female: 43-51 cm
