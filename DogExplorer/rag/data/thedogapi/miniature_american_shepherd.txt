Breed: Miniature American Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, athletic herding dog with a striking double coat and keen intelligence, essentially a compact version of the Australian Shepherd with intense work drive and devotion

History:
Developed in California during the late 1960s from small Australian Shepherds, bred for compact size while maintaining herding ability. Became popular with equestrians traveling to horse shows and was recognized by the AKC Herding Group in 2015.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, devoted, loyal, eager to please, work-focused
Breed group: Herding
Origin: United States
Life span: 12-13 years
Weight: 9-18 kg
Height: Male: 36-46; Female: 33-43 cm
