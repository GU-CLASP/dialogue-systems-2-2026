Breed: Miniature Fox Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, agile, and smooth-coated terrier known for its spirited, alert nature and compact build. Developed in Australia as a versatile vermin hunter and lively companion with an intelligent, courageous temperament.

History:
Developed in Australia during the late 19th and early 20th centuries from crosses of English Fox Terriers and other small terriers brought by settlers, refined as a versatile vermin hunter and spirited companion for farm work

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, confident, loyal
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Australia
Life span: 12-18 years
Weight: 3-5.5 kg
Height: 24-31 cm
