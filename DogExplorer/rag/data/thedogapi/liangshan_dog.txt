Breed: Liangshan Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A primitive, large native Chinese breed from the mountainous Liangshan region of Sichuan, known for its lean athleticism, protective instincts, and versatility as both a hunting and livestock guardian dog with exceptional courage and endurance

History:
An ancient regional landrace breed developed over centuries by the Yi people in the Liangshan mountains of Sichuan. Traditionally used both for hunting boar, rabbits, and mountain game, as well as protecting livestock from predators. Developed in isolation and recognized regionally in China but not by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, independent, intelligent, protective, alert
Breed group: Primitive
Origin: Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture, Sichuan Province, China
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 30-40; Female: 25-35 kg
Height: Male: 61-71; Female: 56-66 cm
