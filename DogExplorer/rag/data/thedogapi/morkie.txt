Breed: Morkie
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small mixed breed companion dog combining the gentle nature of the Maltese with the spirited personality of the Yorkshire Terrier. Known for its long, silky, low-shedding coat and toy-sized stature.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed that became popular in the late 1990s and early 2000s in North America. Developed by crossing purebred Maltese with Yorkshire Terrier to create a small, low-shedding companion dog. Not recognized by major kennel clubs but popular with designer breed enthusiasts.

Temperament: Affectionate, playful, alert, energetic, intelligent
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 1.8-4 kg
Height: 15-23 cm
