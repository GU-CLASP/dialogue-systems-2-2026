Breed: Samoyed
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A powerful and graceful medium to large Arctic spitz breed with a thick, all-white double coat and distinctive 'Samoyed smile' created by the upturned corners of the mouth

History:
Ancient breed developed by the nomadic Samoyede people of Siberia over 3,000 years ago for herding reindeer, pulling sleds, and companionship. Their thick coats protected them from brutal climate, and they often slept with families for warmth. Brought out of Siberia in late 19th and early 20th centuries. Recognized by AKC in 1906.

Temperament: Friendly, gentle, adaptable, alert, intelligent, playful
Breed group: Working
Origin: Siberia
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-29; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 53-60; Female: 48-53 cm
