Breed: Havanese
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy companion dog with long, silky double coat and cheerful temperament. Cuba's national dog, known for its intelligent disposition and making an ideal city companion.

History:
Developed from Bichon-type ancestors brought by Spanish settlers, became companions to Cuban aristocracy. Nearly extinct after Cuban Revolution but preserved by Cuban families who fled to the United States.

Temperament: Outgoing, intelligent, friendly, playful, affectionate, adaptable
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Cuba
Weight: 3-6 kg
Height: 22-29 cm
