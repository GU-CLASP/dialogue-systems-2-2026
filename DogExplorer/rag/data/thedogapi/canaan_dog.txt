Breed: Canaan Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized primitive spitz-type pariah dog with erect ears, wedge-shaped head, and bushy tail carried curled over the back, known for natural survival instincts and versatility as herder and guardian

History:
Ancient pariah dog from the land of Canaan that survived for thousands of years in the wild. In the 1930s, Dr. Rudolphina Menzel began redomesticating them for use as guard and service dogs. Recognized by AKC in 1997 and is the national dog of Israel.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, independent, protective, confident
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Israel
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 16-20 kg
Height: Male: 51-61; Female: 48-58 cm
