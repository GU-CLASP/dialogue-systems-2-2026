Breed: Great Dane
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Giant, regal breed known as the 'Apollo of Dogs' with a powerful, well-muscled body, sleek coat, and dignified presence combined with noble bearing despite imposing size

History:
Originally bred by German nobility from Mastiff-type dogs to hunt wild boar and deer, later refined as estate guardians and companions. Declared national dog of Germany in 1876 and standardized as 'Deutsche Dogge'

Temperament: Friendly, gentle, confident, loyal, affectionate
Breed group: Working
Origin: Germany
Life span: 7-10 years
Weight: Male: 64-79; Female: 50-64 kg
Height: Male: 76-81; Female: 71-76 cm
