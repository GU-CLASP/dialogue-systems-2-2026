Breed: Tatra Shepherd Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and purely white livestock guardian dog from the Tatra Mountains with dense double coat, imposing presence, and noble bearing. Ancient flock guardian bred for autonomous work protecting sheep from wolves and bears.

History:
Ancient breed developed for centuries by Goral highlanders in the Polish Tatra Mountains as independent livestock guardian protecting flocks from wolves and bears. White coat helped shepherds distinguish it from predators at night. Recognized by FCI and The Kennel Club (UK).

Temperament: Protective, intelligent, calm, confident, loyal, independent, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Tatra Mountains, Poland
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 40-59; Female: 36-51 kg
Height: Male: 65-71; Female: 60-66 cm
