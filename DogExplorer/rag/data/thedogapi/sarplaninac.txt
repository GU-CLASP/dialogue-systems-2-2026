Breed: Šarplaninac
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, robust livestock guardian dog from the Balkan region with a dense, weather-resistant double coat and calm, independent protective nature. This ancient molossoid-type breed is known for its powerful build and natural guardian instincts.

History:
Ancient molossoid-type livestock guardian breed developed in the Šar Mountains region, used for centuries to protect sheep from wolves and bears. First standardized by the Yugoslavian Kennel Club in 1939 and recognized by the FCI in 1957 as the Yugoslav Shepherd Dog—Sharplanina.

Temperament: Independent, protective, loyal, calm, courageous, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Šar Mountains region (Serbia, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Albania)
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 35-55; Female: 30-50 kg
Height: Male: 61-70; Female: 56-66 cm
