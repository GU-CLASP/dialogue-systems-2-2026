Breed: Foxhoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A modern mixed breed cross between a Foxhound and Standard Poodle that combines the Poodle's trainability and potentially low-shedding coat with the Foxhound's stamina, scenting abilities, and pack mentality. Coat type and temperament can vary significantly depending on which parent's traits are more dominant.

History:
A modern designer crossbreed created by mixing American or English Foxhound with Standard Poodle to combine scenting ability, stamina, and sociable nature with intelligence and potentially lower-shedding coat. Not recognized by major kennel clubs
