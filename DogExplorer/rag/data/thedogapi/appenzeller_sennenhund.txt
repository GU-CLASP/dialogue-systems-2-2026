Breed: Appenzeller Sennenhund
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, muscular and agile Swiss mountain dog with distinctive tricolor coat and tightly curled tail carried over the back. Known for their lively, self-assured expression and work-focused temperament, this ancient breed combines herding instincts with guarding abilities.

History:
One of the four Swiss Sennenhund breeds, this ancient breed from the Appenzell region was used for centuries as an all-purpose farm dog for herding cattle, guarding property, and pulling carts. Formally established in the late 19th century through the efforts of forester Max Siber and Professor Albert Heim.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, confident, loyal, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Working
Origin: Appenzell region, Switzerland
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 22-32; Female: 20-28 kg
Height: Male: 51-56; Female: 48-54 cm
