Description:
Medium-sized, rustic-looking French scenthound with distinctive blue-mottled, rough coat and strong hunting instincts. Known for exceptional stamina and a melodious voice, this persistent and versatile hunter combines keen scenting ability with rugged durability.

History:
Ancient French hound developed in the Gascogne region by crossing Grand Bleu de Gascogne with rough-coated griffon breeds to create a robust, keen-nosed hunting hound suited for rough terrain. Prized for exceptional nose and stamina.
