Breed: Huntaway
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, sturdily-built herding dog from New Zealand, known for its deep, powerful bark used to drive sheep over large distances using voice and physical presence

History:
Developed in late 19th and early 20th century New Zealand by farmers who crossed various British sheepdogs, collies, and herding breeds to create a dog that could move large flocks of sheep over vast distances using its powerful voice and stamina

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, work-focused, loyal, adaptable
Breed group: Herding
Origin: New Zealand
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 29-40; Female: 25-35 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-63 cm
