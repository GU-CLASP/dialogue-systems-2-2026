Breed: Goldendoodle
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large crossbreed between a Golden Retriever and Poodle, known for its friendly temperament, intelligent nature, and low-shedding wavy or curly coat. As a mixed breed, appearance and temperament can vary by generation.

History:
Designer crossbreed first developed in the late 20th century, becoming popular in the 1990s. Originally created to produce a guide dog for individuals with allergies by combining the low-shedding coat of the Poodle with the gentle, trainable nature of the Golden Retriever.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, gentle, affectionate, outgoing, playful
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States, Australia
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: 23-41 kg
Height: 51-61 cm
