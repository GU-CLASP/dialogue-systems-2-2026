Breed: Cesky Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A well-muscled, short-legged hunting terrier with a silky, wavy coat and natural drop ears, known for its calm and gentle temperament compared to other terriers. Bred for hunting in Bohemian forests, this less-active and quieter terrier was developed to work effectively in packs.

History:
Developed in the mid-20th century by Czech breeder František Horák, who crossed Scottish Terriers with Sealyham Terriers to create a tenacious but more tractable hunting terrier for the forests of Bohemia. Recognized by the FCI in 1963 and AKC in 2011.

Temperament: Calm, gentle, playful, intelligent, loyal, devoted
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Czech Republic
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 6-11 kg
Height: 25-33 cm
