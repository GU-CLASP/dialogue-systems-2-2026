Breed: Rize Koyun
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A rare, large and powerful Turkish livestock guardian dog from the Rize province, featuring a thick double coat and protective temperament. This regional landrace is specifically adapted for protecting flocks in the mountainous Black Sea region.

History:
A landrace livestock guardian dog developed over centuries by shepherds in Turkey's Rize province in the Black Sea region. Bred to protect sheep and other livestock from predators in rugged, mountainous terrain. Shares ancestry with other Turkish shepherd breeds but specifically adapted for local climate and terrain. Not recognized as a standardized breed by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Protective, independent, courageous, loyal, alert
Breed group: Working
Origin: Rize Province, Turkey
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 45-59; Female: 40-55 kg
Height: Male: 69-79; Female: 66-74 cm
