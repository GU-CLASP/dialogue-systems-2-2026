Breed: Airedale Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
The largest of all terrier breeds, the Airedale is a large, athletic dog with a distinctive wiry black and tan coat and confident, alert demeanor. Known as the 'King of Terriers', this highly intelligent and versatile breed combines courage with an outgoing temperament.

History:
Developed in the Aire Valley of Yorkshire, England, by crossing Otterhounds with various terrier breeds. Originally bred by working-class fanciers to hunt otters and rats, their intelligence and courage led to their use as police dogs, war dogs, and all-around companions.

Temperament: Confident, intelligent, courageous, alert, energetic, outgoing
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Yorkshire, England
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 23-32; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 58-61; Female: 56-58 cm
