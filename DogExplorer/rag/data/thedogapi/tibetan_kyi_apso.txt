Breed: Tibetan Kyi Apso
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful Tibetan guardian dog with dense, shaggy double coat, developed for guarding livestock in harsh mountain climates. Known as the 'Bearded Lion Dog', this rugged working breed combines protective instincts with independent temperament.

History:
Ancient Tibetan guardian breed developed by nomadic herders to protect livestock and property in harsh Himalayan mountain conditions. Known as the 'Bearded Lion Dog' and considered holy bearers of good luck. Related to but distinct from the Tibetan Mastiff.

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, alert, intelligent
Breed group: Guardian
Origin: Tibet
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 35-54; Female: 30-45 kg
Height: Male: 61-76; Female: 56-71 cm
