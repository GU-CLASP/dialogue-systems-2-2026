Breed: Tibetan Mastiff
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient and imposing guardian breed with massive size, noble bearing, and dense double coat. Known for its independent nature, impressive presence, and deep, powerful bark.

History:
Ancient guardian breed developed in the isolated Himalayas, used by Tibetan nomads and monasteries to protect livestock, villages, and property. Considered a progenitor of many modern mastiff breeds.

Temperament: Independent, protective, intelligent, loyal, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: Tibet
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-73; Female: 34-54 kg
Height: Male: 66-74; Female: 61-69 cm
