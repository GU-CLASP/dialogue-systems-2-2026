Breed: Sarabi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A large, powerful livestock guardian landrace breed from Sarab County in Iran, known for its massive frame, protective instincts, loyalty, and imposing presence

History:
An ancient landrace breed from the Sarab region of northwestern Iran, developed over centuries as a powerful livestock guardian dog to protect flocks from large predators such as wolves and bears. Not officially recognized by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Protective, independent, calm, loyal, courageous
Breed group: Working
Origin: Sarab County, East Azerbaijan Province, Iran
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 54-90; Female: 45-77 kg
Height: Male: 71-89; Female: 71-81 cm
