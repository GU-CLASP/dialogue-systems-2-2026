Breed: Pražský Krysařík
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Very small, elegant toy breed with fine bone structure, alert expression, and smooth or long coat varieties. Originally bred in Bohemia as a companion and ratter in royal courts.

History:
Ancient Czech breed dating to Middle Ages, developed as companion and ratting dog favored by nobility in Prague Castle and royal courts. Recognized by FCI as Czech national breed and revived from near-extinction in the 1980s.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, affectionate, lively, playful, confident
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Czech Republic
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 1-3 kg
Height: Male: 20-24; Female: 20-23 cm
