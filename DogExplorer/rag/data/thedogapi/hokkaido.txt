Breed: Hokkaido
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, athletic spitz-type hunting dog with thick double coat, prick ears, curled tail, and alert expression. Originally bred by the Ainu people for hunting large game in harsh climates of Hokkaido, Japan.

History:
Ancient Japanese breed developed by the indigenous Ainu people of Hokkaido for hunting large game such as bears and deer. Designated as living natural monument in Japan in 1937.

Temperament: Alert, loyal, courageous, intelligent, independent, devoted
Breed group: Working
Origin: Hokkaido, Japan
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: Male: 20-30; Female: 20-26 kg
Height: Male: 46-54; Female: 46-51 cm
