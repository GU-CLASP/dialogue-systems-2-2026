Breed: West Siberian Laika
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized spitz-type hunting dog with wolf-like features, erect ears, curled tail, and dense double coat. Known for its alertness, versatility, and adaptability, this strong and agile breed was developed for hunting in Siberian forests.

History:
Ancient spitz breed developed by the Mansi and Khanty indigenous peoples of Russia's Ural Mountains and West Siberia for hunting versatility. First breed standard established in the 1920s, refined and standardized in the Soviet era, and recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Alert, independent, loyal, energetic, intelligent
Breed group: Spitz and Primitive Types
Origin: West Siberia, Russia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 16-25; Female: 16-23 kg
Height: Male: 53-62; Female: 50-58 cm
