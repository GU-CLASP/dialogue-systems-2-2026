Breed: Kazakh Tazy
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient Central Asian sighthound with elegant, long-legged build and characteristic feathering on ears, tail, and legs. Known for exceptional speed, stamina, and endurance, developed for hunting in harsh steppe conditions.

History:
Ancient sighthound developed by nomadic Kazakh tribes for hunting game such as fox, hare, badger, and wolf across vast steppes. Name 'Tazy' means 'pure' or 'noble' reflecting esteemed status. Provisionally recognized by FCI in 2024, preservation efforts ongoing.

Temperament: Independent, intelligent, alert, loyal, calm, confident
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Kazakhstan
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-29; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 60-70; Female: 55-65 cm
