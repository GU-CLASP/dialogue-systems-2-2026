Breed: Karelian Bear Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, sturdily-built spitz-type hunting dog with distinctive black and white coat, bred for courage and tenacity in hunting large game including bears, moose, and wild boar

History:
Ancient breed developed in the Karelia region of Finland and Russia from spitz-type dogs to hunt large game. Standardized in the 1930s and nearly became extinct after World War II but was saved by dedicated Finnish breeders. One of Finland's most prized national breeds

Temperament: Courageous, independent, loyal, alert, protective
Breed group: Working
Origin: Karelia Finland
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 20-23; Female: 17-20 kg
Height: Male: 54-61; Female: 48-56 cm
