Breed: Miniature Pinscher
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact, and elegant toy breed with a distinctive high-stepping 'hackney' gait and spirited personality. Despite resembling a miniature Doberman, this is an older, distinct German breed with a proud carriage and 'big dog' attitude in a small package.

History:
An old German breed (Zwergpinscher) developed centuries ago to hunt rats in homes and stables. Not a miniaturized Doberman Pinscher, but a distinct older breed likely descended from German Pinschers with contributions from Italian Greyhounds and Dachshunds.

Temperament: Energetic, alert, confident, intelligent, courageous, fearless
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Germany
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 3.5-4.5 kg
Height: 25-32 cm
