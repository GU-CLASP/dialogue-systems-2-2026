Breed: Kunming Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, wolf-like working dog from China resembling a German Shepherd, developed specifically for military and police duties with strong protective instincts and high intelligence

History:
Developed in the 1950s in Kunming, China, by crossing German Shepherd Dogs with local Chinese dogs and wolfdogs. Bred specifically as a military working dog for the People's Liberation Army, suited for high-altitude conditions, and officially recognized in China in 1988. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs.

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, courageous, alert, protective, work-focused
Breed group: Working
Origin: Kunming, Yunnan Province, China
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 30-38; Female: 25-34 kg
Height: Male: 64-68; Female: 60-66 cm
