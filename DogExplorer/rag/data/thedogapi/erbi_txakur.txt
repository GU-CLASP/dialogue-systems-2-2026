Breed: Erbi Txakur
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, agile scent hound from the Basque Country, specifically bred for hunting hare and small game, known for its endurance, keen sense of smell, and ability to work over challenging terrain

History:
Ancient landrace breed from the Basque regions of Spain, used for centuries by local hunters for stamina and scenting ability in pursuit of hare and small game over challenging terrain. Recognized by the Real Sociedad Canina de España (RSCE) and regional registries.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, alert, loyal, work-focused
Breed group: Scent Hound
Origin: Basque Country, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
