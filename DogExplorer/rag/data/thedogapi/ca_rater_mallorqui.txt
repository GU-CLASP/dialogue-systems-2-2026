Breed: Ca Rater Mallorquí
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, agile terrier-like breed from Mallorca with a compact, athletic build and smooth short coat. Known for intelligence, alertness, and exceptional skill in hunting rats and other vermin.

History:
Developed on the Spanish island of Mallorca specifically for controlling rats and other vermin in farms, stables, and households. Used for centuries due to its small size and agility to pursue prey into tight spaces. Provisionally recognized by the FCI and recognized as a native Spanish breed.

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, loyal, courageous
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Mallorca, Balearic Islands, Spain
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 10-15; Female: 8-13 kg
Height: Male: 30-38; Female: 29-36 cm
