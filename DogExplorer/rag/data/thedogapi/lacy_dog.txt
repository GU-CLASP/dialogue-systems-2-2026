Breed: Lacy Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized athletic working dog developed in the American Southwest for herding and hunting with a short, sleek coat and intense drive. Known for its high energy and strong work focus, this breed can be blue, red, or tricolor.

History:
Developed by the Lacy family in Burnet County, Texas, in the mid-1800s as an all-purpose working dog for herding, trailing, and hunting, using English Shepherd, Greyhound, scent hound, and possibly coyote stock. Officially designated as the State Dog of Texas in 2005.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, work-focused, devoted, loyal, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Texas Hill Country, United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 14-23; Female: 11-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-53; Female: 43-53 cm
