Breed: Burgos Pointer
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, robust Spanish pointing breed with distinctive white and liver coat, athletic build, and noble demeanor. Known for its calm temperament, droopy ears, and exceptional hunting prowess in varied terrain.

History:
Ancient Spanish pointer breed descended from old Spanish Pointer, developed over centuries in the Castile region for hunting feathered and small game in varied terrain. Lineage dates back to at least the 16th century and recognized by the FCI.

Temperament: Loyal, calm, intelligent, docile, work-focused
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Burgos, Castile and León, Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-30; Female: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 61-67; Female: 58-64 cm
