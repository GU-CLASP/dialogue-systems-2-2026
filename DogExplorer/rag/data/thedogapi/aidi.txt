Breed: Aidi
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large, powerful and muscular Moroccan mountain dog with thick, weather-resistant double coat bred as a livestock guardian and protector in the rugged Atlas Mountains

History:
Ancient Berber breed from the Atlas Mountains of Morocco, used for centuries by Berber tribes as a livestock guardian protecting herds of sheep and goats from predators. Valued for its courage, adaptability, and keen senses in protecting property and tents.

Temperament: Alert, protective, independent, loyal, courageous, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Working
Origin: Atlas Mountains, Morocco
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: 23-25 kg
Height: Male: 52-62; Female: 46-55 cm
