Breed: Bulgarian Scenthound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, well-proportioned scent hound with a strong, elegant build and short, dense coat. Developed for hunting in Bulgarian mountainous terrain, this robust breed has exceptional scenting ability and is traditionally used for trailing both small and large game including wild boar, deer, and hare.

History:
An ancient breed from the Balkans, believed brought to the region by the Slavs. Developed in Bulgaria for hunting in mountainous terrain, it's the most popular hunting dog in Bulgaria, prized for exceptional scent capabilities and endurance. Recognized by FCI as 'Bulgarian Hound (Barak)'

Temperament: Intelligent, loyal, alert, energetic, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Bulgaria
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-23 kg
Height: Male: 51-58; Female: 48-54 cm
