Breed: Briquet Griffon Vendéen
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rough-coated French scenthound known for its courage, determination, and stamina in the hunt, with a sturdy build designed for hunting in dense terrain and a friendly, energetic disposition

History:
Developed in the Vendée region of France before World War I from the larger Grand Griffon Vendéen. Selectively bred down in size by the Comte d'Elva to create a smaller faster hound for hunting medium game like hare and fox. Nearly decimated by World Wars but carefully restored

Temperament: Courageous, energetic, independent, friendly, alert, intelligent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Vendée, France
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 22-24 kg
Height: 48-56 cm
