Breed: Kai Ken
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Japanese spitz-type breed with distinctive brindle coat and athletic build, renowned for agility and loyalty as a skilled mountain hunter

History:
Ancient Japanese breed from the mountainous Kai region where brindle coat provided camouflage. Used for hunting wild boar and deer, designated as a national monument in 1934 to preserve breed purity. One of six native Japanese spitz breeds.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, loyal, courageous, independent, athletic
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: Kai Province, Yamanashi Prefecture, Japan
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 14-20; Female: 11-16 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 39-50 cm
