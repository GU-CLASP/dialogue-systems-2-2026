Breed: Montenegrin Mountain Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, agile scenthound from the Balkans with a distinctive black and tan coat and robust build. Known for its docility, dedication to hunting, and keen tracking ability in mountainous terrain.

History:
Ancient breed native to Montenegro's mountainous regions, developed for hunting hare, fox, and other small game. Formerly known as the 'black hound', it shares origins with other Balkan scenthounds and was recognized by the FCI with breed standard established in 1924. Prized by local hunters for stamina and strong sense of smell.

Temperament: Alert, loyal, energetic, docile, intelligent, devoted
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Montenegro
Life span: 11-14 years
Weight: Male: 20-25; Female: 18-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 43-53 cm
