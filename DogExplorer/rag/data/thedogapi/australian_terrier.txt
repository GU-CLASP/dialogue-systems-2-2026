Breed: Australian Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, sturdy terrier with harsh double coat and distinctive topknot, known for its spirited, alert expression and courageous personality

History:
Developed in Australia during the 19th century from British terrier breeds including Cairn, Skye, and Yorkshire Terriers. Bred as all-purpose exterminators of rodents and snakes, as well as loyal watchdogs and companions. Recognized by AKC in 1960.

Temperament: Courageous, alert, friendly, loyal, intelligent
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Australia
Life span: 11-15 years
Weight: 7-9 kg
Height: 25-28 cm
