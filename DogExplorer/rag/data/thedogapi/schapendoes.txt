Breed: Schapendoes
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized Dutch herding dog with a long, shaggy double coat and lively, cheerful disposition. Known for its springy, effortless movement and lightly built frame, this energetic breed combines intelligence with an independent herding nature.

History:
Ancient Dutch herding breed developed to herd sheep in the wetlands of the Netherlands. Nearly extinct after WWII but successfully revived by P.M.C. Toepoel and dedicated fanciers in the 1940s-1950s from remaining dogs.

Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, energetic, playful, courageous, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Netherlands
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 12-20 kg
Height: Male: 43-51; Female: 40-48 cm
