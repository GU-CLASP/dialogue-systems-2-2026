Breed: Georgian Shepherd
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog from the Caucasus mountains of Georgia, with a thick weather-resistant double coat and strong protective instincts. An ancient landrace breed known for its courage, decision-making abilities, and formidable guarding capabilities against large predators like wolves and bears.

History:
Ancient landrace breed developed in the Caucasus mountains of Georgia for centuries as a formidable livestock guardian, protecting sheep and cattle from large predators like wolves and bears. Closely related to and sometimes considered a regional variant of the Caucasian Shepherd Dog, recognized by FCI as part of the broader Caucasian Shepherd Dog (FCI No. 328).

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, courageous, calm, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Georgia (Caucasus region)
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 50-70; Female: 41-60 kg
Height: Male: 65-75; Female: 62-70 cm
