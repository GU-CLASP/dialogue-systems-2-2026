Breed: Old English Sheepdog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, athletic herding dog of great strength with distinctive profuse, shaggy double coat in gray and white, famous for its peek-a-boo hairdo and characteristic bear-like gait

History:
Developed in western England in the late 18th century likely from breeds like Bearded Collie and Russian Owtchar. Bred as drovers' dogs to drive cattle and sheep to market. Tails were historically docked to identify them as working dogs exempting them from taxes. Recognized by AKC in 1888

Temperament: Intelligent, gentle, playful, adaptable, affectionate
Breed group: Herding
Origin: England
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 32-45; Female: 27-39 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 53-56 cm
