Breed: American Hairless Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small to medium-sized, active and inquisitive terrier that comes in both hairless and coated varieties, notable for its alert expression, compact build, and friendly, intelligent nature

History:
Originated in Louisiana in 1972 when a hairless puppy named Josephine was born in a litter of Rat Terriers. Developed by Edwin and Willie Scott, recognized by UKC in 1999 and AKC in 2016

Temperament: Alert, energetic, intelligent, friendly, playful, curious
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: United States
Weight: 5-7 kg
Height: 30-41 cm
