Breed: Florida Cracker Cur
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A medium-sized, athletic cur-type herding dog native to Florida, known for its intelligence, loyalty, and tenacity in working cattle and hunting in rugged terrain

History:
Descended from Spanish dogs brought to Florida in the 1500s, developed by early cattlemen ('Crackers') to work the tough Florida Cracker Cattle in rugged terrain. The breed faced extinction but was revived by dedicated enthusiasts and maintained by regional breed organizations.

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, energetic, protective, work-focused, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Florida, United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 14-25; Female: 11-23 kg
Height: Male: 46-56; Female: 43-53 cm
