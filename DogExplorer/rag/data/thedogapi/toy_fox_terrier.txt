Breed: Toy Fox Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, athletic, elegant terrier with short, fine coat and alert, lively demeanor, combining toy breed size with true terrier temperament and the spirit of a larger dog

History:
Developed in the early 20th century in the United States by crossing small Smooth Fox Terriers with toy breeds including Chihuahuas, Manchester Terriers, and Italian Greyhounds to create a smaller, versatile companion and ratter. Recognized by the AKC in 2003.

Temperament: Alert, intelligent, energetic, friendly, playful, loyal, outgoing
Breed group: Toy
Origin: United States
Weight: 1.6-3.2 kg
Height: 22-29 cm
