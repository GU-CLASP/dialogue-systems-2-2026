Breed: Vizsla
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized, energetic and graceful gundog of Hungarian origin with a sleek, golden-rust colored coat and athletic build. Known for their affectionate, gentle nature, they are versatile hunters and eager companions with high energy drive.

History:
Ancient Hungarian sporting breed descended from hunting dogs of the Magyar tribes who settled in Hungary over a thousand years ago. Developed as a versatile pointer-retriever by Hungarian nobility, the breed nearly faced extinction after World War II but was preserved and re-established by dedicated enthusiasts.

Temperament: Energetic, affectionate, gentle, intelligent, athletic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 25-27; Female: 20-25 kg
Height: Male: 56-61; Female: 53-58 cm
