Breed: American English Coonhound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Athletic medium to large scent hound with excellent tracking ability, swift and powerful build, and a hard, protective short coat. Bred for treeing raccoons and fox, known for speed, endurance, and distinctive melodious baying.

History:
Descended from English Foxhounds brought to American colonies in the 17th and 18th centuries. Developed by breeders in the American South for hunting raccoons and foxes in rougher terrain, recognized by AKC in 2011

Temperament: Energetic, friendly, intelligent, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: United States
Life span: 11-12 years
Weight: Male: 20-30; Female: 18-27 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 58-64 cm
