Breed: Kuvasz
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful white Hungarian livestock guardian dog with a dense double coat, noble bearing, and fiercely loyal, protective nature

History:
Ancient Hungarian breed descended from large molosser-type dogs from Tibet. Historically served as royal guard dogs for Magyar kings and fierce livestock guardians, prized for their loyalty and courage in protecting flocks and estates from predators and thieves

Temperament: Loyal, protective, independent, courageous, intelligent, calm
Breed group: Working
Origin: Hungary
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-52; Female: 32-41 kg
Height: Male: 71-76; Female: 66-71 cm
