Breed: Cocker Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact sporting breed with silky coat, merry expression, and affectionate nature

History:
Descended from English Cocker Spaniels, developed in the US for smaller size and specialized for woodcock hunting; officially recognized as a distinct breed by the AKC in 1946

Temperament: Friendly, affectionate, playful, intelligent, outgoing
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 11-14; Female: 9-11 kg
Height: Male: 34-39; Female: 32-37 cm
