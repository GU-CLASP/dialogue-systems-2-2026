Breed: Löwchen
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small companion dog known as the 'Little Lion Dog' with a long, flowing coat traditionally clipped into a distinctive lion trim featuring a plumed tail and mane. This bright, lively toy breed combines an affectionate nature with alert intelligence.

History:
Ancient breed with documented history in pre-Renaissance Europe, depicted in historical artworks for centuries and cherished by European nobility. Nearly extinct after WWII but saved through dedicated breeding efforts by Madame Bennert and other breeders. Once known as the rarest dog in the world before numbers recovered.

Temperament: Affectionate, outgoing, playful, intelligent, alert
Breed group: Non-Sporting
Origin: France/Germany
Weight: 4-8 kg
Height: 30-36 cm
