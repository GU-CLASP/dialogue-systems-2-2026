Breed: Phalène
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
The Phalène is the drop-eared variety of the Continental Toy Spaniel, an elegant and fine-boned toy breed with a long, flowing silky coat and alert expression. Known as the 'moth-eared' spaniel, this small, delicate breed is closely related to the Papillon.

History:
The Phalène, or 'moth-eared' spaniel, is the original drop-eared variety of the Continental Toy Spaniel, predating the erect-eared Papillon. Known since at least the 16th century, these toy spaniels were favorites in European royal courts and are depicted in famous paintings. The FCI recognizes it as distinct from the Papillon for its ear carriage, though both types are often born in the same litters.
