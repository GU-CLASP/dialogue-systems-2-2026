Breed: Philippine Forest Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-small native landrace dog adapted to the Philippines' forests, characterized by agility and alertness. This ancient indigenous breed has evolved to thrive in forest environments.

History:
Ancient native dog population indigenous to the Philippines, adapted to forest environments and traditionally used for hunting and companionship. Not formally recognized by major kennel clubs.
