Breed: Welsh Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Compact, sturdy medium-sized terrier with distinctive harsh wiry black and tan coat and lively, spirited expression. One of the oldest terrier breeds from Wales, known for its rugged build and friendly disposition.

History:
One of the oldest terrier breeds, originating in Wales where it was developed for hunting fox, badger, otter, and vermin. Originally known as the Old English Terrier or Black-and-Tan Wire Haired Terrier, it was formally recognized by The Kennel Club in 1886 and AKC in 1888.

Temperament: Friendly, alert, intelligent, spirited, energetic, confident
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Wales
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 9-10 kg
Height: 38-39 cm
