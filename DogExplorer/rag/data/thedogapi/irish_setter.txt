Breed: Irish Setter
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Elegant, athletic gundog known for its stunning mahogany or rich chestnut red flowing coat and high-energy, friendly temperament

History:
Developed in 18th-century Ireland as a versatile gundog for setting and retrieving game birds by crossing Irish Water Spaniel, English Setter, and Pointer. The breed's striking mahogany color became a hallmark in the 19th century, distinguishing it from earlier red-and-white setters

Temperament: Friendly, outgoing, energetic, intelligent, playful, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Ireland
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 27-34; Female: 24-29 kg
Height: Male: 66-74; Female: 61-69 cm
