Breed: Entlebucher Mountain Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
The smallest of the four Swiss Mountain Dogs, the Entlebucher is a compact, sturdy, and muscular herding dog with a distinctive tricolor coat. Known for its intelligence, loyalty, and agile working ability.

History:
The smallest of the four Swiss Sennenhund breeds, developed in the Entlebuch valley region of Switzerland as an all-around farm dog for herding cattle and driving livestock to market. First documented in 1889 and recognized for its working versatility.

Temperament: Intelligent, energetic, loyal, alert, confident, devoted
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Entlebuch valley, Switzerland
Life span: 11-13 years
Weight: Male: 23-30; Female: 18-25 kg
Height: Male: 43-53; Female: 41-51 cm
