Breed: American Cocker Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
The smallest member of the Sporting Group, the American Cocker Spaniel is a medium-sized sporting dog with a beautifully chiseled head, long flowing silky coat, long ears, and gentle expression

History:
Developed in the United States from English Cocker Spaniels brought over in the late 1800s. American breeders selected for smaller size, more refined head, and heavier coat for woodcock hunting, leading to recognition as a separate breed by the AKC in 1946

Temperament: Gentle, smart, happy, outgoing, affectionate
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 11-14; Female: 9-11 kg
Height: Male: 37-39; Female: 34-37 cm
