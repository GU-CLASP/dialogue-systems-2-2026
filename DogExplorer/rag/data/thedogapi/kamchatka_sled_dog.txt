Breed: Kamchatka Sled Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An ancient, powerful landrace sled dog from Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula with a thick double coat and strong, athletic build. This large spitz-type breed was developed for endurance and work in harsh Arctic conditions, possessing a primitive appearance and resilient nature.

History:
Ancient aboriginal landrace sled dog developed over centuries by indigenous peoples including the Itelmen and Koryak peoples of Kamchatka Peninsula for transportation and hauling in severe Arctic conditions. A foundational type for many modern sled breeds valued for stamina, resilience, and work ethic.

Temperament: Hardy, independent, work-focused, loyal, intelligent, energetic
Breed group: Working
Origin: Kamchatka Peninsula, Russia
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 23-40; Female: 18-35 kg
Height: Male: 53-66; Female: 51-61 cm
