Breed: Jack Russell Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, energetic earth-working terrier with a predominantly white coat and sturdy build, known for its bold, athletic nature and lively temperament, originally bred for fox hunting

History:
Developed in southern England during the mid-1800s by Reverend John Russell for fox hunting, bred to bolt foxes from dens. The shorter-legged variety standard was primarily developed in Australia.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, alert, courageous, confident, playful
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 6-8 kg
Height: 25-30 cm
