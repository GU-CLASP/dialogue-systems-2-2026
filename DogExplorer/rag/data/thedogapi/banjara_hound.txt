Breed: Banjara Hound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Ancient, lean athletic sighthound landrace native to India, traditionally used by nomadic Banjara people for hunting game over harsh terrain. Known for endurance, loyalty, and speed, bred for function over form.

History:
Ancient landrace sighthound developed by the Banjara nomadic tribes of India, primarily in the Maharashtra region. For centuries bred for hunting deer, hare, and medium game over open terrain, also serving as flock guardian and family protector. Not recognized by major international kennel clubs but established in regional registries.

Temperament: Independent, loyal, alert, intelligent, courageous
Breed group: Hound
Origin: India
Life span: 10-14 years

Height: Male: 66-76; Female: 61-71 cm
