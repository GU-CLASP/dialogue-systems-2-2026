Breed: Frenchton
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact crossbreed combining French Bulldog and Boston Terrier traits with distinctive bat ears, muscular build, and expressive features. Known for its sturdy build with a slightly longer snout than the French Bulldog parent.

History:
Modern designer crossbreed developed in the United States starting in the 1990s, combining French Bulldog and Boston Terrier to create a healthier companion with fewer respiratory issues while retaining the friendly, compact nature of both parent breeds

Temperament: Friendly, playful, intelligent, affectionate, adaptable
Breed group: Mixed
Origin: United States
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 7-11; Female: 6-10 kg
Height: Male: 36-41; Female: 33-38 cm
