Breed: Spanish Water Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rustic water dog with distinctive curly woolly coat, athletic build, and versatile multi-purpose working ability

History:
Ancient all-purpose farm dog from the Iberian Peninsula, used for centuries in Spain for herding livestock, retrieving waterfowl for hunters, and assisting fishermen. Nearly extinct but successfully revived in the 1970s

Temperament: Loyal, intelligent, work-focused, athletic, energetic, alert
Breed group: Herding
Origin: Spain
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 18-22; Female: 14-18 kg
Height: Male: 44-50; Female: 40-46 cm
