Breed: Bankhar Dog
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful ancient livestock guardian dog from Mongolia with thick double coat and independent, protective temperament. This landrace breed is adapted to survive and work in harsh climates.

History:
Ancient landrace livestock guardian breed developed by nomadic Mongolian herders for over 15,000 years to protect livestock from wolves snow leopards and other predators. Modern conservation efforts by organizations like the Mongolian Bankhar Dog Project work to preserve and reintroduce the breed to traditional working roles.

Temperament: Protective, independent, loyal, calm, alert, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Mongolia
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 39-57; Female: 32-48 kg
Height: Male: 64-76; Female: 61-71 cm
