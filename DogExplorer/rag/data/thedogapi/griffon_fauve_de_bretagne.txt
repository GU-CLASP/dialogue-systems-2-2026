Breed: Griffon Fauve de Bretagne
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized rough-coated French scent hound with harsh, wiry fawn to red coat and keen hunting instincts, known for tenacity in the hunt and rustic appearance

History:
Ancient French scenthound dating back to the 13th century from the Brittany region, originally used to hunt wolves and wild boar. Nearly extinct after WWII but revived by dedicated enthusiasts using remaining bloodlines and crosses with other French hounds
