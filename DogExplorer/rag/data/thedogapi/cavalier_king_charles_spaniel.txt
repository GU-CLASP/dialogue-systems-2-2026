Breed: Cavalier King Charles Spaniel
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, elegant toy spaniel with silky coat, large expressive eyes, and noble appearance, known for its gentle expression and affectionate temperament, equally at home as a lap dog or active companion

History:
Named after King Charles II who favored the breed, descended from toy spaniels depicted in 16th-18th century British court paintings. The modern breed was revived in the 1920s by enthusiasts seeking to restore the longer-nosed type seen in old portraits

Temperament: Affectionate, gentle, friendly, playful, eager to please, adaptable
Breed group: Toy
Origin: United Kingdom
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: 6-8 kg
Height: 30-33 cm
