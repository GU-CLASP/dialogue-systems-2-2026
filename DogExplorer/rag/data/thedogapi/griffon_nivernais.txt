Breed: Griffon Nivernais
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium-sized French scent hound with a robust build, rough shaggy coat, and rugged appearance. Known for exceptional hunting ability in difficult terrain and strong work-focused instincts.

History:
Ancient French scent hound from the Nivernais region, originally used by medieval French nobility for hunting wolf and wild boar. Nearly extinct after World War II and the French Revolution, but revived in the 19th century through careful breeding using Grand Griffon Vendéen, Otterhound, and local stock.

Temperament: Independent, courageous, energetic, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Nivernais region, France
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 22-25; Female: 20-23 kg
Height: Male: 55-61; Female: 52-60 cm
