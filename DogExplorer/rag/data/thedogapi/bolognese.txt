Breed: Bolognese
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
A small, stocky, and compact white toy breed with a long, fluffy, pure-white cotton-like coat, belonging to the Bichon family. Known for its calm, devoted temperament and affectionate nature as a companion dog.

History:
An ancient noble breed of the Bichon family, originating in Bologna, Italy, dating to 11th-12th centuries. Prized companions to royalty and aristocracy from the Roman era through the Renaissance, favored as palace companions.

Temperament: Calm, devoted, intelligent, playful, gentle, loyal
Breed group: Toy
Origin: Bologna, Italy
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: 2.5-4 kg
Height: 25-30 cm
