Breed: Hanoverian Scenthound
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Robust medium-large scenthound known for its exceptional tracking ability and calm, determined temperament. Descended from medieval Liam hounds, this breed was specifically developed to track wounded game for hunters.

History:
Descended from medieval Liam hounds in Germany; developed to track wounded game for hunters. Recognized by the FCI (Standard No. 213).

Temperament: Calm, loyal, determined, independent
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Germany
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: 36-45 kg
Height: 48-55 cm
