Breed: Weimaraner
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, sleek, athletic sporting dog with distinctive silver-gray coat and striking amber or blue-gray eyes, known for speed, stamina, and intelligence. Originally bred for hunting large game, earning the nickname 'Gray Ghost' for its coat and stealth.

History:
Developed at the court of Weimar, Germany, in the early 19th century by German nobility who sought to create an all-around hunting dog for large game. The breed's parentage was a closely guarded secret for many years.

Temperament: Energetic, intelligent, loyal, friendly, alert, courageous, athletic
Breed group: Sporting
Origin: Weimar, Germany
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: Male: 32-41; Female: 25-34 kg
Height: Male: 64-69; Female: 58-64 cm
