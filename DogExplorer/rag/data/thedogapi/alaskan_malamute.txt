Breed: Alaskan Malamute
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
An immensely strong, heavy-duty freighting dog of spitz type, the Alaskan Malamute is a large, powerful Arctic sled dog with a thick double coat, deep chest, well-muscled body, wolf-like appearance, and characteristically plumed tail carried over the back

History:
One of the oldest Arctic sled dogs, developed by the native Mahlemut Inuit tribe of northwestern Alaska. Essential for survival, used for hauling heavy freight across Arctic terrain, hunting large predators like seals and polar bears

Temperament: Affectionate, friendly, loyal, playful, intelligent, energetic, outgoing
Breed group: Working
Origin: Alaska, United States
Life span: 10-14 years
Weight: Male: 36-41; Female: 32-36 kg
Height: Male: 61-66; Female: 56-61 cm
