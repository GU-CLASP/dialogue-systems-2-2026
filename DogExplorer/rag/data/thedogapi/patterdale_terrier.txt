Breed: Patterdale Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact, and tough working terrier developed in the Lake District of England for hunting and dispatching vermin. Known for its strong build, endurance, and confident, work-focused nature with smooth or broken coat.

History:
Developed in the Lake District of Northern England in the early 20th century as a tough, game working terrier for fox hunting and vermin control in rocky fells. Bred for stamina, courage, and ability to work in tight burrows. Not currently recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Energetic, courageous, confident, independent, alert, work-focused
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England (Lake District)
Life span: 10-13 years
Weight: 5-6 kg
Height: 25-38 cm
