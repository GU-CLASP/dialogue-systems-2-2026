Breed: Black Mouth Cur
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Medium to large rugged, well-muscled working and hunting dog from the American South, known for its versatility, courage, intelligence, and characteristic black-pigmented muzzle and lips

History:
Developed in the southern United States as versatile pioneer and farm dog for herding cattle and hogs protecting homestead and hunting variety of game from squirrels to bears valued for stamina and reliability

Temperament: Loyal, courageous, intelligent, energetic, protective, work-focused
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Southern United States
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: Male: 18-43; Female: 16-36 kg
Height: Male: 46-64; Female: 41-58 cm
