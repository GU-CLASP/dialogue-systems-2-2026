Breed: Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, rough-coated French scent hound with sturdy build, distinctive beard and mustache, built low to the ground for hunting rabbit and hare through dense underbrush. Known for their happy demeanor and lively personality.

History:
Developed in the 16th century Vendée region of France from larger Grand Griffon Vendéen hounds, bred specifically for hunting rabbit and hare in dense brush. The name describes the breed: Petit (small), Basset (low to the ground), Griffon (rough-coated), and Vendéen (from Vendée). First officially recognized in late 19th century.

Temperament: Happy, outgoing, independent, alert, confident, energetic
Breed group: Hound
Origin: Vendée, France
Life span: 12-16 years
Weight: 11-18 kg
Height: 33-38 cm
