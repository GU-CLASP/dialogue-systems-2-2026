Breed: Jagdterrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact German hunting terrier with wiry black and tan coat, bred for versatility, tenacity, and intense working drive in the field

History:
Developed in Germany after World War I by hunters who wanted to create a breed solely for hunting performance. Selectively bred from Fox Terrier lines and various terrier breeds to create a versatile, tenacious, and courageous hunting dog.

Temperament: Courageous, intelligent, energetic, work-focused, independent
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: Germany
Life span: 10-15 years
Weight: Male: 9-10; Female: 7.5-9 kg
Height: Male: 33-40; Female: 33-39.5 cm
