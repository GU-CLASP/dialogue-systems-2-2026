Breed: Karakachan
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful livestock guardian dog with dense double coat, bred by nomadic shepherds to protect flocks in mountainous terrain of the Balkans. Known for its formidable presence and unwavering loyalty to its flock and family.

History:
One of Europe's oldest breeds descended from dogs of the Thracians, developed and preserved by the Karakachans nomadic shepherds of the Balkans for livestock protection. Nearly extinct but being preserved through breeding programs and recognized by FCI

Temperament: Independent, protective, loyal, alert, courageous, intelligent
Breed group: Working
Origin: Bulgaria
Life span: 12-14 years
Weight: Male: 40-57; Female: 35-50 kg
Height: Male: 65-75; Female: 60-70 cm
