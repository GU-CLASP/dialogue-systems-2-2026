Breed: Molossus of Epirus
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Large, powerful, and imposing livestock guardian dog from the Epirus region of Greece, descended from ancient Molossian dogs. Characterized by its robust build, dense double coat, and calm yet protective demeanor with strong guardian instincts.

History:
Ancient livestock guardian breed from the mountainous Epirus region of Greece, descended from the Molossian dogs of antiquity and developed over centuries to guard livestock from predators like wolves and bears. Officially recognized by the Kennel Club of Greece in 2006.

Temperament: Protective, loyal, independent, calm, courageous, confident
Breed group: Working
Origin: Epirus, Greece
Life span: 10-12 years
Weight: Male: 45-68; Female: 40-59 kg
Height: Male: 66-75; Female: 64-71 cm
