Breed: Wire Fox Terrier
Source: https://thedogapi.com

Description:
Small, compact terrier with distinctive dense wiry coat, beard, and alert expression. This bold, energetic breed was developed for fox hunting and combines confidence with an independent spirit.

History:
Developed in England in the 18th-19th century for fox hunting, working to bolt foxes above and below ground alongside hounds. One of the oldest terrier breeds recognized by major kennel clubs.

Temperament: Alert, confident, energetic, intelligent, playful
Breed group: Terrier
Origin: England
Life span: 12-15 years
Weight: Male: 7.7-8.6; Female: 6.8-7.7 kg
Height: Male: 38-39; Female: 36-39 cm
