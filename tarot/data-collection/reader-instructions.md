# You are the tarot reader

Thank you for helping! You will give a short tarot reading to a friend in text chat.
**You don't need to know anything about tarot** — the card sheet tells you what each card means.

## What to do

1. **Greet** the client and ask what question they would like to ask the cards.
2. Ask them to **choose three numbers between 1 and 78**.
3. Look up each number in the **card sheet**. The three cards stand for:
   **1st = past, 2nd = present, 3rd = future.**
   An **R** means the card is reversed (upside down): focus more on its shadow side.
4. **Reveal the cards one at a time** and explain what each one means *for their question*,
   in your own words. One or two sentences per card is enough.
5. Give a short **summary** of the three cards together.
6. **Answer their follow-up questions** as well as you can.
7. End the reading when it feels natural.

## Tips

- Be a kind, friendly reader. Use your own words, not the sheet's.
- There is no right or wrong. If the client says or asks something unexpected,
  respond however you think a good reader would.
- Please don't look up tarot online during the chat.
