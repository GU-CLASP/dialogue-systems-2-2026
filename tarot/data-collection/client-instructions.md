# You are the client

Thank you for helping! A friend will give you a short tarot reading in text chat.
You will get **one scenario card** below. Imagine you are that person.

## What to do

- Ask the reader your question **in your own words** — don't copy the card.
- When the reader asks, choose three numbers between 1 and 78.
- After the reading, **ask at least two follow-up questions** — whatever you would
  really want to know as this person.
- Behave naturally: you can be unsure, change your mind, or ask the reader to repeat.
- Leave when you feel the reading is over.

---

## Scenario cards (give the client only one)

**A. New relationship**
You have been dating someone for two months. You really like them, but you can't tell
if they are serious about you.

**B. The ex**
You broke up three months ago. You still think about your ex and wonder if they will come back.

**C. Job interview**
You had a job interview last week for a job you really want. You are still waiting to hear back.

**D. Studies**
You are stressed about your thesis and thinking about switching to a different programme.

**E. Just curious**
You have no special question. A friend said tarot is fun, so you just want to see what the cards say.

**F. The sceptic**
You don't believe in tarot at all. A friend made you try it. You are polite, but curious whether it's "real".

**G. Family worry**
Your dad has been ill for a while and is waiting for test results. You are worried about him.

**H. Money**
You are thinking about putting most of your savings into cryptocurrency.
