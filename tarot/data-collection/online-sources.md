## What clients ask
- **Career**: "Will the company give me an offer after the interview?", "What about my career if I move back home?"
- **Love**: "Will we get back together?", "How does he feel about me?", "Where is this relationship going in the next six months?"
- **Big decisions**: stay abroad or go home after graduation; buy a house.
- **Practical first-timer questions**: "Do I have to say my question out loud, or just think it?", "Is reversed counted from my side or the reader's?"

## How readers run a reading
- Many readers **ask for the question first** and help the client make it clear ("say what's really bothering you, then ask").
- Some readers let the client **only think the question**, then shuffle and have the client pick cards.
- Clients value readers who **explain card by card**, then give **advice**, and are **warm and reassuring** ("felt like a friend", "joked to ease my anxiety").
- Clients complain when a reading is **too short or vague** ("5 minutes, just told me it will be stable — felt like 99 yuan for a pep talk").

## Readers' own rules (several notes, very consistent)
1. **One question per reading.** Don't pack several questions into one spread.
2. **Don't ask the same question again** because you didn't like the answer; extend it instead.
3. **No health, life-and-death or legal questions** — see a doctor, the police, a lawyer. Also no gambling, lottery or exam results.
4. **Short-term only** (a few months, at most two years); no "will we marry in this life?".
5. **Don't ask to control others** ("how do I make him come back?") — rephrase to your own choice ("what happens if I reach out?").
6. **Reversed is not a disaster** — it means stuck, needs adjusting.
7. **Tarot is a tendency, not a verdict**; don't read when very upset.
